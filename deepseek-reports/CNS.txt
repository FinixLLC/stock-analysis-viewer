Investment Analysis Report: Cohen & Steers, Inc. (CNS)

Overview:
Cohen & Steers, Inc. is an investment management firm catering to institutional clients, primarily generating revenue through fees based on assets under management.

Financial Performance:
- Net Income: Showed a 5% increase from $39.7 million in Q3 2024 to $41.7 million in Q3 2025.
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Increased by 5%, rising from $0.59 to $0.62, indicating improved earnings per share.
- Dividends: Declared dividends of $0.62 per share in Q3 2025 and $0.59 in Q3 2024, reflecting a higher payout ratio.

Stockholder Equity:
- Total stockholders' equity grew from $498.1 million in 2024 to $564.7 million in 2025, signaling financial stability.
- Common stock and additional paid-in capital saw slight increases, possibly due to share buybacks or new share issuances.

Shareholder Returns:
- Significant jump in common stock repurchases ($26.9 million in Q3 2025 vs. $68,000 in Q3 2024), indicating a strong return of value to shareholders.
- Dividends increased by $0.03 per share, showing commitment to shareholder returns.

Key Metrics:
- Return on Equity (ROE): Improved from 6.9% to 7.8%, suggesting more efficient use of equity.
- Limited data on P/E ratio and debt-to-equity ratio hinders a comprehensive valuation assessment.

Risks:
- Dependency on institutional clients exposes CNS to market volatility, which could affect revenue if investment performance wanes.
- High dividend payouts and buybacks may limit reinvestment in business growth and innovation.

Opportunities:
- Potential for expansion into new markets or product offerings, especially in alternative investments.
- Focus on ESG initiatives aligns with growing investor preferences for sustainability.

Conclusion:
CNS demonstrates financial stability with improving profitability and efficient equity use. However, the absence of revenue data and key ratios like P/E and debt-to-equity leaves some uncertainties. Further analysis with complete financial statements is recommended to fully assess investment potential.
