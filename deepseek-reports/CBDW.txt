Analysis of CBDW's Financial Health

1. Revenue Concerns:
   - CBDW reported no revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2025, with only $7,195 in the nine-month period. The absence of consistent revenue generation is a significant concern, as it indicates an inability to sustain operations through sales.

2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):
   - COGS for the nine months in 2025 was $7,313, resulting in a small gross loss of $118. However, no COGS was reported for the three-month period, suggesting potential inconsistencies or insufficient sales volume.

3. Operating Expenses:
   - There was a notable decrease in SG&A expenses from 2024 to 2025, with significant reductions observed. This could indicate cost-cutting measures but raises questions about the sustainability of such reductions without impacting operations.

4. Interest Expense:
   - Interest expenses surged in 2025 compared to 2024, suggesting potential increases in debt or higher interest rates. This could negatively impact future financial health if not managed properly.

5. Net Loss and Per Share Data:
   - Despite a reduction in net loss per share, CBDW continued to operate at a loss in both periods of 2025. The weighted average common shares outstanding increased significantly, potentially due to dilution from convertible instruments.

6. Stockholders' Deficit:
   - As of September 30, 2024, CBDW had a total stockholders' deficit of $4.195 million, indicating financial instability and the need for immediate corrective actions.

Conclusion:
CBDW faces critical challenges, including lack of revenue, high operating expenses, significant net losses, and potential issues with derivatives and interest expenses. While there have been improvements in certain metrics, the company's financial health remains precarious. Immediate steps to generate revenue and reduce expenses are essential for sustainability.
