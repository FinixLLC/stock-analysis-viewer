Investment Assessment of NABL: A Structured Analysis

   - Cybersecurity Risks: Potential breaches could harm reputation and finances. Past incidents and current measures are crucial to evaluate.
   - Intellectual Property: Protecting IP is vital for market share. The number of patents/trademarks and enforcement efforts should be considered.
   - Human Resources: Employee retention is key for a tech company. Retention rates and satisfaction initiatives need scrutiny.
   - Financial Performance Fluctuations: Inconsistent margins could indicate underlying issues; historical financial data would provide insight.
   - International Operations Risks: Currency fluctuations impact profitability, especially if a significant portion of revenue comes from abroad.

2. Opportunities:

   - Market Expansion: Growth in international markets, despite risks, could drive revenue.
