4. Opportunities:
- Strong Earnings Growth: Consistent net income growth indicates effective management and market presence.
- Adaptability: Potential to thrive in changing economic conditions if agile.

5. Missing Information:

6. Investment Assessment:
- SHW appears financially stable with solid earnings, but lacks detailed data for comprehensive risk assessment. Further analysis of financial metrics and industry trends is recommended before making an investment decision.
