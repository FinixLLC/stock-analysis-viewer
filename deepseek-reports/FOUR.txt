3. Balance Sheet Analysis:

4. Cash Flow Statement Analysis:

   - Financing activities: Debt issuance or share buybacks.

5. Key Financial Ratios:

6. Risks and Opportunities:

7. Investment Assessment:

   - Valuation metrics: P/E ratio, EV/EBITDA.
   - Industry context: Cyclical vs. non-cyclical; competitive landscape.

8. Additional Considerations:
