Key Financial Metrics to Consider:

1. Revenue Growth: Look for consistent quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year growth, indicating a healthy company.
2. Profit Margins: High net profit and gross margins suggest efficiency and strong pricing or cost management.
3. Cash Flow: Positive operating cash flow is essential for sustainability and growth investments.
4. Debt Levels: Moderate debt-to-equity ratio indicates manageable financial leverage without excessive risk.
5. Return on Equity (ROE): Higher ROE suggests effective use of equity to generate profits.
6. Liquidity Ratios: Current and quick ratios above 1 ensure short-term solvency.
