Analysis of FirstSun Capital Bancorp (FSUN) Financial Report

Overview:
FirstSun Capital Bancorp (FSUN) has filed a 10-Q quarterly report, which includes unaudited financial statements and management discussions. The report highlights key risk factors and provides balance sheet data for two quarters.

Key Findings:

1. Balance Sheet Growth:
   - Assets: Cash and cash equivalents increased slightly, indicating liquidity. Securities available-for-sale also rose, suggesting investment activity. Loans held-for-sale grew significantly, reflecting potential loan origination or sales.
   - Liabilities & Equity: Deposits surged by over $1 billion, a substantial increase to $6.7 billion, indicating strong deposit growth. Total liabilities increased by $1.3 billion, and equity rose, though not proportionally as much.

2. Risk Factors:
   - FSUN faces risks from economic downturns, regulatory changes, cybersecurity threats, competition, employee retention, credit loss volatility, accounting changes, third-party reliance, and capital availability.
   - The report lacks specifics on loan portfolio quality, interest rate risk, and geographic/industry concentrations, making it hard to assess actual risk levels.

3. Opportunities & Risks:
   - Opportunities: Deposit growth provides funds for lending, potentially driving net interest income if rates remain favorable.
   - Risks: Economic downturns could increase defaults; competition may pressure margins; cybersecurity threats pose operational risks.

4. Missing Information:
   - The report lacks income statements and cash flow details, crucial for assessing profitability and cash management.
   - No discussion on credit quality or interest rate sensitivity hinders a comprehensive risk assessment.

Conclusion:
FSUN shows growth in assets and liabilities but lacks sufficient detail to fully assess financial health. Investors should seek more information on profitability, credit quality, interest rate exposure, and specific risks before making informed decisions.
