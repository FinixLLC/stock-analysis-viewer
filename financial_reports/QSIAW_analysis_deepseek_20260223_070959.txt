Risks:

- Sustainability of Financing: Heavy reliance on financing activities may pose risks if external funding becomes difficult to secure.
- Share Dilution: Continued issuance of shares could impact shareholder value and stock performance.

Opportunities:
- Growth Potential: The ability to raise significant capital through financing suggests potential for expansion or new ventures, provided these investments yield returns.
- Strategic Investments: Capital expenditures may lead to future growth if effectively utilized in competitive markets or innovative products.

Investment Assessment:
While QSIAW has managed to secure substantial financing, the fundamental issues in operations and profitability are concerning. Investors should be cautious due to the increasing net loss and reliance on external funding. Long-term viability depends on addressing operational inefficiencies and reducing dependence on financing activities. Further analysis of revenue streams, cost structures, and market conditions is recommended before making investment decisions.

Conclusion:
Investors should approach QSIAW with caution. While there are opportunities for growth through strategic investments, the current financial health raises significant concerns that need to be carefully managed.
