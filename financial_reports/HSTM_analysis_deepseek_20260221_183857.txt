Analysis of HSTM Financial Report

   - The report indicates a revenue decrease from $249,075 (December 31, 2023) to $228,753 (September 30, 2025), which is an 8% decline. However, this comparison spans more than a year, making it less straightforward. It's unclear if this reflects a nine-month period in both years.

2. Net Income:

3. Comprehensive Income:
   - Comprehensive loss improved from -$481 to -$254, indicating a smaller loss in 2025 compared to 2024. However, the report's phrasing is confusing, suggesting it worsened when it actually improved.

4. Cash Flow and Dividends:

5. Balance Sheet:
   - Current assets rose by 2%, while current liabilities jumped 32%. This increase in liabilities could signal higher financial risk.
   - Stockholders' equity decreased, particularly retained earnings dropping by 8%, and accumulated other comprehensive loss worsened, suggesting underlying issues in equity health.

6. Conclusion:
   - The report's conclusion about revenue decline and net income decrease may be misleading due to potential date misinterpretation. Further verification is needed for accurate assessment.
   - Without detailed cash flow information, assessing liquidity and overall financial health is challenging.

Recommendations:
- Verify the accuracy of the revenue and net income figures as there appears to be a discrepancy in their presentation.
- Request more detailed cash flow statements to better understand liquidity and financial stability.
