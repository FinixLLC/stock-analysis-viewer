Investment Analysis of Carter's Inc. (CRI)

1. Revenue Overview:
- Q3 2025 vs Q3 2024: Net sales slightly decreased from $758.5 million to $757.8 million, indicating a minor dip.
- Three Quarters YTD: Total revenue remained stable at approximately $1.97 billion compared to $1.98 billion in the prior year.

2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):
- Q3 2025 vs Q3 2024: COGS increased by $13.7 million, rising from $402.5 million to $416.2 million.
- YTD Comparison: COGS for three quarters grew by $30 million, from $1.03 billion to $1.06 billion.

3. Gross Profit:
- Q3 2025 vs Q3 2024: Decreased by $14.4 million, dropping from $356 million to $341.6 million.
- YTD Comparison: Reduced by $39.6 million, from $954.1 million to $914.5 million.

4. Operating Income:
- Q3 2025 vs Q3 2024: Plunged by approximately 62%, falling from $77 million to $29.1 million.
- YTD Comparison: Dropped by over $112 million, decreasing from $171.5 million to $59.2 million.

5. Net Income:
- Q3 2025 vs Q3 2024: Decreased by nearly 80%, from $58.3 million to $11.6 million.
- YTD Comparison: Reduced by about 78%, falling from $124 million to $27.6 million.

6. Dividend Analysis:
- Per Share Decline: From $0.80 per quarter in 2024 to $0.25 in 2025, and annualized from $2.40 to $0.90.

7. Risks:
- Significant drop in operating and net income suggests potential issues with expense management or external pressures like increased costs or interest expenses.
- Pension plan settlements may indicate ongoing financial strain.

8. Opportunities:
- Potential for cost optimization, especially in COGS and operational expenses.
- Recovery if temporary factors like restructuring are addressed.

9. Industry Context:
- Consideration of sector-specific challenges such as retail competition or supply chain issues.

10. Investment Assessment:
- Caution Advised: Declining profitability and reduced dividends raise concerns. Investors should seek more data on expense drivers.
- Long-term Potential: If the company can manage expenses and stabilize revenue, future recovery is possible.

In conclusion, while CRI maintains stable revenue, the financial health is under significant strain due to rising costs and plummeting profits. Further investigation into cash flows and industry context is recommended for a comprehensive assessment.
