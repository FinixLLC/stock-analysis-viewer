3. Expense Management: While accrued expenses declined, it's crucial to ensure this trend continues without compromising essential operations.

- Economic environment sensitivity, particularly regarding interest rate fluctuations impacting yields and refinancing activity.

Opportunities:

- Larger deposit base offering more funds for income generation through lending.

Investment Assessment:
SPFI demonstrates positive performance with asset growth, increased interest income, and efficient expense management.
