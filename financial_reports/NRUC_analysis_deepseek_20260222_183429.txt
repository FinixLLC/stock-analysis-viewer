Key Areas of Analysis:

1. Liquidity:
   - Current Ratio: Calculate by dividing current assets by current liabilities. A ratio above 1 indicates good liquidity.
   - Importance: Ensures the company can meet short-term obligations.

2. Leverage (Debt Management):
   - Debt-to-Equity Ratio: Compares total debt to shareholders' equity. Lower ratios are preferable for lower financial risk.
   - Interest Coverage Ratio: Measures earnings against interest expenses. Higher values indicate better ability to meet interest payments.

3. Profitability:
   - Net Profit Margin: Assesses profitability relative to revenue. Consistent growth signals a healthy company.
   - Return on Equity (ROE): Indicates how effectively equity is being utilized to generate profits.

4. Operating Efficiency:
   - Asset Turnover Ratio: Measures how efficiently assets are used to generate sales.
   - Inventory Turnover: For utility companies, this might not be as critical unless they have significant inventory.

5. Growth Trends:
   - Revenue Growth: Consistent revenue increases indicate a growing company.
   - Earnings Per Share (EPS): Reflects profitability and growth over time.

6. Cash Flow Analysis:
