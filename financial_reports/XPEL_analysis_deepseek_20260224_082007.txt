5. Opportunities:

- Low Leverage: The debt-to-equity ratio of 0.29 offers flexibility for potential debt financing.

6. Conclusion and Recommendations:
While XPEL shows signs of growth, the decline in net income and rising liabilities are concerning. Investors should await more comprehensive financial data, including revenue figures and cash flow details, before making informed decisions. The company's ability to manage inventory and control expenses will be crucial for future profitability.

Final Assessment: Caution is advised due to mixed signals and the need for more data. Potential investors should monitor XPEL's ability to sustain growth and improve profitability.
