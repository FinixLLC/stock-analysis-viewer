Analysis of Acushnet Holdings Corp.'s Financial Health

Positive Indicators:
1. Revenue Growth: Both quarter-over-quarter and year-to-date, revenue has increased, indicating strong sales performance.
2. Liquidity: Cash and equivalents have risen, enhancing short-term financial stability.
3. Profitability: Gross profit improved for the nine months, and income before taxes increased, suggesting effective cost management.

Areas of Concern:
1. Debt Levels: Both short-term and long-term debt have increased, raising potential risks related to debt servicing if revenue growth slows.
2. Operating Expenses: Selling, general, and administrative expenses are rising, which could impact profit margins if not controlled efficiently.

Missing Information:
- EBITDA and operating cash flow figures, which would provide deeper insights into profitability and liquidity.
- Industry specifics, affecting market position and competitive analysis.
- Off-balance-sheet items and contingent liabilities that might pose financial risks.

Risks:
- Increased debt could strain financial flexibility.
- Rising expenses may indicate inefficiencies or aggressive spending strategies.

Opportunities:
- Potential for sustained revenue growth through expansion or new markets.
- Strong cash position supports investments or acquisitions to drive further growth.

Conclusion:
Acushnet shows promising signs of growth with rising revenues and profits, but the company must manage debt and expenses carefully. Additional financial metrics and industry context would enhance this analysis, providing a clearer picture of sustainability and market positioning.
