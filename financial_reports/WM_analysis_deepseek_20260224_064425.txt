Investment Assessment of Waste Management Inc. (WM)

1. Financial Overview:
- Net Income: WM's net income decreased by $180 million from $2,147 million in 2024 to $1,967 million in 2025. This decline could be due to higher operational costs or reduced revenues.
- Cash Flow: Despite the drop in net income, cash flow from operations increased by $466 million, indicating improved efficiency or effective working capital management.

2. Capital Expenditure and Acquisitions:
- WM invested heavily in capital expenditures ($2,339 million) and acquisitions ($395 million), suggesting strategic investments in infrastructure and expansion. This could enhance future profitability but ties up significant cash.

3. Debt Management:
- The company engaged in substantial debt activity, with new borrowings of $14,956 million offset by repayments of $15,772 million. While this indicates a focus on optimizing the capital structure, it also poses risks if interest rates rise.

4. Risks and Opportunities:
- Risks: Heavy capex could strain cash flow if returns are insufficient. Rising debt levels may increase financing costs.
- Opportunities: Investments in sustainable practices align with growing regulatory and consumer demands for eco-friendly services, positioning WM for long-term growth.

5. External Factors:
- Competition and regulatory changes could impact profitability. However, WM's strong cash position supports its ability to navigate these challenges.

6. Conclusion:
WM demonstrates strong cash management and strategic investments. While the net income decline is concerning, their operational efficiency and prudent debt management suggest potential for long-term growth. Investors should monitor margin improvements and external market conditions closely.
