5. Opportunities:

6. Data Gaps:
- Absence of detailed financial metrics (income statement, balance sheet) hinders a comprehensive assessment of profitability, solvency, and liquidity.

7. Conclusion:
GPI's financial health is challenging to evaluate due to insufficient data. While there are risks related to market conditions and geographic focus, opportunities exist through diversification and innovation. Further detailed financial analysis is recommended before making investment decisions.
