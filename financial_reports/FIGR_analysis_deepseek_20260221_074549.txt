Key Areas of Analysis:

1. Liquidity:

   - Quick Ratio: Assesses liquidity without inventory, providing a more stringent measure.

2. Profitability:
   - Net Profit Margin: Measures net income relative to revenue.
   - Return on Equity (ROE): Reflects how well the company uses shareholders' equity to generate profits.
   - Return on Assets (ROA): Indicates efficiency in using assets to generate earnings.

3. Leverage:
   - Debt-to-Equity Ratio: Balances debt against equity, signaling financial risk.
   - Interest Coverage Ratio: Measures ability to meet interest obligations with earnings before interest and taxes (EBITDA).

4. Efficiency:
   - Asset Turnover: Assesses how effectively assets generate revenue.
   - Inventory Turnover: Indicates inventory management efficiency.

5. Growth:
   - Revenue Growth: Year-over-year comparison to assess expansion.
   - Net Income Growth: Reflects profitability trends over time.
