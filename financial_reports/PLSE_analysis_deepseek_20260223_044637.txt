Weaknesses:

Risks:

- Operational Losses: The increasing deficit suggests persistent issues with profitability, which could hinder long-term growth and sustainability.

Opportunities:
- Lease Optimization: Reviewing lease agreements could lead to cost savings, freeing up funds for other investments.
- Revenue Growth: Strategies to boost sales or operational efficiency could improve net income and reduce the accumulated deficit.

Recommendations:
1. Focus on Profitability: Implement measures to increase revenue and reduce expenses to address the growing deficit.
2. Improve Cash Management: Explore ways to enhance cash inflows, such as optimizing inventory turnover or improving receivables management.
3. Strategic Leasing: Negotiate better lease terms or consider relocating if current leases are too burdensome.

Conclusion:
