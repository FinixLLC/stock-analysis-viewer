4. Risks:

- Uncertainty around credit quality and interest rate sensitivity without additional data.

5. Opportunities:

- Effective management of credit risk, suggesting prudent lending practices.

6. Investment Assessment:
Tompkins Financial Corporation shows robust financial performance, but investors should remain vigilant about expense trends and seek more comprehensive financial disclosures. The company's ability to maintain profitability while managing expenses will be key to sustained growth.
