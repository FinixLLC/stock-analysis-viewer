Investment Analysis Report: Alkermes PLC (ALKS)

---

### Financial Health Overview

1. Revenue Growth & Profitability: 
   - Review the Income Statement for trends in revenue growth and net income. Look for signs of increasing sales and profitability, especially considering ALKS's position in the biotech sector.

2. Cash Flow Analysis:
   - Positive cash flow from operating activities indicates strong liquidity. Check capital expenditures (investing activities) to understand expansion or equipment upgrades. Financing activities will reveal debt or equity issuance details.

3. Balance Sheet Insights:
   - Assess liquidity through the current ratio (current assets / current liabilities). A ratio above 1 suggests comfortable short-term obligations coverage.
   - Evaluate shareholders' equity for consistent growth, indicating strong net worth and efficient management.

4. Financial Ratios:
   - Calculate key ratios such as P/E ratio, debt-to-equity, and gross margin to gauge financial health and risk levels.

### Risks

2. Product Dependency:

3. Market Volatility:
   - The biotech sector's volatility and competition necessitate understanding ALKS's market position relative to peers.

### Opportunities
