   - Accumulated other comprehensive loss increased, which may include factors like foreign exchange fluctuations.

6. Cash Flow Concerns:
   - Missing cash flow data hinders a complete assessment of liquidity and financial health, crucial for investment decisions.

7. Risks:

   - Decreasing cash levels and growing accumulated deficit indicate potential financial strain.

8. Opportunities:

Conclusion:
