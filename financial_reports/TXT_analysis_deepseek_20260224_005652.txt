1. Strong Cash Flow: The company has demonstrated robust cash generation from operations, which is a significant strength. This has allowed them to increase their cash reserves despite other cash outflows.

3. Efficient Asset Management: Reductions in accounts receivable and inventory suggest effective management of these assets, improving liquidity.

2. Debt Management: While long-term debt was issued in 2025, the impact of this on financial flexibility and interest coverage ratios needs assessment to gauge risk levels.

3. Industry Context: Missing information on industry conditions and competitor performance makes it challenging to evaluate Textron's relative position and growth prospects.

Risks:
1. Debt Levels: Increased debt could pose risks if interest rates rise or earnings decline, affecting their ability to service debt.

2. Market Concentration: Potential vulnerability if the company is overly reliant on specific markets or products with fluctuating demand.

Opportunities:
1. Strategic Acquisitions: Expanding through acquisitions in new markets or product lines could drive growth, though past success and pipeline strength are crucial considerations.

2. Investment in Innovation: R&D investments for new products could enhance competitiveness and open new revenue streams.

3. Shareholder Value: With strong cash reserves, considering dividend increases or further share buybacks could be strategic moves.

Conclusion:
Textron is managing well with improved cash flow and effective asset management, but it must monitor debt levels and industry risks. Strategic focus on growth opportunities and prudent financial management will be key to sustaining success.
