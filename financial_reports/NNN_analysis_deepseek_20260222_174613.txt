5. Investment Consideration:
While NNN shows growth in total equity, the accumulated deficit is a concern. Potential investors should consider the company's ability to reverse losses and manage dilution effectively before making decisions.

NNN presents mixed signals with positive capital raising but concerning net losses. Further analysis of revenue, expenses, cash flow, and debt is essential for a comprehensive investment decision.
