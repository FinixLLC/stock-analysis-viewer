- Competition: Performance relative to peers and new market entrants is not discussed.

6. Investment Considerations:
- Revenue and Margin Trends: Essential for understanding growth and efficiency.
- Debt Management: Monitoring debt levels and repayment strategies is crucial.
- Market Position: Assessing competitive strength against peers.

DRI presents opportunities through its strong brand presence but faces significant risks from rising costs and lease obligations.
