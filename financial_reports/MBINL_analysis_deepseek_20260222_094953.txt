- Liabilities Growth: Around $1.63 billion (14.3%), primarily from deposit increases.
- Positive Trends: Deposit growth, reduced borrowings, indicating improved financial health.

5. Concerns:

- The significant increase in noninterest-bearing deposits is notable; potential reasons include new customers or retention strategies.

6. Overall Assessment:
Merchants Bancorp shows financial improvement with strong deposit growth and reduced borrowings. However, more details on operational strategies are needed for a comprehensive assessment.
