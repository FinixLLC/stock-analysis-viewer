5. Opportunities and Risks:

- Risks: Concerns include possible goodwill impairment, limited cash reserves, and the impact of high accounts payable on financial stability.

6. Conclusion:
While SKY exhibits a stable financial structure with robust retained earnings, the lack of detailed liquidity information and elevated goodwill pose significant risks. Investors should approach this opportunity with caution, seeking additional financial data to make informed decisions. Further analysis is essential before considering investment in SKY.
