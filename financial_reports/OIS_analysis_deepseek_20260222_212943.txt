Analysis of Oil States International (OIS) Financial Health

1. Overview:
- Filing Type: 10-Q (quarterly report)
- Date Analyzed: February 2026
- Ticker Symbol: OIS on NYSE
- Outstanding Shares: ~59.7 million as of October 2025

2. Financial Information:
- The report includes unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements, which are standard for a 10-Q filing but subject to change upon audited results.

3. Risks and Opportunities:
- Risks Identified: Market conditions, competition, regulatory matters.
- Opportunities: Potential growth in oilfield services, especially with shale drilling trends.
- Missing Details: Specific competitors, intensity of competition, environmental regulations impact, and strategic initiatives.

4. Compliance and Governance:
- OIS is compliant with Section 13/15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act, indicating good corporate governance practices.

5. Industry Context:
- Oilfield services are cyclical and volatile, influenced by oil prices and shale production trends.

6. Areas for Improvement in Analysis:
- Financial Metrics: Need specific numbers (revenue, profits, debt, cash flow) to assess trends.
- Risk Factors: Require concrete examples or incidents to evaluate significance.
- Strategic Initiatives: Lack of information on how OIS mitigates risks and drives growth.

7. Conclusion:
While the report provides a foundational analysis, it lacks depth in financial data and specific risk details. To fully assess OIS's investment potential, additional information on financial performance, industry context, and strategic actions is necessary.
