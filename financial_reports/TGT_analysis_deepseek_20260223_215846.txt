6. Dividends and Retained Earnings:

- Retained Earnings: Growth in retained earnings supports ongoing investments and strategic planning.

7. Risks and Considerations:
- Net Earnings Decline: The decrease in net earnings warrants attention to ensure it's not indicative of broader issues such as margin compression or increased costs.
- Capital Expenditure Risk: While beneficial for future growth, excessive investment without corresponding returns could strain cash flow.

8. Conclusion:

However, investors should closely monitor the net earnings trend and capital expenditure efficiency to ensure these initiatives yield expected returns. The company's position appears solid for long-term growth, contingent on effective management of current investments and lease obligations.
