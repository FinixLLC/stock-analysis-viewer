Investment Analysis of Oak Street Health, Inc. (OSH)

Overview:
Oak Street Health is a healthcare services company focused on providing primary care to seniors in the U.S. The analysis covers their financial performance from 2023 to 2025.

Key Findings:

1. Revenue Growth:
   - From 2023 to 2024, revenue increased by 14%, showing strong growth.
   - However, from 2024 to 2025, revenue growth dropped significantly to 0.15%, indicating potential market saturation or external challenges.

2. Net Income:
   - Net income improved from -$636k in 2023 to -$301k in 2024, but remained negative.
   - In 2025, net income slightly decreased due to accretion expenses, highlighting profitability concerns.

3. Accretion Expenses:
   - Significant accretion expenses ($4.9 million in 2023 and $958k in 2025) negatively impact net income, suggesting issues with convertible preferred shares or similar instruments.

4. Cash Flow:
   - Positive operating cash flow indicates strong liquidity for covering expenses.
   - Negative investing and financing cash flows suggest expansion or capital raising activities.

Key Metrics:
- Gross Margin: Improved from 12% to 14%, showing operational efficiency.
- Operating Expenses: Decreased significantly, indicating effective cost management.
- Net Margin: Remains negative, with no profitability yet.

Risks and Concerns:
- High Accretion Expenses: A major drain on net income.
- Revenue Growth Decline: The sharp drop in growth from 14% to near flat raises sustainability concerns.
- Market Concentration: Reliance on a few states poses geographic risk.
- Regulatory Risks: Potential impact from healthcare regulations not discussed.

Opportunities:
- Growing senior population presents demand for their services.
- Effective cost management could support future profitability.

Investment Assessment:
While OSH shows initial positive signs with revenue growth and improving net income, the high accretion expenses and significant drop in revenue growth are concerning. The company's path to profitability is uncertain without addressing these issues.

Conclusion:
OSH exhibits potential but faces critical challenges, particularly regarding profitability and sustainability of growth. Investors should closely monitor their ability to reduce accretion expenses and maintain or increase revenue growth.
