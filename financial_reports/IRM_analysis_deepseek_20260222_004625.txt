Investment Analysis of Iron Mountain Incorporated (IRM)

1. Financial Overview:
- Assets: Increased from $18.7 million to $20.6 million, indicating growth in investments or expansion.
- Liabilities: Rose significantly from $14.39 million to $16.95 million, raising concerns about financial leverage.
- Equity: Deteriorated into a larger deficit ($304k to $683k), signaling potential capital issues.

2. Income Statement:
- Revenue Growth: Both storage rental and service revenues increased, reflecting strong demand in their core business segments.
- Operating Expenses: Faster growth of expenses (cost of sales and SG&A) compared to revenue, leading to a net income decline from $78k to $59k.

3. Balance Sheet:
- Current Assets: Slight increase in cash and accounts receivable, supporting liquidity but not offsetting the liability growth.
- Long-term Debt: A significant rise of over $2.4 million, posing a substantial financial risk.

4. Risks:
- Debt Burden: High debt levels could strain cash flow and require careful management to avoid financial stress.
- Negative Equity: Indicates potential capital needs or ownership concerns if not addressed.

5. Opportunities:
- Revenue Growth: Strong performance in storage and services suggests a viable market position with expansion potential.
- Industry Trends: Growing demand for data security could further benefit IRM, provided they manage costs effectively.

6. Missing Information:
- No details on non-operating income/expenses or cash flow statements, which are crucial for comprehensive analysis.

Conclusion:
IRM shows revenue growth but faces challenges with rising expenses and debt. While the industry offers opportunities, investors should be cautious due to financial risks. Further information on cash flows and debt management is essential before making investment decisions.
