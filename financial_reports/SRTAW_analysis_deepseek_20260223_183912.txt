Risks:
- High operating expenses relative to revenue could lead to continued losses if not managed effectively.
- Potential financial risk from high debt without sufficient cash flow stability.

Opportunities:
- Strong market position or effective sales strategies driving revenue growth. Sustaining this growth with controlled expenses could improve profitability.

Recommendations:
- Further investigation into the nature of discontinued operations and their sustainability is crucial.
- Obtain comprehensive financial data, including cash flows, to assess overall health and investment risk.

Conclusion:
While there are positive indicators like revenue growth, the reliance on one-time gains and missing financial data complicate the assessment. Investors should seek additional information before making informed decisions about SRTAW as an investment opportunity.
