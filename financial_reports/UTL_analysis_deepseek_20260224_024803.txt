6. Areas for Further Data:

7. Competitive Landscape:
- While AWS and Azure are noted, other competitors and new entrants could impact UTL's market share, requiring deeper analysis.

UTL shows promise with revenue growth and a strong cash position, but lacks critical details necessary for a comprehensive investment assessment. To make an informed decision, further data on revenue composition, margins, customer metrics, and competitive positioning are essential.
