Analysis of KRT's Financial Health

1. Revenue Growth: 
   - Consistent increase in both three-month ($10%$) and nine-month ($9.7%$) periods, indicating expansion or efficiency improvements.

2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):
   - Higher percentage increases than revenue: $17%$ for three months and $12%$ for nine months, potentially compressing margins.

3. Gross Profit:
   - Slight decrease in three-month ($0.24%$) but modest improvement over nine months ($7%$), suggesting challenges in maintaining profitability despite revenue growth.

4. Operating Expenses:
   - Rising by $6%$ in both periods, possibly due to operational investments or inefficiencies, affecting margins.

5. Operating Income:
   - Significant decline in three-month ($24%$) and slight decrease over nine months, highlighting pressure on profitability despite revenue growth.

6. Other Income/Expenses:
   - Fluctuating results: increase in three months but decrease over nine months, indicating potential one-time factors.

7. Net Income and EPS:
   - Decrease in both periods for net income ($17%$ drop in three months), with EPS reflecting this trend, despite a slight improvement over nine months due to share growth.

8. Weighted Average Common Shares:
   - Slight increase, potentially diluting EPS and complicating profit sustainability.

9. External and Internal Factors:
   - Consider industry trends and macroeconomic factors affecting costs. Internal management decisions on expenses and cost control are crucial.

10. Risks and Concerns:
    - Margin compression due to rising COGS and operating expenses, necessitating strategic cost management and investment returns.

Conclusion: KRT demonstrates revenue growth but faces profitability challenges due to escalating costs and expenses. The company must address these issues to ensure sustainable growth and margin improvement.
