Analysis of LE's Financial Performance:

   - Year-to-Date: Still a loss of $6.8 million, though improved from last year's $12.3 million loss.

7. Earnings Per Share (EPS):
   - Improved to profitability in the latest quarter, signaling positive trends for shareholders.

Conclusion:
LE is showing signs of recovery with improved profitability in the latest quarter and reduced expenses. However, the year-to-date figures indicate they are still not breaking even overall. The company's ability to sustain these improvements will be crucial for long-term success. Investors may view recent performance positively but would likely seek consistent results over more periods before fully committing.
