4. Opportunities:

5. External Factors:

6. Investment Assessment:
While MCK demonstrates robust financial health with improving profitability and liquidity, the lack of detailed debt information and segment performance data introduces uncertainty. MCK shows positive signs of financial strength, but further analysis on debt levels, segment performance, and accounting policies is necessary to fully assess its investment potential.
