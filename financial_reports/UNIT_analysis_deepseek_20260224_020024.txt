Balance Sheet:

Key Transactions:

Risks:
1. Financial Risk: High debt levels and net loss raise solvency concerns.
2. Operational Risk: Late supplier payments indicate potential cash flow issues.
3. Legal Risk: Pending IP lawsuit poses financial threats.
4. Customer Concentration: 30% revenue from one customer increases dependency risk.

Opportunities:
1. Cost Reduction: Potential to cut expenses and improve efficiency.
2. Diversification: Reducing reliance on a single customer by expanding markets or products.
3. Supply Chain Management: Improving supplier relations to enhance stability.

Investment Assessment:
UNIT faces significant risks, including high debt, legal issues, and customer concentration. While there are opportunities for improvement, the current financial health is precarious. More data on trends, industry comparisons, and detailed debt structure would be crucial before making an investment decision. A cautious approach is recommended until these risks are mitigated and profitability improves.
