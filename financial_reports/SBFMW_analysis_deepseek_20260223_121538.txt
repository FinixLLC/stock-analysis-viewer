- Intangible Assets Decline: Potential loss of competitive edge due to reduced IP assets.

- Investment in Assets: Property and equipment growth indicates future capacity building.

Limitations of Analysis:

- No trend analysis over time or external factors affecting performance.

Conclusion:
Sunshine Biopharma Inc. shows signs of operational activity with inventory buildup and asset investment but faces risks from high inventory and reduced intangible assets.
