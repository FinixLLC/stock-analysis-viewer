Investment Assessment Report: Nasdaq Inc. (NDAQ)

1. Executive Summary:
Nasdaq Inc. demonstrates positive revenue growth across both quarterly and year-to-date metrics, driven by gains in Capital Access Platforms and Financial Technology segments. However, operating expenses are rising, particularly in compensation and technology infrastructure, which may impact future profitability.

2. Key Strengths:
- Revenue Growth: Consistent increases in both Q3 2025 vs Q3 2024 and year-to-date performance indicate expansion.
- Strong Cash Position: Maintained liquidity, crucial for strategic investments and stability.
- Gross Profit Margin: Despite a slight decrease, operating income has improved significantly.

3. Risks:
- Increased Debt: Higher leverage poses risks in economic downturns or rising interest rates.
- Revenue Concentration: Dependence on Market Services, which showed a decline, could affect overall performance.

4. Opportunities:
- Segment Growth: Expansion into higher-margin areas like Capital Access and Financial Technology offers potential.
- Strategic Flexibility: Strong cash reserves allow for investments or shareholder returns.

5. Areas of Concern:
- Expense Management: Rising compensation and technology costs need justification to ensure they support growth.
- Macroeconomic Factors: External factors such as interest rates and market volatility could impact performance.

6. Investment Consideration:
While NDAQ shows promising growth, investors should monitor expense trends and debt levels. Sustaining revenue growth and managing expenses effectively will be crucial for long-term profitability.

In conclusion, Nasdaq Inc. presents a cautiously optimistic outlook with strong fundamentals but requires close monitoring of operational efficiency and debt management to realize its investment potential.
