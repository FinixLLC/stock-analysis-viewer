## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (CRWD_10-K_20260826.txt)

CrowdStrike is a cybersecurity company that specializes in providing cloud-native, AI-driven solutions to protect organizations against cyber threats. Founded in 2011, the company developed the Falcon platform, an AI-native cybersecurity operating system designed to detect and prevent breaches by leveraging vast amounts of security data. The platform uses a single lightweight sensor to collect data across endpoints, cloud workloads, identities, and third-party sources, which is then ingested once and reused for multiple security functions. This approach allows CrowdStrike to train its AI models continuously, improving threat detection and response capabilities in real time.

The company's competitive moat lies in its cloud-native architecture and the ability to harness high-fidelity data from a global network of customers, creating a powerful network effect. This enables CrowdStrike to anticipate and counter evolving cyber threats more effectively than legacy cybersecurity products, which are often on-premise, siloed, and unable to scale with the increasing complexity and speed of modern attacks.

Management's priorities include expanding customer adoption through a land-and-expand sales strategy, investing in research and development to enhance the Falcon platform, and leveraging professional services as a lead generator for new subscriptions. The company also focuses on maintaining high customer retention rates and increasing sales by offering flexible subscription models and additional cloud modules.

The 10-K filing highlights several material risks, including the July 19, 2024 incident, which resulted in system crashes for certain Windows systems due to a sensor update. This incident led to legal claims, increased expenses, and potential impacts on customer retention and sales cycles. Additionally, CrowdStrike faces challenges related to market adoption of cloud-based solutions, competition from legacy vendors, and the need to maintain high gross retention rates amidst evolving customer demands and potential discounts. The company must also navigate elongated sales cycles and manage the risks associated with rapid technological advancements in AI and cybersecurity.
