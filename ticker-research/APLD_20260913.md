## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (APLD_10-K_20260906.txt)

Applied Digital Corporation (APLD) is a U.S.-based company that designs, develops, and operates high-performance, sustainably engineered data centers and colocation services for artificial intelligence (AI), networking, and blockchain workloads. The company focuses on providing digital infrastructure solutions to the rapidly growing industries of high-performance computing (HPC) and AI. As of May 31, 2026, APLD operates in two core business segments: Data Center Hosting and HPC Hosting.

The Data Center Hosting Business provides energized infrastructure services primarily to crypto mining customers. The company currently serves one such customer with a remaining contractual term of approximately one and a half years. As of May 31, 2026, the company operates two facilities in Jamestown and Ellendale, North Dakota, with capacities of 106 MW and 180 MW, respectively, both operating at full capacity.

The HPC Hosting Business is focused on designing, constructing, and operating next-generation data centers to support high-density computing applications such as AI, machine learning, and other HPC developments. The company has multiple projects in development across its Polaris Forge campuses, with a total expected capacity of 400 MW at Polaris Forge 1, an additional 200 MW at Polaris Forge 2, and plans for further expansion at Polaris Forge 3. These facilities are designed to host high-density graphics processing unit (GPU) architectures and other HPC applications.

APLD has established partnerships with hyperscalers and other large customers, including CoreWeave and two U.S.-based investment-grade hyperscalers, securing long-term leases for significant portions of its data center capacity. The company generated $385.3 million in revenue from the HPC Hosting Business during the fiscal year ended May 31, 2026.

In addition to its core businesses, APLD completed the divestiture of its cloud business into a separate entity, ChronoScale Corporation (CHRN), on May 5, 2026. ChronoScale operates the company's historic cloud services business, providing AI and machine learning developers with access to cloud computing equipment at third-party colocation centers in Colorado, Minnesota, and Utah.

The company faces risks related to legal proceedings, including a securities class action lawsuit and a derivative action alleging material misstatements regarding its business operations. These lawsuits could result in significant financial losses if resolved unfavorably. Additionally, the company's ability to execute on its growth strategy depends on securing additional capital, managing construction timelines, and maintaining customer relationships.

Management has prioritized strategic investments in HPC infrastructure, partnerships with hyperscalers, and operational efficiency to support long-term growth. The company also completed organizational restructuring to facilitate debt offerings for specific projects, enhancing its financial flexibility.

In summary, APLD is positioned as a key player in the high-performance computing and AI infrastructure sectors, with a focus on expanding its data center capacity to meet growing demand from hyperscalers and other large customers. However, it faces material risks related to ongoing legal disputes and the execution of its growth strategy.
