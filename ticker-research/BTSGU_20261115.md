## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (BTSGU_10-K_20260906.txt)

BTSGU operates as a leading home and community-based healthcare services platform, delivering complementary pharmacy and provider services to complex patients, specifically those with Senior and Specialty needs including behavioral populations. The company’s business model is built on an integrated, scaled approach that addresses critical services for high-need, high-cost patients in lower-cost home and community settings, primarily serving Medicare, Medicaid, and commercially-insured populations. This platform combines daily medication therapy management through a local pharmacy and delivery model with clinical and supportive care services, such as home health, hospice, rehab therapy, and home-based primary care. By co-locating these services in 40 states and serving over 465,000 patients daily across all 50 states, BTSGU leverages its scale to drive operational excellence, reduce hospitalizations, and manage chronic conditions more effectively than disjointed, separately-administered health services.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its market-leading scale, which provides significant economies of scale in purchasing, supplier contracting, technology, and payor relations. Additionally, the company differentiates itself through its specialized focus on high-touch, white-glove service delivery for medically complex patients, achieving high quality metrics such as 99.99% order accuracy in pharmacy and significantly lower hospitalization rates in home-based primary care compared to national averages. The integration of pharmacy and provider services creates a longitudinal view of patient needs, fostering strong relationships and enabling value-based care strategies that improve outcomes and reduce costs.

Management’s stated priorities center on delivering high-quality, cost-effective care to a growing population of patients with chronic conditions, while leveraging the company’s integrated platform to drive growth and operational efficiency. Key strategic initiatives include expanding the use of complementary pharmacy and provider services to address multiple patient needs, enhancing value-based care capabilities through home-based primary care, transitional care management, and clinical care coordination hubs, and executing value-based care contracts with internal ACOs and external payors. The company is also focused on growing its Infusion and Specialty Pharmacy segment, which has shown significant revenue growth, and continuing to invest in technology and positions to support business expansion.

Additionally, the company highlights the risks associated with its dependence on a limited number of specialty branded drugs for revenue growth in the Infusion and Specialty Pharmacy segment, as well as the challenges of maintaining high service levels and quality metrics in a highly competitive and regulated healthcare environment.
