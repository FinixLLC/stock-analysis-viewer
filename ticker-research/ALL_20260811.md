## Business Profile

Source: FY2025 10-K (fiscal year ended 2025-12-31, filed 2026-02-20), fetched live from SEC EDGAR and read directly (Item 1 Business).

What the company does: Allstate is the third-largest personal property-and-casualty insurer in the U.S. by 2024 statutory direct premiums written (per A.M. Best), with 211 million policies in force, ~53,000 employees, and an $83.24B investment portfolio. Best known via its "You're In Good Hands With Allstate®" brand.

Reportable segments:
1. Allstate Protection — private passenger auto, homeowners, other personal lines, and select commercial P&C insurance, sold through exclusive agents, independent agents, contact centers, and online, under the Allstate, National General, Direct Auto, and Answer Financial brands.
2. Run-off Property-Liability — legacy P&C policies written from the 1960s through the mid-1980s, carrying asbestos, environmental, and other run-off claim exposure.
3. Protection Services — Protection Plans, Roadside assistance, Dealer Services, Identity Protection, and Arity (mobility intelligence/analytics using automotive telematics data).
4. Corporate — holding-company activities and certain non-insurance operations.

Strategy, in the company's own words: two-pronged — increase personal-lines market share and broaden protection offerings, executed via "Transformative Growth," a multi-channel distribution strategy (exclusive agency, independent agency, direct) intended to deliver "affordable, simple and connected" protection at low cost. The company frames its explicit objective as being a "low cost digital provider."

Moat / competitive framing: brand recognition (the "Good Hands" positioning), multi-channel distribution scale, and a stated focus on disciplined underwriting/pricing to "earn attractive returns."
