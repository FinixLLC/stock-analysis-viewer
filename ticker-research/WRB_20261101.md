The most material risks flagged in the filing relate to the inherent uncertainty in estimating loss reserves, which represent approximately $19.0 billion of the company’s liabilities. The filing highlights that long-tail lines, such as excess casualty and assumed reinsurance, carry higher degrees of uncertainty and variability due to extended claim settlement tails and reliance on data from ceding companies.
