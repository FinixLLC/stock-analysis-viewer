The company operates two main chains: Ross Dress for Less and dd's DISCOUNTS, which cater to different customer segments but share a common business model of offering high-quality products at significantly lower prices than department stores or other retailers. This strategy allows the company to attract price-conscious consumers seeking value without compromising on quality.

The competitive moat of Ross Stores is built on its ability to source merchandise efficiently and deliver it to customers at compelling prices. Additionally, Ross Stores' focus on customer experience through store improvements and marketing initiatives helps differentiate it from competitors.

The resolution of these legal matters and the ability to navigate economic uncertainties are critical for maintaining profitability and growth.
