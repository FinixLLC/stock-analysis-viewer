## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ULTA_10-K_20260827.txt)

Ulta Beauty, Inc. operates as the largest specialty beauty retailer in the United States, founded in 1990 to bridge the traditional separation between prestige, mass, and salon product channels. The company’s business model centers on a "beauty enthusiast" target consumer, offering a differentiated assortment of approximately 30,000 products from around 600 brands across all price points, alongside full-service salon operations. Ulta’s competitive moat is built on several key pillars: a dominant omnichannel footprint with over 1,500 U.S. stores and robust digital capabilities; a highly effective loyalty program with over 46 million members, which drives approximately 95% of total sales and provides deep consumer data for personalization; and exclusive or first-to-market access to certain brands, including its private label, Ulta Beauty Collection. Additionally, the company leverages its scale to offer a seamless shopping experience that integrates physical discovery with digital convenience, such as same-day delivery and buy-online-pickup-in-store options.

Management’s stated priorities are outlined in the "Ulta Beauty Unleashed" plan, which focuses on three strategic areas: driving core business growth through operational excellence, store execution, and digital acceleration; scaling new, accretive businesses such as wellness products, the UB Marketplace, international expansion, and retail media; and aligning the company’s foundation for future success by optimizing workflows, streamlining costs, and fostering an associate-centered culture. The company aims to grow its U.S. store footprint to more than 1,800 locations while simultaneously enhancing its digital presence and expanding into the converging beauty and wellness categories.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily centered on the company’s reliance on consumer spending resilience amidst persistent inflationary and macroeconomic pressures, which could impact sales growth and profitability. Ulta also highlights the competitive nature of the beauty industry, noting that it competes across all major categories and price points against various distribution channels. Furthermore, the company acknowledges that its performance is dependent on its ability to execute strategic priorities, manage the complexities of its omnichannel operations, and maintain high levels of guest engagement in a rapidly evolving market where consumer expectations continue to shift. The filing also notes that comparable sales have fluctuated in the past and are expected to continue to fluctuate due to factors such as general economic conditions, merchandise strategy changes, and the effectiveness of marketing activities.
