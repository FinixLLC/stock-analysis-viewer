## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (FTNT_10-K_20260906.txt)

Fortinet is a leading cybersecurity company that specializes in integrating networking and security solutions to protect people, devices, and data across various environments. The company's core platform, the Fortinet Security Fabric, encompasses secure networking, unified Secure Access Service Edge (SASE), and AI-driven security operations (SecOps). This integrated approach allows organizations to manage and secure their networks efficiently while addressing evolving cybersecurity threats.

Fortinet's competitive moat is built on its proprietary technologies, including FortiOS, a unified operating system that converges networking and AI-powered security; FortiASIC, which enhances performance and efficiency through specialized hardware; and FortiCloud, a global cloud infrastructure that provides flexible connectivity and cost savings. Additionally, the company leverages FortiAI, an advanced AI framework, to enhance threat detection and response capabilities, offering dual-layered defense across its platform.

Management's stated priorities include driving innovation in secure networking, expanding SASE solutions, and leveraging AI to improve SecOps. The company is also focused on addressing the cybersecurity skills shortage through training and certification programs, aiming to equip customers, partners, and employees with essential skills.

The 10-K filing highlights several material risks, including macroeconomic factors such as inflation, interest rate changes, and currency fluctuations; supply chain constraints that could affect manufacturing capacity and delivery times; competition in the cybersecurity market; and the need to maintain a skilled workforce. Additionally, the company faces risks related to government regulation, geopolitical tensions, and the unpredictability of sales cycles, particularly for large enterprise customers.

Fortinet's comprehensive portfolio of over 50 products and services is designed to provide end-to-end security solutions across multiple industries, including financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, and government organizations. The company's commitment to innovation, coupled with its extensive patent portfolio and recognition in industry reports, underscores its position as a leader in the cybersecurity space.
