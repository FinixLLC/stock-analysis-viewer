## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (AVGO_10-K_20260906.txt)

Broadcom (AVGO) is a global technology leader that designs, develops, and supplies semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company operates through two main segments: semiconductor solutions and infrastructure software. Its semiconductor offerings include a wide range of products such as custom accelerators, Ethernet switching and routing silicon, NICs, PHYs, optical components, RF front-end modules, and storage controllers. These solutions cater to various markets, including AI data centers, networking, wireless devices, servers, storage systems, broadband access, and industrial applications. The company's infrastructure software portfolio includes private cloud, mainframe software, cybersecurity, enterprise software, and FC SAN management solutions, enabling customers to modernize, optimize, and secure their IT environments.

Broadcom's competitive moat is built on its extensive IP portfolio, deep engineering expertise, and a robust R&D strategy combined with strategic acquisitions. The company has a long-standing history of innovation, tracing back to its origins from AT&T/Bell Labs, Lucent, and Hewlett-Packard, and has expanded through significant acquisitions like VMware. This allows Broadcom to maintain technology leadership and deliver category-leading solutions across multiple markets.

Management's priorities include sustaining technology leadership, driving innovation in AI-related products, expanding into cloud-based solutions, and enhancing customer relationships. The company focuses on strengthening ties with hyperscalers, enterprise customers, and government agencies while increasing penetration of its software offerings.

Key risks flagged in the filing include dependence on a few major customers, economic and market conditions affecting demand, inventory management challenges, supply chain disruptions, cybersecurity threats, regulatory changes, and tax-related uncertainties. These factors could impact revenue growth, profitability, and operational efficiency.
