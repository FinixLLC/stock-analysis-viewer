## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (QLYS_10-K_20260827.txt)

Qualys is a leading provider of a cloud-based platform that delivers integrated IT, security, and compliance solutions, operating on a software-as-a-service (SaaS) model primarily through renewable annual subscriptions. The company’s Enterprise TruRisk Platform allows customers to identify and manage IT and operational technology assets across on-premises, endpoint, cloud, container, and mobile environments, while simultaneously discovering vulnerabilities, quantifying cyber risk, and verifying remediation actions. Qualys generates revenue by invoicing customers for the entire subscription amount at the start of the term, which is recognized ratably over the subscription period; in 2025, revenues reached $669.1 million, with 76% of the year-over-year revenue increase driven by existing customers and 63% derived from foreign markets. The company’s competitive moat is built on its unified, multi-tenant cloud architecture that eliminates the need for customers to purchase or manage hardware, offering real-time visibility and seamless scaling that contrasts with the fragmented, costly nature of traditional disparate security products. Additionally, Qualys leverages one of the industry’s largest knowledge bases of vulnerability signatures and a shared core services infrastructure—including asset tagging, big data correlation, and automated remediation workflows—that enables a single, unified view of security posture for IT, security, and compliance teams. Management’s stated priorities focus on continuing to invest in research and development to improve existing solutions and develop new capabilities, expanding sales and marketing efforts to support new platform solutions, and maintaining investments in shared cloud platform infrastructure and customer support to drive continued revenue growth from both new and existing customers. The filing flags several material risks, including the potential for macroeconomic uncertainty, inflationary pressure, high interest rates, and geopolitical conflicts to cause customers to reduce or eliminate IT security spending, as well as the risk that new sales personnel may not become productive quickly enough to offset increased hiring costs. Furthermore, the company notes that its business is exposed to the complexities of a globally distributed customer base and the evolving nature of cyber threats, while also acknowledging that the timing and amount of deferred tax asset valuation allowance releases remain subject to future profitability levels.
