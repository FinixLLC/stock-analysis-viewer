## Business Profile

Source: live SEC 10-K fetch (FY2025, filed 2026-02-26, self-extracted and synthesized directly from Item 1 Business and Item 1A Risk Factors) + Q2 2026 8-K.

What the company does: Eaton (founded 1911, Ireland-domiciled/Dublin HQ) is "an intelligent power management company" with FY2025 revenue of $27.4B across five reportable segments as of the FY2025 10-K: Electrical Americas, Electrical Global, Aerospace, Vehicle, and eMobility. Following the Q1 2026 re-segmentation and the just-announced Mobility separation, the Vehicle and eMobility segments were first combined into a single "Mobility" segment and are now slated to be spun off entirely — meaning Eaton's forward-looking core will be Electrical Americas, Electrical Global, and Aerospace, serving data center, utility, industrial, commercial, machine-building, residential, and aerospace/defense/space end markets.

Moat, as described in the company's own words: Eaton frames its position as "capitalizing on the megatrends of electrification, digitalization, and the reindustrialization of... megaprojects in North America and increased global infrastructure spending" — i.e., a broad secular-tailwind thesis rather than a narrow product moat. More specifically, the company states it has "a strong competitive position" and is "considered among the market leaders" in both Electrical (data center/utility power management) and Aerospace segments, competing on "performance of products and systems, technology, customer service and support, and price." The current data-center power-management supercycle (organic Electrical Americas orders +41% on a 12-month rolling basis) is the dominant near-term moat/growth driver being emphasized by management.

Segment mix and pending transformation: Electrical Americas + Electrical Global together are now the clear core growth engine (record sales, record orders, expanding backlogs in both), while Aerospace is a smaller but also strong (13% YoY growth, record operating profit) complementary segment. The Mobility segment (Vehicle + eMobility, ~$841M quarterly sales, roughly flat-to-slightly-declining organically) is being separated out via Reverse Morris Trust — a tax-efficient structure — expected to close Q1 2027, which will materially concentrate Eaton's remaining business toward the highest-growth, highest-margin (data center/electrification/aerospace) end of its current portfolio.

Management's stated priorities (from Item 1 and Q2 2026 earnings commentary): (1) Execute the Mobility separation cleanly and on the stated Q1 2027 timeline; (2) continue building out data-center-adjacent capacity via acquisitions (Fibrebond — modular data-center power solutions; Resilient Power Systems — solid-state transformer tech; pending Boyd Thermal — liquid cooling for hyperscale/colocation "chip to grid" thermal management; Ultra PCS — next-gen aerospace, closed Jan 2026); (3) sustain segment-margin expansion, particularly in Electrical Americas where sequential margin gains were emphasized this quarter.

Risks the company itself flags as most material (Item 1A, operational-risk-first ordering):
1. Acquisition integration risk — explicitly cited given Eaton's active, multi-deal acquisition strategy (Fibrebond, Resilient, Ultra PCS, pending Boyd Thermal, plus the upcoming Mobility separation itself as a major structural transaction with its own execution risk).
2. Global production-facility disruption risk (natural disaster, labor strike, war/geopolitical instability, public health).
3. Raw material/component/labor inflation and shortages, plus explicit single-source supplier dependency risk — "we also maintain single-source supplier relationships because either alternative sources are not available, or the relationship is advantageous."
4. AI-technology risk — both the opportunity of internal AI-driven efficiency and the risk of governance/compliance/IP-leakage issues from adopting generative AI internally, an increasingly standard disclosure across this sector.
5. Cybersecurity/IT infrastructure risk, standard for a company at this scale with global manufacturing/OT systems.
