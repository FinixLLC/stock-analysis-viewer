## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (PCAR_10-K_20260827.txt)

PACCAR operates as a global technology company with a diversified business model spanning three primary segments: Truck, Parts, and Financial Services. The Truck segment involves the design and manufacture of light-, medium-, and heavy-duty commercial vehicles, marketed under the Kenworth and Peterbilt nameplates in North America, DAF in Europe, and a mix of Kenworth and DAF in Mexico, Australia, and South America. The Parts segment distributes aftermarket components for these vehicles, while the Financial Services segment generates revenue by financing or leasing PACCAR products across North America, Europe, Australia, and South America. This integrated structure allows the company to capture value across the vehicle lifecycle, from initial sale through aftermarket support and financing.

The company’s competitive position is supported by its status as a market leader with significant scale, evidenced by its 87th consecutive year of net income and a strong balance sheet that has enabled $9.2 billion in capital investments over the past decade. PACCAR maintains a rigorous credit application process for its financial services arm, supporting a $22.80 billion loan and lease portfolio, and holds substantial market share in key regions, including approximately 30% in the U.S. and Canadian heavy-duty truck market and 13.5% in the European over-16-tonne market. The company emphasizes quality and technological innovation, investing in next-generation clean diesel and alternative powertrains, autonomous driving systems, and artificial intelligence to drive customer value and operational efficiency.

Management’s stated priorities focus on maintaining long-term profitability and a strong balance sheet while navigating a challenging demand environment. The company is actively investing in manufacturing capabilities, such as new robotic paint and engine remanufacturing facilities, and enhancing its parts distribution network to improve service delivery. Strategic initiatives include embedding artificial intelligence across the business, investing in the Amplify Cell Technologies battery joint venture, and optimizing production locations to mitigate tariff impacts, particularly by leveraging U.S.-based factories for North American markets. PACCAR also aims to grow its financial services market share and maintain consistent capital and R&D spending, with 2026 capital investments expected between $725 million and $775 million.

The filing identifies several material risks that have already impacted 2025 results and may affect future performance. The most significant risk is the adverse impact of U.S. import tariffs and trade policies, which have negatively affected truck order intake and profit margins since March 2025, despite mitigating actions such as local production. The company faces exposure to changes in tariff policy, including potential Supreme Court rulings on the International Emergency Economic Power Acts. Additionally, PACCAR is subject to regulatory risks, specifically regarding EPA emissions standards and NOx limits, which could impact product requirements and warranty costs. Economic volatility remains a key risk, as weaker freight transportation conditions could lead to increased credit losses, higher repossessions, and lower new business volume in the Financial Services segment. Finally, the company recorded a $350 million pre-tax charge in 2025 related to civil litigation in Europe, highlighting ongoing legal risks.
