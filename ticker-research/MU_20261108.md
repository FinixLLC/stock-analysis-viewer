Micron Technology operates as a leading memory and storage semiconductor manufacturer, deriving its revenue primarily from the sale of DRAM and NAND products. This suggests a strong position in the most advanced and in-demand memory technologies, allowing the company to leverage industry-wide supply discipline and constrained supply to improve pricing power.
