## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (HUM_10-K_20260906.txt)

Humana Inc. is a healthcare services company headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky, offering insurance products and health care services through its two main segments: Insurance and CenterWell. The Insurance segment provides Medicare Advantage plans, stand-alone prescription drug plans (PDPs), Medicare supplements, and state-based Medicaid contracts. It also includes pharmacy benefit management (PBM) services and military health programs. The CenterWell segment focuses on enhancing healthcare experiences through pharmacy solutions, primary care, and home health services.

Humana's competitive moat is built on its extensive network of providers, integrated care delivery model, and long-standing presence in the Medicare market. Its risk-adjustment expertise under the Medicare Advantage program allows it to secure higher payments for sicker members, contributing to profitability. Additionally, its vertically integrated operations across insurance and care services enable cost efficiencies and better member outcomes.

Management's priorities include driving growth in Medicare and Medicaid markets, enhancing member engagement through data analytics and value-based care models, and optimizing costs through workforce reductions and operational restructuring. The company is also focusing on exiting unprofitable businesses, such as the Employer Group Commercial Medical Products, to concentrate resources on higher-margin segments.

Key risks highlighted in the filing include regulatory changes impacting Medicare Advantage profitability, rising medical costs, competitive pressures, and potential payment adjustments under risk-adjustment models. Additionally, macroeconomic factors and operational challenges related to transforming its business model pose significant risks to financial performance.
