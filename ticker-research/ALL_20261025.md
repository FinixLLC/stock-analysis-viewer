## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ALL_10-K_20260906.txt)

The Allstate Corporation operates as a diversified insurance and financial services provider, with its core business model centered on the Allstate Protection segment, which writes private passenger auto, homeowners, and other personal lines insurance for consumers. The company distributes these products through a multi-channel approach involving exclusive agents, independent agents, and direct-to-consumer contact centers and online platforms. Beyond traditional property and casualty insurance, Allstate maintains a Protection Services segment that generates revenue from extended warranties (Protection Plans), roadside assistance, dealer services, identity protection, and the Arity technology platform. The company’s financial performance is driven by premium growth, underwriting discipline, and investment income, with recent strategic moves including the divestiture of its employer voluntary benefits and group health businesses to focus on its core insurance operations.

The company’s competitive moat is characterized by its scale and diversified distribution network, allowing it to offer "affordable, simple and connected protection products" across multiple channels. Allstate leverages significant reinsurance programs to manage aggregate countrywide exposure, catastrophe risk, and capital requirements, supporting the financial strength ratings of its subsidiaries. Additionally, the company utilizes intercompany reinsurance to maintain underwriting control and manages long-tail liabilities through specific reserve methodologies and indemnification mechanisms, such as the Michigan Catastrophic Claims Association (MCCA).

Management’s stated priorities focus on a "Transformative Growth" initiative, with an emphasis on international growth within the Protection Plans business and implementing rate increases in states where acceptable returns are achieved to keep pace with increasing costs. The company aims to offer products that allow customers to interact with the firm when, where, and how they want, while monitoring key performance indicators such as policies in force, new business sales, claim frequency and severity, and return on equity.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to the estimation of claims and claims expense reserves, which significantly influence underwriting results. The company notes that reserve reestimates can vary substantially based on claim activity and development factors, with 2025 results heavily impacted by favorable severity development in personal auto injury coverage. Other material risks include exposure to catastrophe losses, the potential adverse impact of U.S. government tariffs on vehicle pricing, replacement parts availability, and building material costs, and the inherent uncertainty in collecting reinsurance recoverables, particularly from reinsurers with declining financial strength. The company also highlights risks associated with macroeconomic factors, including inflation, supply chain disruptions, and the evolving global trade environment.
