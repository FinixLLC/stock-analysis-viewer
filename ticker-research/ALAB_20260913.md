## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ALAB_10-K_20260906.txt)

Astera Labs is a semiconductor company specializing in connectivity solutions for cloud and AI infrastructure. The company's business model revolves around developing and delivering its Intelligent Connectivity Platform, which integrates hardware and software to address data, network, and memory bottlenecks in high-performance systems. This platform includes products such as ICs, boards, and modules, supported by the COSMOS software suite, enabling customers to configure, manage, and optimize their connectivity solutions.

The company's competitive moat lies in its differentiated platform approach, which combines hardware with embedded software for seamless integration and customization. Astera Labs collaborates closely with hyperscalers and system OEMs, leveraging trusted relationships and early interoperability testing through its Interop Lab to ensure compatibility and reduce deployment risks. Its focus on solving specific connectivity challenges in AI and cloud infrastructure gives it a unique position in the market.

Management's priorities include maintaining strong customer relationships, driving innovation through significant R&D investments, and expanding product offerings to meet growing demand. The company emphasizes scalability, performance, and reliability, with a particular focus on addressing hyperscalers' unique requirements for cloud-scale operations.

Key risks identified in the filing include reliance on a small number of major customers, which accounted for 86% of revenue in 2025, making the company vulnerable to changes in their demand. Additionally, the company faces intense competition from established players like Broadcom and Marvell, which could impact its market share. Other risks include supply chain disruptions, given its reliance on third-party manufacturers, and the need to maintain intellectual property protection to sustain its competitive edge.
