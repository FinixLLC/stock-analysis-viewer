## Business Profile

Source: FY2026 10-K (fiscal year ended 2026-01-31, filed 2026-03-11), fetched live from SEC EDGAR and read directly (Item 1 Business).

What the company does: MongoDB describes itself as "the developer data platform company," whose mission is to empower developers via "the world's leading, modern general purpose database." Its core product is built on a unique document-based architecture designed to handle unstructured data while retaining the performance/reliability strengths traditionally associated with relational databases. Organizations can deploy it in the cloud, on-premises, or in hybrid environments.

Platform breadth: beyond the core database, the developer data platform includes tightly integrated capabilities — search, time series, data lifecycle management, application-driven analytics, and stream processing — intended to let developers address a broader range of application requirements from a single vendor.

Business model: explicitly combines "the developer mindshare and adoption benefits of open source with the economic benefits of a proprietary software subscription business model" — i.e., a land-and-expand motion built on free/open-source developer adoption that converts into paid Atlas (cloud) and Enterprise Advanced subscriptions.

Company history: incorporated in Delaware in November 2007 as "10Gen, Inc.," renamed MongoDB, Inc. in August 2013; headquartered in New York City.
