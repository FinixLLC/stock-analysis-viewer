## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (MP_10-K_20260827.txt)

MP Materials Corp. is the largest producer of rare earth materials in the Western Hemisphere and the only scaled, vertically integrated source of critical rare earths and magnet materials in North America. The company operates through two reportable segments: Materials and Magnetics. The Materials segment encompasses upstream and midstream operations at the Mountain Pass facility in California, where the company mines bastnaesite ore and processes it into rare earth concentrate, refined rare earth oxides (primarily neodymium-praseodymium or NdPr), and heavy rare earth elements (HREE). The Magnetics segment consists of downstream operations at the Independence Facility in Texas, which converts NdPr oxide into rare earth metal, alloys, and neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) permanent magnets. The business model has shifted from historically selling raw concentrate primarily to Chinese refiners to a vertically integrated strategy that produces separated oxides and finished magnets for domestic and international customers, including General Motors and Apple.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its unique position as the sole large-scale rare earth mining and processing site in North America, combined with an industry-leading cost structure and high environmental standards. This geographic exclusivity is reinforced by a "transformational" public-private partnership with the U.S. Department of War (DoW), formerly the Department of Defense. This partnership provides a significant structural advantage through a ten-year price floor protection agreement for NdPr products, a guaranteed minimum EBITDA of $140 million annually for the future 10X magnet facility, and a long-term offtake agreement where the DoW has the right to purchase all magnets produced at that facility. Additionally, the company has secured long-term supply agreements with major commercial anchors like Apple and General Motors, which include substantial prepayments, further de-risking the downstream magnet manufacturing business.

Management’s stated priorities focus on restoring the full rare earth magnetics supply chain to the United States to maximize long-term stockholder returns while supporting national security. Key strategic initiatives include scaling domestic magnet production capacity to an estimated 10,000 metric tons per year by expanding the Independence Facility and constructing a second facility (the 10X Facility). The company is also prioritizing the extension of HREE refining capabilities at Mountain Pass to produce samarium oxide and terbium/dysprosium, as well as developing commercial-scale recycling capabilities for magnet scrap. A critical strategic shift occurred in July 2025 when the company ceased all product sales to China to align with its domestic supply chain objectives and DoW agreements, pivoting its revenue focus toward separated products sold to Asian markets (Japan, South Korea) and domestic magnet customers.

The filing flags several material risks, most notably the immediate financial impact of ceasing shipments to China, which resulted in a 21% year-over-year revenue decline in the Materials segment and a material negative impact on cash flows and liquidity in the short term. The company is currently stockpiling unsold concentrate, which ties up capital. Additionally, the company faces execution risks associated with rapidly scaling its downstream magnet manufacturing, as the Magnetics segment only began generating substantial revenue in 2025 and commenced permanent magnet manufacturing in December 2025. The business is also exposed to geopolitical volatility, evidenced by recent Chinese export controls and trade disputes, which have created market disruption and uncertainty regarding the permanence of supply chain shifts. Finally, the company relies heavily on the successful execution of its DoW partnership, including the construction of the 10X Facility and HREE expansion, which requires significant capital expenditure and operational precision to meet the guaranteed EBITDA thresholds and production milestones.
