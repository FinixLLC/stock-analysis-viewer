## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (CPRT_10-K_20260906.txt)

Copart is a leading global provider of online auctions and vehicle remarketing services, operating across the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Brazil, Canada, and several other international markets. The company’s core business model involves processing and selling vehicles, primarily total-loss or stolen vehicles sourced from insurance companies, which account for approximately 81% of the vehicles processed in fiscal 2025. Copart utilizes its proprietary Virtual Bidding Third Generation (VB3) platform to facilitate these sales, a two-step internet auction process that combines open preliminary bidding with real-time virtual auctions. This technology allows registered buyers from anywhere in the world to participate, significantly expanding the buyer pool and increasing competitive bidding. In most regions, including the U.S., Copart acts as an agent, earning revenue through transaction fees, storage, and delivery services. However, in the U.K., Germany, and Spain, the company also operates on a principal basis, purchasing vehicles outright for resale and selling dismantled parts, which contributes to its "vehicle sales" revenue stream.

The company’s competitive moat is built on its global virtual platform that eliminates the need for physical attendance at auctions, thereby reducing costs for buyers and increasing efficiency for the company. Copart cites its ability to maximize sales prices through global buyer access, its extensive network of local facilities for vehicle inspection and storage, and its specialized capability to respond rapidly to catastrophic weather events as key differentiators. The filing notes that the company has established a leading market position by integrating technology to enhance operating efficiency, such as web-based order processing and vehicle imaging, while maintaining a local presence that reduces towing and third-party storage expenses for sellers.

Management’s stated priorities focus on generating sustainable profits for stockholders while providing environmental and social benefits. The company emphasizes its role in extending the useful life of vehicles to reduce the carbon footprint associated with new manufacturing and in facilitating the recycling of parts and raw materials. Strategically, management prioritizes geographic expansion through the acquisition and development of new facilities in both existing and new regions to strengthen global coverage. Additionally, the company seeks to increase revenues and profitability by pursuing global, national, and regional seller agreements, expanding service offerings, and integrating new facilities into its operational procedures to drive cost efficiencies.

The filing identifies several material risks and factors affecting financial performance. Revenue is heavily dependent on total loss frequency, which is driven by the relationship between repair costs, used car values, and auction returns. Copart notes that while long-term trends suggest an increase in total loss frequency due to rising repair costs and vehicle complexity, near-term fluctuations in used car prices and repair costs can inversely impact growth rates. The company also faces risks related to commodity pricing, particularly the price of crushed car bodies, which affects the selling price of vehicles sold for scrap or dismantling. Furthermore, foreign currency exchange rates impact international auction participation and revenue, and the company’s results are subject to seasonality, accident frequency, and the mix of vehicles sold. The filing also highlights that the company cannot precisely predict the movements of these macroeconomic factors, which creates uncertainty in forecasting financial results.
