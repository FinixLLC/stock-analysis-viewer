## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. (NYSE: WELL) is a real estate investment trust (REIT) focused on seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care across the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company operates as an umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT), with its primary operations conducted through Welltower OP LLC. Its business model centers on acquiring, developing, and managing properties that cater to aging populations, including independent living, assisted living, memory care, and post-acute care facilities. Welltower also invests in outpatient medical buildings leased to healthcare providers.

The company's competitive moat is bolstered by its disciplined capital allocation strategy, data science platform, and the Welltower Business System, which together aim to enhance operational efficiency and drive long-term growth. Additionally, Welltower benefits from a diversified portfolio across property types, geographic locations, and partnerships, reducing reliance on any single segment or market.

Management's priorities include generating long-term compounding per share earnings growth for existing shareholders, investing in high-quality operators with staying power, and focusing on affluent micro-markets to ensure durable cash flow. The company emphasizes risk management through structured investments that protect against permanent capital loss while allowing for sustained growth.

Key risks identified in the filing include potential declines in occupancy rates due to demographic shifts or economic downturns, regulatory changes affecting healthcare reimbursement, and credit risks associated with tenants and operators. Additionally, the company faces interest rate sensitivity, as its debt obligations could increase costs in a rising rate environment.
