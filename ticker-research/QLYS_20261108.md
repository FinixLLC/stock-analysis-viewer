## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (QLYS_10-K_20260827.txt)

Qualys operates as a leading provider of a cloud-based platform that delivers integrated IT, security, and compliance solutions, primarily through a software-as-a-service (SaaS) model with renewable annual subscriptions. The company’s Enterprise TruRisk Platform enables organizations to identify and manage IT and operational technology assets across on-premises, cloud, container, and mobile environments, while simultaneously collecting security data, prioritizing vulnerabilities, quantifying cyber risk, and verifying remediation actions. By offering a unified view of security and compliance posture, Qualys addresses the fragmentation of disparate security tools, allowing customers to reduce total cost of ownership and gain real-time visibility without managing hardware. The company serves over 10,000 customers, including a majority of the Forbes Global 100, and generated $669.1 million in revenue in 2025, driven by both new customer acquisition and expansion from existing subscribers.

Qualys’s competitive moat is rooted in its integrated, multi-tenant cloud architecture and its extensive knowledge base of vulnerability signatures, which are updated in real-time. The platform’s design eliminates the need for customers to purchase or manage infrastructure, offering seamless scaling and easy global scanning capabilities. A key differentiator is the "TruRisk" scoring capability, which provides a quantitative metric of risk by combining severity, exploitability, asset criticality, and business context to help prioritize threats. Furthermore, the company’s core services, including asset tagging, big data correlation via Elasticsearch, and automated remediation workflows, create a sticky ecosystem where customers can expand their subscriptions to additional "Cloud Apps" (such as Cybersecurity Asset Management and Total Application Security) without switching vendors. The filing notes that the company maintains one of the largest knowledge bases of vulnerability signatures in the industry, reinforcing its technical depth.

Management’s stated priorities focus on continuing to invest in research and development to improve existing solutions and develop new capabilities, as well as expanding sales and marketing efforts to support new solutions and drive demand. The company explicitly expects to continue increasing absolute dollar amounts for R&D, sales and marketing, and general and administrative expenses to support business growth. Additionally, management emphasizes the importance of maintaining a globally distributed cloud platform to rapidly deliver new solutions and security updates, while also expanding shared cloud platform infrastructures and customer support teams. The strategic direction includes leveraging AI-driven workflows and policy-based response rules within solutions like Enterprise TruRisk Management to automate remediation and centralize cyber risk response.

The most material risks flagged in the filing include the potential adverse impact of the current macroeconomic environment, characterized by inflationary pressure, high interest rates, market volatility, and geopolitical conflicts, which could lead customers to reduce or eliminate IT security spending. The company also notes that new sales personnel may not be immediately productive, potentially causing sales and marketing expenses to rise without corresponding revenue increases. Furthermore, the filing highlights risks related to the timing and amount of deferred tax asset valuation allowance releases, which depend on sustained profitability in certain jurisdictions. While the provided excerpts do not contain a dedicated "Risk Factors" section, the MD&A explicitly warns that macroeconomic uncertainty and extended sales cycles could have a material adverse effect on the business, and that the company’s ability to generate incremental revenue from new hires and marketing programs is not guaranteed.
