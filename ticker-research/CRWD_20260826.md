## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (CRWD_10-K_20260826.txt)

CrowdStrike is a cybersecurity company that specializes in providing cloud-native, AI-driven solutions to protect organizations against cyber threats. Founded in 2011, the company developed the Falcon platform, an AI-native operating system for cybersecurity designed to detect and prevent breaches in real time. The platform uses a single lightweight sensor to collect data across endpoints, cloud workloads, identities, and third-party sources, which is then analyzed using advanced graph technologies (Threat Graph, Intel Graph, and Asset Graph) to provide actionable insights. This approach allows CrowdStrike to offer a unified solution that consolidates multiple security functions, reducing complexity and costs for customers.

The company's competitive moat lies in its cloud-native architecture and AI-driven Security Cloud, which enables it to process vast amounts of high-fidelity data to continuously improve its threat detection and response capabilities. The more data fed into the platform, the more intelligent it becomes, creating a network effect that enhances its value over time. This scalability and adaptability differentiate CrowdStrike from legacy cybersecurity products, which often struggle with siloed data, limited integration, and slower response times.

Management's priorities include expanding the customer base through a land-and-expand sales strategy, enhancing the Falcon platform with new cloud modules, and investing in research and development to maintain technological leadership. The company also emphasizes customer retention by offering flexible subscription models and professional services that drive upselling opportunities.

Key risks identified in the filings include the July 19, 2024 incident, which caused system crashes for certain Windows systems and led to legal claims and reputational damage. This incident has impacted sales cycles, customer renewals, and revenue growth, with ongoing costs associated with resolving related issues. Additionally, CrowdStrike faces challenges in customer adoption of cloud-based solutions, competition from legacy vendors, and the need to maintain high retention rates as it expands its offerings. The company must also navigate elongated sales cycles and potential discounts or flexible terms offered post-incident, which could affect future financial performance.
