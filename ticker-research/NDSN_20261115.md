## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (NDSN_10-K_20260827.txt)

Nordson Corporation is a diversified precision technology company that engineers, manufactures, and markets differentiated products and systems for the precision dispensing, application, and control of adhesives, coatings, polymers, sealants, and biomaterials, as well as for testing, inspection, and surface treatment. The company serves a broad range of end markets, including consumer non-durables, consumer durables, and technology sectors such as packaging, electronics, medical, and transportation. Nordson’s business model relies on a direct global sales and service network operating in more than 35 countries, with approximately 67% of its 2025 revenues generated outside the United States. The company drives growth through organic innovation, high levels of customer service, and strategic acquisitions of companies with differentiated precision technology portfolios that complement existing capabilities and diversify into attractive high-growth end markets.

Nordson’s competitive moat is derived from a combination of intellectual property and human capital. While the company maintains a substantial portfolio of over 2,100 granted and pending patents and more than 1,000 trademarks, management explicitly states that the competitive advantage is largely attributable to the technical, marketing, and sales competence of its employees rather than any single patent or trademark. This is supported by a culture of innovation, a "Nordson Business System" (NBS) that uses data-based segmentation to identify profitable growth opportunities, and a division-led organizational structure that fosters an "Owner Mindset." The company also leverages its ability to integrate proprietary technologies into customer production processes, offering solutions that increase uptime, enable faster line speeds, and reduce material consumption.

Management’s stated priorities are centered on the "Ascend" strategy, launched in 2021, which aims to deliver top-tier revenue growth with leading margins and returns. This strategy is built on three pillars: the NBS Next growth framework, the Owner Mindset organizational structure, and the Winning Teams talent strategy. A key priority is providing genuine customer satisfaction, which management identifies as the foundation of the business. Additionally, the company emphasizes long-term growth and returns over short-term quarterly fluctuations, stating that it does not intend to alter foundational objectives to mitigate temporary impacts. The company also prioritizes employee self-fulfillment, equitable compensation, and community engagement, having donated over $183 million to communities since 1989.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to macroeconomic conditions and supply chain dynamics. Nordson operates in a competitive global marketplace affected by industry capacity changes, global competition, and economic conditions in the United States and abroad. The company notes that its costs and product availability can be affected by changes in raw material market prices, tariffs on imports (particularly from China), and disruptions in the supply of components and finished goods. While the company reports stabilization in the global supply chain and improved lead times, it continues to monitor risks related to geopolitical tensions and volatile shipping rates, particularly for Asia-origin shipments. Additionally, the company faces risks associated with intellectual property protection and enforcement, as well as the complexities of estimating fair values for business combinations and goodwill impairment, which require significant management judgment and assumptions regarding future cash flows, discount rates, and market conditions.
