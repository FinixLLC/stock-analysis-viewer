## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (IDXX_10-K_20260906.txt)

IDEXX Laboratories operates through three distinct business segments: the Companion Animal Group (CAG), which provides diagnostic and information management solutions for the veterinary industry; Water, which offers water quality testing products; and Livestock, Poultry, and Dairy (LPD), which supplies diagnostic products for animal health and food safety. The company’s core business model is anchored in a "razor-and-blade" dynamic within the CAG segment, where it places capital diagnostic instruments (such as the IDEXX VetLab suite) that generate non-recurring revenue, while simultaneously driving high-margin, recurring revenue from associated consumables, reference laboratory services, and subscription-based software. For the year ended December 31, 2025, recurring diagnostic revenue accounted for approximately 79% of consolidated revenue, providing a durable and profitable income stream that is significantly more valuable than instrument sales alone.

The company’s competitive moat is built on high customer retention and loyalty, driven by the integration of its hardware, software, and laboratory services into veterinary practice workflows. Management cites differentiation based on time-to-result, ease-of-use, breadth of the diagnostic menu, and the ability to provide a single view of patient diagnostic results through integrated platforms like IVLS and VetConnect PLUS. In the Water segment, the moat is reinforced by regulatory approvals required for compliance testing, which creates barriers to entry for competitors. Additionally, the company leverages its global scale in reference laboratories to achieve operational efficiencies and price increases, while its software offerings benefit from a large installed base that enables syndicated data offerings and sticky SaaS subscriptions.

Management’s stated priorities focus on growing volumes at existing customers by increasing utilization of existing and new test offerings, acquiring new customers, and realizing price increases based on differentiated products. The company emphasizes expanding the installed base of premium instruments to drive consumable sales and is actively pursuing international growth, particularly in the Water and LPD segments. Strategic initiatives include enhancing real-time care testing workflows, integrating AI-powered tools into diagnostic imaging and software, and expanding the menu of available tests to capture higher utilization per patient sample.

The filing flags several material risks, including exposure to macroeconomic and sector headwinds that constrain volume growth, particularly in the CAG segment. The company notes that disease outbreaks in the LPD segment are episodic and unpredictable, leading to volatile demand that can spike during outbreaks or drop significantly following successful eradication programs. Additionally, the business faces risks related to inflationary cost effects on product and labor costs, which can pressure gross margins if not offset by pricing benefits. The filing also highlights the operational risk that new or acquired reference laboratories may operate at a loss until they achieve sufficient scale, potentially negatively impacting short-term operating margins.
