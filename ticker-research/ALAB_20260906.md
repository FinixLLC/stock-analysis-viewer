## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ALAB_10-K_20260906.txt)

Astera Labs (ALAB) is a semiconductor company specializing in connectivity solutions for cloud and AI infrastructure. The company's business model revolves around its Intelligent Connectivity Platform, which integrates hardware and software to address data, network, and memory bottlenecks in high-performance systems. This platform includes products such as ICs, boards, and modules built on industry-standard protocols like PCIe, Ethernet, and CXL, along with COSMOS, a software suite that enhances configuration, management, and optimization of connectivity solutions.

Competitive moat: ALAB's competitive advantage lies in its differentiated platform, which combines hardware and software to provide tailored solutions for hyperscalers and system OEMs. The company's ability to offer a complete, interoperable solution with COSMOS software is a key differentiator. Additionally, its early adoption of cloud-based R&D allows for faster innovation and product development compared to competitors.

Management priorities: Management emphasizes maintaining strong relationships with hyperscalers and AI accelerator vendors, driving product innovation through significant investments in R&D, and expanding the company's market presence. The focus on customer-specific solutions and continuous platform enhancement underscores the commitment to meeting unique cloud scale requirements.

Risks: The 10-K highlights several material risks, including dependence on a small number of large customers (top three end customers accounted for ~86% of revenue in 2025), supply chain vulnerabilities due to reliance on third-party manufacturers, and intense competition from established players. Additionally, the company faces risks related to intellectual property protection and market adoption of its products.
