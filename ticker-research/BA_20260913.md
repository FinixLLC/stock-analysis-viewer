The company is also developing new models like the 777X and derivatives of the 737. BDS specializes in military aircraft, weapons systems, satellite technology, and strategic defense solutions. BGS provides maintenance, engineering, logistics, and training services to both commercial and defense customers.

Additionally, the company faces challenges from fixed-price development programs in the defense sector and ongoing efforts to recover from past production quality issues.
