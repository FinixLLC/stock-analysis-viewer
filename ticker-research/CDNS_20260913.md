## Business Profile

Cadence is a global leader in developing computational, AI-driven software, accelerated hardware, and silicon intellectual property (IP) products and solutions. The company serves customers in the semiconductor and systems industries, empowering them to design and verify innovative electronic products efficiently. Cadence's mission is to enable customers to bring groundbreaking electronic products to market, driving economic growth and improving daily life.

The company operates through a strategy called Intelligent System Design (ISD), which focuses on three pillars: Design Excellence, System Innovation, and Pervasive Intelligence. This approach allows Cadence to address the increasing complexity of chip and system designs by integrating advanced technologies like agentic AI, generative AI, machine learning, and digital twin algorithms into its products.

Cadence's competitive moat is built on its deep expertise in computational software, hardware acceleration, and IP solutions, which are critical for optimizing performance, power, and area (PPA) of semiconductors. The company's ability to leverage AI across infrastructure, physical systems, and life sciences provides a significant advantage in addressing customer needs.

Management priorities include expanding into new verticals such as life sciences, enhancing sustainability through energy-efficient designs, and investing heavily in research and development to maintain technological leadership. Cadence also focuses on strategic acquisitions to strengthen its product portfolio, such as the acquisition of VLAB Works for automotive software capabilities and Secure-IC for embedded security solutions.

Key risks identified by the company include macroeconomic factors like inflation and geopolitical tensions, which could impact global markets and trade. Additionally, regulatory changes, particularly export controls affecting operations in China, pose significant challenges. The company is actively monitoring these developments to mitigate potential impacts on its business.
