## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (PCAR_10-K_20260827.txt)

PACCAR is a global technology company specializing in the design, manufacture, and distribution of high-quality light-, medium-, and heavy-duty commercial trucks under well-known brands such as Kenworth, Peterbilt, and DAF. The company operates across multiple regions, including North America, Europe, South America, Australia, and Mexico. In addition to its Truck segment, PACCAR also runs a Parts segment that distributes aftermarket parts for trucks and related vehicles, and a Financial Services segment that provides financing and leasing solutions for its products in various markets.

The competitive moat of PACCAR is bolstered by its strong brand presence, extensive distribution network, and robust financial services offerings. The company's ability to produce trucks locally in key markets like the U.S., Canada, and Mexico helps mitigate the impact of import tariffs, which have been a significant challenge. This localization strategy also enhances supply chain efficiency and reduces exposure to trade-related disruptions.

Management has emphasized several priorities: investing in advanced technologies such as clean diesel engines, alternative powertrains, connected vehicle services, and autonomous driving systems; embedding artificial intelligence across operations for innovation and performance enhancement; and maintaining a strong balance sheet to support capital investments and research and development. The company is also focused on expanding its financial services capabilities to increase market share and support growth.

Key risks highlighted in the filing include the adverse effects of import tariffs on truck order intake and profit margins, potential changes in trade policies, evolving emissions regulations, and economic conditions that could impact freight demand and credit performance. Additionally, the company faces competitive pressures in pricing and market share across its segments, particularly in light of increased regulatory requirements and shifting consumer preferences for alternative powertrains.
