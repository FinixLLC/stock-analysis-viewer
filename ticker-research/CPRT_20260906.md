## Business Profile

Copart, Inc. is a leading global provider of online auctions and vehicle remarketing services, operating across multiple countries including the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Brazil, Canada, the United Arab Emirates, Spain, Finland, Oman, the Republic of Ireland, and Bahrain. The company specializes in facilitating the sale of vehicles primarily through its proprietary VB3 virtual bidding platform, which enables internet-based auctions accessible to registered buyers worldwide. Its primary clients are insurance companies, though it also serves dealers, individuals, charities, rental car companies, banks, finance companies, and fleet operators.

Copart's business model involves acting as an agent in most regions, earning revenue from auction and related fees, while in the U.K., Germany, and Spain, it sometimes operates as a principal, purchasing vehicles outright for resale. The company emphasizes environmental stewardship by extending vehicle lifecycles, reducing the need for new manufacturing, and supporting recycling efforts. It also contributes to community development by providing affordable transportation solutions globally and responding to natural disasters, such as hurricanes in South Florida.

Management priorities include expanding operations through facility openings and acquisitions, enhancing service offerings, and leveraging technology like VB3 to increase auction participation and efficiency. The company has opened numerous facilities across the U.S. and internationally over recent years to strengthen its global coverage.

Key risks flagged in filings include market volatility affecting vehicle prices, changes in total loss frequency influenced by repair costs and used car values, foreign exchange fluctuations impacting international sales, and macroeconomic factors influencing demand for vehicles and parts. Additionally, reliance on insurance companies as primary sellers introduces dependency on their salvage decisions, which can vary with economic conditions and industry trends.

Copart's financial performance is driven by service revenues from auction fees and vehicle sales, with operating costs primarily related to facility operations, transportation, and labor. The company has demonstrated consistent growth in both service revenues and vehicle sales over recent years, reflecting its strategic expansion and market penetration efforts.
