## Business Profile

Comfort Systems USA, Inc. (FIX) is a national provider of mechanical and electrical contracting services, operating primarily in the commercial, industrial, and institutional sectors. The company’s business model is bifurcated into two segments: mechanical services, which account for 73.3% of revenue and include HVAC, plumbing, piping, and controls, and electrical services, which comprise the remaining 26.7%. FIX generates approximately 63.2% of its revenue from installation services in newly constructed facilities and 36.8% from renovation, expansion, maintenance, repair, and replacement services in existing buildings. The company operates through 50 operating units across 190 locations in 142 U.S. cities, serving a diverse customer base that is heavily weighted toward the technology sector (45.0% of revenue) and manufacturing (22.1%). FIX typically competes on a local or regional basis, with projects generally completed within six to nine months and an average contract price of approximately $2.9 million.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its scale, financial strength, and operational capabilities rather than proprietary technology or exclusive market access. FIX cites its national presence, which allows for purchasing economies and the ability to leverage resources across locations, as key advantages. The company emphasizes its strong balance sheet, evidenced by positive free cash flow in each of the last 27 calendar years and a credit facility with $921.0 million available as of December 31, 2025, which supports its bonding needs and competitive positioning. Additionally, FIX highlights its investments in design and building information modeling, prefabrication, and modular/off-site construction capabilities as differentiators that improve productivity and quality. The company also notes that its diverse customer and geographic base reduces exposure to negative developments in any single sector or region.

Management’s stated priorities focus on improving profitability and generating growth through several strategic initiatives. Key objectives include achieving excellence in core competencies such as safety, customer service, and design/build expertise; attracting and retaining skilled labor through enhanced career paths and training; and achieving operating efficiencies through centralized purchasing and best practices. FIX is actively pursuing growth through acquisitions of complementary businesses with strong workforces and safety records. Furthermore, management is emphasizing a "Service Growth Initiative" to expand maintenance and repair revenue, which currently represents approximately 7.3% of total revenue, by investing in managerial and sales resources. The company also prioritizes the adoption of leading technologies, including prefabrication and modular construction, to maintain a leading position in design and modeling excellence.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to the fixed-price nature of much of its project work. If initial cost estimates are inaccurate or cost overruns occur that cannot be recovered through change orders, FIX may experience reduced profits or significant losses. The company also identifies labor scarcity and rising labor costs as significant challenges, noting that skilled labor in the building trades has become increasingly scarce and valuable. Supply chain risks, including delays in the delivery of materials and equipment and persistent cost pressures, are also highlighted as ongoing concerns. Additionally, FIX notes that it operates in a highly competitive environment with low barriers to entry, where price for value is the most influential factor for customer selection. The company also faces risks related to economic uncertainty, which can cause significant slowdowns in spending decisions for building construction and renovation, particularly in the manufacturing and technology sectors that drive much of its demand.
