## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (RVMD_10-K_20260827.txt)

RVMD is a clinical-stage precision oncology company focused on developing novel targeted therapies for RAS-addicted cancers. The company leverages advanced structure-based drug discovery capabilities and proprietary technologies to create small molecules targeting unconventional binding sites in RAS proteins, which are common drivers of various cancers. RVMD's pipeline includes multiple RAS(ON) Inhibitors designed as monotherapies or combination regimens, such as daraxonrasib (a multi-selective inhibitor), zoldonrasib (G12D-selective), elironrasib (G12C-selective), and others targeting different RAS mutations. The company has received Breakthrough Therapy Designations for several of its candidates across cancers like pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) and non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). RVMD collaborates with other biopharma companies to explore combination therapies, enhancing its approach to overcoming drug resistance and improving patient outcomes.

RVMD's competitive moat lies in its deep expertise in RAS biology, innovative technologies enabling the creation of selective inhibitors, and a broad pipeline targeting multiple RAS mutations. The company's ability to address unmet needs in cancers with high RAS mutation prevalence provides significant market potential. Additionally, strategic collaborations enhance RVMD's capacity to explore combination therapies, further differentiating its approach.

Management prioritizes advancing its deep pipeline of RAS(ON) Inhibitors through clinical trials, pursuing regulatory approvals, and exploring combination therapies to maximize therapeutic impact. The company also focuses on building partnerships to expand its reach and optimize treatment regimens for patients with RAS-addicted cancers.

Key risks include the lengthy and uncertain regulatory approval process, potential delays in clinical trials due to recruitment challenges or adverse events, competition from other biopharma companies developing similar therapies, and reliance on third-party manufacturers and collaborators. Additionally, RVMD faces the risk of not securing adequate funding for its operations and clinical development programs.
