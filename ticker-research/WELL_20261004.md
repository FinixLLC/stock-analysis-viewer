## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. is a real estate investment trust (REIT) and S&P 500 company that positions itself at the center of the "silver economy" by owning and investing in rental housing for aging seniors across the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company operates through an umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT) structure, with Welltower Inc. holding approximately 98.4% of Welltower OP LLC, which conducts the day-to-day business. Welltower’s portfolio consists of over 2,500 communities, predominantly focused on seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care. The company’s business model is diversified across three reportable segments: Seniors Housing Operating (78% of 2025 revenues), Triple-net (11% of 2025 revenues), and Outpatient Medical (7% of 2025 revenues). In the Seniors Housing Operating segment, Welltower acts as a landlord and asset manager, relying on external partners for property management under incentive-based contracts; major partners include Care UK, Cogir Management Company, and Sunrise Senior Living. The Triple-net segment involves leasing properties to operators under long-term master leases (typically 10 to 20 years) where the tenant bears operating costs, with a significant portion of this revenue derived from a relationship with Integra Healthcare Properties. The Outpatient Medical segment consists primarily of triple-net leased buildings affiliated with health systems. Beyond real estate ownership, Welltower generates income from real estate loans, which totaled approximately $2.08 billion as of December 31, 2025, yielding approximately 8.9% per annum, and holds investments in unconsolidated entities valued at approximately $1.81 billion.

The company’s competitive moat appears to be rooted in its scale, geographic diversification, and a proprietary operational framework. Welltower cites a "disciplined approach to capital allocation powered by our Data Science platform" and a "Welltower Business System" as drivers of superior operating results. The company emphasizes its ability to invest in "affluent micro-markets" benefiting from secular demand for senior housing, aiming to generate durable cash flow growth. Additionally, the structure of its Triple-net leases provides a defensive advantage; specifically, the use of "master leases" bundles multiple properties under a single tenant agreement, preventing tenants from cherry-picking high-performing assets during bankruptcy or renewal processes, thereby spreading risk across the entire leased portfolio.

Management’s stated priorities are explicitly focused on the "long-term compounding of per share growth" for existing investors. To achieve this, the company seeks to allocate capital with a "singular focus" on generating long-term earnings growth, partnering with high-quality operators who demonstrate staying power across economic cycles, and investing at a "compelling basis" that provides a meaningful margin of safety. The filing highlights a strategy of protecting downside risk and avoiding permanent capital loss while pursuing sustained long-term growth. Furthermore, management emphasizes the use of its Data Science platform to drive disciplined capital allocation and superior operating results.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to operational dependency and credit exposure. In the Seniors Housing Operating segment, Welltower explicitly states it "relies on our partners to manage these properties effectively and efficiently," creating a dependency on third-party operators for performance. In the Triple-net segment, the company notes that certain leases contain purchase options that could result in the disposition of properties for less than full market value if exercised. Regarding its loan portfolio, the filing details the risk of credit losses, noting that loans are evaluated for collectability based on borrower financial strength and collateral value, and that loans with deteriorated credit quality may be placed on non-accrual status. The company recognized a provision for loan losses of $(9.4) million in 2025, indicating active management of credit risk within its $2.08 billion loan portfolio. Additionally, the company faces construction risk, with outstanding construction investments of approximately $739 million and committed additional funds of approximately $493 million as of year-end 2025.
