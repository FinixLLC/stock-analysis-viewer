TTD's competitive advantage lies in its focus on the buy side of the advertising market, ensuring independence and objectivity by avoiding conflicts of interest associated with serving both buyers and sellers. The company's platform is built on a data-driven foundation, leveraging AI to enhance decision-making and campaign optimization. **Management Priorities:**

The company prioritizes innovation in technology, particularly in AI-driven features like Koa, its AI co-pilot. TTD also focuses on expanding its reach into new markets, including CTV and international regions, while investing in platform enhancements to support programmatic advertising growth.

Additionally, TTD faces challenges in retaining and growing client spend, as well as managing costs associated with platform development and international expansion. Seasonal fluctuations in advertising activity further impact revenue stability.
