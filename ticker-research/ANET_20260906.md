## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ANET_10-K_20260906.txt)

Arista Networks (ANET) is a leading provider of cloud networking-as-a-service solutions, focusing on enabling customers to access and manage their data across various centers—AI, Data, Campus, and WAN—through a unified, open, and standards-based approach. The company's business model revolves around delivering high-speed, reliable, and secure networking solutions that integrate seamlessly from the client to the cloud. Its offerings include hardware platforms, software-defined networking tools, and services designed to optimize performance, reduce operational complexity, and enhance security for customers ranging from large cloud providers to enterprises.

Arista's competitive moat is built on its differentiated product portfolio, which leverages EOS (Extensible Operating System) and NetDL (Network Data Lake) to provide real-time telemetry, intelligent automation, and consistent network management. The company emphasizes open standards to avoid vendor lock-in, offering solutions that are scalable, efficient, and future-proof. Its focus on AI-driven networking and the ability to rapidly innovate in response to market demands further strengthens its position.

Management has prioritized expanding into new markets, particularly those driven by generative AI and hyperscale data centers, while also investing heavily in research and development to maintain technological leadership. The company is committed to enhancing customer support and broadening its channel partnerships to reach a wider audience.

Key risks flagged in the filing include reliance on a small number of large customers, which accounted for significant portions of revenue (26% and 16% from two major clients in 2025), making sales highly concentrated. Additionally, macroeconomic uncertainties, supply chain disruptions, and inflationary pressures pose challenges to gross margins and operational stability. The company also faces competitive pressures as new market entrants target AI-related infrastructure investments, potentially affecting demand for its products.
