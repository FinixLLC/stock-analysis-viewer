## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (IDXX_10-K_20260906.txt)

IDEXX Laboratories operates through three primary segments: the Companion Animal Group (CAG), which is the dominant revenue driver, Water, and Livestock, Poultry, and Dairy (LPD). The company’s core business model in CAG is built on a "razor and blade" strategy, selling point-of-care diagnostic instruments to veterinary practices in exchange for multi-year commitments to purchase high-margin recurring consumables, such as IDEXX VetLab reagents and SNAP rapid assay kits. This model is further reinforced by reference laboratory services, veterinary practice management software (including SaaS offerings like Cornerstone, ezyVet, and Animana), and diagnostic imaging systems. In 2025, recurring diagnostic revenue accounted for approximately 79% of consolidated revenue, providing a durable and highly profitable income stream that significantly outperforms the non-recurring instrument sales in terms of gross margins.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its integrated ecosystem, which combines hardware, software, and laboratory services to create high switching costs and deep customer loyalty. IDEXX differentiates its products through superior time-to-result, ease of use, and the breadth of its diagnostic menu, which allows veterinarians to track patient trends across a single platform. The company cites high customer retention rates and a growing installed base of premium instruments as key drivers of volume growth. Additionally, in the Water segment, IDEXX maintains a regulatory moat by securing necessary approvals for compliance testing in various countries, while in LPD, it relies on differentiated test performance for government disease control programs. The integration of AI-powered tools in imaging and practice management further enhances the value proposition, making it difficult for competitors to replicate the full workflow efficiency IDEXX provides.

Management’s stated priorities focus on growing volumes at existing customers by increasing the utilization of both existing and new test offerings, acquiring new customers, and maintaining high loyalty. A key strategic objective is to increase diagnostic testing within veterinary practices by placing more IDEXX VetLab instruments and driving higher utilization per instrument. The company also prioritizes expanding its SaaS installed base for practice management and diagnostic imaging, leveraging these platforms to offer syndicated data and enhanced client communication tools. Furthermore, management emphasizes operational productivity and pricing benefits to offset inflationary cost pressures, while continuing to invest in research and development for new products and services that enhance medical awareness and testing capabilities.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily centered on the episodic and unpredictable nature of demand in the LPD segment, where disease outbreaks can cause exceptional demand spikes, while successful eradication programs can lead to significantly decreased demand. In the CAG segment, the company notes that growth is partially constrained by macroeconomic and sector headwinds. Additionally, the profitability of new or acquired reference laboratories may be negatively impacted in the short term as they operate at a loss until testing volumes achieve sufficient scale, and acquired laboratories may take several years to reach the profitability of the existing network. The company also highlights the impact of foreign currency exchange rates on revenue and margins, as well as the need to continuously differentiate products to justify premium pricing in a competitive market.
