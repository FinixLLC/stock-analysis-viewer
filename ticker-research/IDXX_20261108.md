## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (IDXX_10-K_20260906.txt)

IDEXX Laboratories operates through three primary segments: the Companion Animal Group (CAG), which provides diagnostic and information management solutions for the veterinary industry; Water, which offers water quality testing products; and Livestock, Poultry and Dairy (LPD), which supplies diagnostic products for animal health and milk quality. The company’s core business model relies heavily on a "razor and blade" strategy within the CAG segment, where it places capital diagnostic instruments (such as the IDEXX VetLab suite) that generate non-recurring revenue, while deriving significantly higher margins from the recurring sale of associated consumables, rapid assay kits, and reference laboratory services. For the year ended December 31, 2025, recurring diagnostic revenue accounted for approximately 79% of consolidated revenue, providing a durable and profitable revenue stream. The company also generates recurring revenue through subscription-based veterinary practice management software (SaaS) and diagnostic imaging systems, which are integrated with its diagnostic platforms to create a cohesive ecosystem for veterinary practices.

The company’s competitive moat is built on high customer retention, an expanding installed base of premium instruments, and the integration of its diagnostic hardware with its software and reference laboratory services. Management notes that long-term success depends on growing volumes at existing customers through increased utilization and new test offerings, acquiring new customers, and maintaining high loyalty. The company differentiates its products based on time-to-result, ease-of-use, breadth of the diagnostic menu, and the ability to provide a single view of patient diagnostic results across point-of-care and reference laboratory modalities. Additionally, in the Water segment, the moat is supported by regulatory approvals required for compliance testing, which creates barriers to entry for competitors. In the CAG segment, the company leverages its large practice management installed base to access veterinary channel transaction activity and offers syndicated data, further deepening customer engagement.

Management’s stated priorities focus on increasing diagnostic testing within veterinary practices by placing new instruments, such as the IDEXX inVue Dx Analyzer, and increasing the utilization of existing instruments through both higher patient sample volumes and more tests per sample. The company aims to educate veterinarians on best medical practices to drive testing demand and introduces new testing capabilities to expand the menu of available tests. A key strategic objective is to realize price increases based on differentiated products and the growing value of the diagnostic offering. In the software and imaging space, management prioritizes enhancing the customer experience through cloud technology, AI-powered tools, and integrated client communication features. For the Water and LPD segments, priorities include increasing international sales, broadening product lines, and securing regulatory approvals to differentiate products from competition.

The filing flags several material risks, including the impact of macroeconomic and sector headwinds, which have constrained volume growth in the CAG segment. The company notes that the expiration of patents and licenses, while not expected to have a material effect individually or in the aggregate, is a factor to monitor. In the Water segment, growth is dependent on the ability to increase international sales and obtain regulatory approvals, which can be a barrier to entry but also a risk if not secured. In the LPD segment, demand is episodic and unpredictable, driven by disease outbreaks and government funding levels; successful eradication programs can lead to significantly decreased demand for certain products. Additionally, the company faces risks related to higher product and labor costs due to inflationary effects, which can impact gross margins, particularly in the Water segment where product and distribution costs increased. The company also notes that acquired reference laboratories may operate at a loss or lower profitability for several years until operating improvements are implemented, potentially negatively affecting operating margins in the short term.
