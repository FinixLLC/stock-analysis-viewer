## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ORCL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Oracle operates as a provider of enterprise information technology frameworks, delivering products and services that build, run, and support IT infrastructure through a hybrid model of cloud-based, on-premise, and hybrid deployments. The company’s business model is anchored in three distinct segments: cloud and software, hardware, and services. The cloud and software segment, which accounted for 87% of total revenues in fiscal 2026, generates income through subscription-based cloud applications and infrastructure (OCI and OCA), perpetual software licenses, and recurring software support contracts. Oracle emphasizes flexibility and interoperability, allowing customers to choose deployment models that suit their specific needs, including multicloud options and the Exadata Cloud@Customer hybrid model. The company is actively transitioning its revenue mix toward cloud services, with cloud revenues rising from 37% of total revenues in fiscal 2024 to 51% in fiscal 2026, driven by the migration of existing on-premise customers to the Oracle Cloud and new subscriptions.

Oracle’s competitive moat is derived from its comprehensive, integrated portfolio of enterprise applications and infrastructure technologies, which management argues differentiates the company from competitors offering more limited or specialized solutions. The company leverages a deep understanding of how applications and infrastructure interact, using its own Oracle Cloud Infrastructure to power its cloud applications, creating a cohesive ecosystem. Key differentiators include the breadth of its cloud applications suite (spanning ERP, HCM, CX, and industry-specific solutions), the integration of AI-driven automation and analytics into these workflows, and the ability to offer flexible, interoperable deployment models that reduce customer risk and complexity. Additionally, Oracle benefits from a large installed base of on-premise customers who are increasingly migrating to the cloud, supported by high renewal rates for software support contracts and a global sales force combined with a partner network.

Management’s stated priorities center on expanding the Oracle Cloud business by facilitating the migration of the existing installed base from on-premise to cloud deployments and capturing new demand from small and medium-sized businesses and non-IT line-of-business purchasers. The company prioritizes the integration of artificial intelligence technologies, including generative AI and embedded AI-driven automation, to enhance customer workflows and productivity. Oracle also emphasizes maintaining choice and flexibility for customers regarding deployment models, viewing this as a critical element of its corporate strategy to address customer needs better than competitors. Significant investment in research and development, totaling $10.3 billion in fiscal 2026, is directed toward enhancing the existing portfolio, developing new technologies, and expanding the geographic footprint and capacity of its cloud operations.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to the execution of the cloud transition and macroeconomic headwinds. Key risks include the company’s ability to manage Oracle Cloud capacity requirements to meet existing and prospective customer demand, as well as the impact of general economic conditions, inflation, tariffs, and trade policies on both customer demand and Oracle’s cost structure. Management notes that margins may be adversely impacted by increases in supply chain and energy costs, the inability to pass tariff-related costs to customers, and foreign currency rate fluctuations. Additionally, the company faces risks associated with governmental budgetary constraints, customer satisfaction with offerings, and the competitive position of its products relative to other cloud infrastructure offerings. The filing also highlights the uncertainty in income tax provisions due to the complexity of global transactions and the potential for final tax outcomes to differ from estimates.
