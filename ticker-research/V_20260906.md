Visa operates as a global leader in digital payments, facilitating secure, reliable, and efficient money movement across over 200 countries and territories. The company's business model centers on transaction processing services, including authorization, clearing, and settlement, primarily through its proprietary VisaNet network. Additionally, Visa's open partnership approach allows clients to access its network through APIs and other integration methods, fostering innovation and ecosystem expansion.

Management emphasizes growth through consumer payments (CP), commercial and money movement solutions (CMS), and value-added services (VAS). Key priorities include expanding contactless payment adoption, enhancing token technology for security, and driving cross-border capabilities. Visa also focuses on financial inclusion by providing prepaid solutions to underserved populations.

The company faces several risks, including legal liabilities from interchange multidistrict litigation, which has led to significant accruals and potential impacts on earnings per share.
