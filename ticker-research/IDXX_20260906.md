## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (IDXX_10-K_20260906.txt)

IDEXX is a global leader in veterinary diagnostics, water quality testing, and livestock health solutions. The company operates through three main segments: Companion Animal Group (CAG), Water, and Livestock, Poultry, and Dairy (LPD). CAG focuses on diagnostic products and services for companion animals, including point-of-care instruments, consumables, reference laboratory services, and veterinary software. The Water segment provides testing solutions for water quality, primarily targeting microbial contamination in drinking water. LPD offers diagnostics for livestock and poultry health, as well as milk quality and producer efficiency.

IDEXX's competitive moat is built on its differentiated product portfolio, strong customer relationships, and recurring revenue streams. The company emphasizes high-margin recurring revenues from consumables, software subscriptions, and services, which accounted for approximately 79% of total revenue in 2025. Its point-of-care diagnostic instruments, such as the IDEXX VetLab suite, generate durable income through consumable sales, while its reference laboratories provide comprehensive testing with rapid turnaround times.

Management prioritizes innovation, customer engagement, and operational efficiency. The company invests in R&D to enhance product offerings, improve service quality, and expand into new markets. Strategic initiatives include expanding the installed base of premium diagnostic instruments, increasing instrument utilization, and leveraging data-driven solutions to support veterinary practices.

Key risks flagged in the filing include macroeconomic challenges impacting customer spending, sector-specific headwinds such as reduced pet visits, regulatory hurdles for water testing products, and the unpredictability of disease outbreaks affecting livestock demand. Additionally, the company faces competition from other diagnostic providers and must manage costs related to inflation and supply chain disruptions.
