## Business Profile

Alliant Energy is a regulated investor-owned public utility holding company headquartered in Madison, Wisconsin. The company operates through its subsidiaries IPL and WPL to provide electric and natural gas services to approximately 1,010,000 electric and 435,000 natural gas customers in the Midwest. Its business model includes generating and distributing electricity and natural gas, as well as engaging in non-utility ventures such as wind farms, energy storage projects, and supply chain solutions through its subsidiary AEF.

The company's competitive moat is rooted in its regulated utility operations, which provide stable cash flows due to long-term contracts and government-regulated rates. Additionally, Alliant Energy benefits from a diversified portfolio of renewable energy sources, including wind and solar, as well as investments in transmission infrastructure, which enhance its ability to serve customers reliably and efficiently.

Management's stated priorities include delivering affordable and reliable energy solutions, investing in customer-focused initiatives, and advancing environmental stewardship. The company emphasizes modernizing its infrastructure, expanding generation capacity with a mix of natural gas, renewable energy, and storage, and supporting economic development through partnerships with large industrial and commercial customers.

Key risks flagged in the filing include regulatory uncertainties, such as rate reviews and changes in tax policies, which can impact profitability and customer rates. Additionally, the company faces operational risks related to fuel costs, extreme weather events affecting its infrastructure, and the need to maintain compliance with safety and environmental regulations. The company also notes risks associated with economic growth and load demand from new customers, particularly data centers, which could require significant investments in generation and distribution capacity.
