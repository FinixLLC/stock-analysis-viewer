## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ALL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Allstate is a leading insurance provider offering products such as auto, homeowners, and other personal lines of insurance. The company operates through various channels, including exclusive agents, independent agents, contact centers, and online platforms. Its business model focuses on delivering affordable, simple, and connected protection solutions to customers.

The competitive moat for Allstate is built on its strong brand recognition, extensive distribution network, and ability to leverage data analytics for personalized customer experiences. The company's multi-year growth strategy emphasizes premium growth, particularly in international markets, as evidenced by the 7.5% increase in premiums written in 2025 compared to 2024.

Management has prioritized several key areas: expanding policies in force (PIF) across its Protection Plans segment, enhancing customer engagement through digital channels, and optimizing underwriting results. The company also focuses on managing catastrophe risks through reinsurance programs and improving operational efficiency to control costs.

The most material risks flagged in the filing include:
1. **Catastrophe Risk**: Allstate faces significant exposure to natural disasters, which can lead to substantial claims payouts and impact profitability.
2. **Reserve Estimation Uncertainty**: The company's financial results are sensitive to reserve reestimates, particularly in personal auto injury coverage, where favorable developments in 2025 contributed to reduced reserves but also introduced variability.
3. **Macroeconomic Factors**: Rising costs of vehicle repairs and building materials, as well as supply chain disruptions, increase claims severity and expenses.
4. **Regulatory and Legal Risks**: The company is involved in coverage disputes with reinsurers and faces potential credit risks from reinsurer financial instability.

Allstate's ability to navigate these risks while maintaining its competitive position will be crucial for sustaining growth and profitability.
