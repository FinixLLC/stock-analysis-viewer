## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ANET_10-K_20260906.txt)

Arista Networks operates as a provider of data-driven, client-to-cloud networking-as-a-service, positioning itself as an industry leader in high-speed Ethernet switching. The company’s business model centers on a "Centers of Data" strategy that unifies four primary domains—AI Centers, Data Centers, Campus Centers, and WAN Centers—through a single software platform anchored by its Extensible Operating System (EOS) and Network Data Lake (NetDL). This approach replaces legacy siloed networking with a unified architecture that emphasizes reliability, open standards to avoid vendor lock-in, and intelligent automation. Arista serves a global customer base segmented into Cloud and AI Titans, AI and Specialty Providers, and Enterprise sectors, with revenue derived approximately 48% from Cloud and AI Titans, 32% from Enterprise, and 20% from AI and Specialty Providers. The product portfolio spans Core networking (AI, Cloud, and Data Center), Cognitive Adjacencies (Campus and Routing), and Software and Services, including AI Ops tools like CloudVision and AVA.

The company’s competitive moat is built on a broad, differentiated portfolio of high-performance platforms using best-of-breed merchant silicon, combined with a rich software-driven networking-as-a-service model. Arista differentiates itself through a modular, software-driven architecture that allows for rapid evolution of offerings while maintaining structural integrity, distinct from competitors who may rely on proprietary or less flexible systems. Key differentiators include uncompromising reliability derived from robust quality assurance and automated diagnostics, consistent real-time telemetry, and an award-winning support system that has earned an industry-leading Net Promoter Score. The company also highlights its ability to partner closely with customers to address changing needs, leveraging a single operating system, data lake, and management solution to lower total network operations costs.

Management’s stated priorities focus on sustaining success in a market characterized by rapid technological evolution and the expansion of generative and agentic AI. To achieve this, Arista aims to increase sales in cloud, AI, and enterprise data center Ethernet switching/routing markets, as well as campus workspace markets, by leveraging its ability to rapidly develop new features and software applications. The growth strategy relies on maintaining agility and increasing investment in research and development to deliver market-leading features, expand product offerings, and build upon technology leadership. Additionally, management prioritizes expanding the global sales force and deepening channel partnerships to reach new customers and increase sales to existing ones. The company is also actively working to optimize its supply chain in response to evolving international trade policies and tariff uncertainties while maintaining a disciplined fulfillment cadence.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily centered on customer concentration and demand volatility. Two customers accounted for more than 10% of total revenue in each of the last three years, with one representing 26% and the other 16% of 2025 revenue. Arista notes unpredictability in the timing of orders from these high-volume customers due to the complexity of large-scale orders, fluctuations in demand, and shifts in capital expenditure budgets, particularly as customers prioritize AI infrastructure over other areas. This concentration leads to pricing discounts that can reduce gross margins and creates volatility in revenue recognition, especially given the increase in customer trials and contracts with acceptance periods in the new AI Ethernet market. Furthermore, the company faces macroeconomic risks, including inflation, supply chain disruptions, and tariff uncertainty, which could impact product costs and gross margins. The filing also highlights the risk of excess or obsolete inventory and supplier liability charges due to the volatility in inventory and purchase commitments resulting from new product introductions and shifts in customer demand.
