## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (IAU_10-K_20260906.txt)

The iShares Gold Trust (IAU) is a grantor trust designed to provide investors with exposure to the price of gold. The company's business model revolves around issuing shares that represent fractional ownership in its net assets, which are primarily composed of physical gold held by a custodian. Investors can buy and sell these shares on the NYSE Arca under the ticker symbol IAU. The trust does not engage in active management or speculative trading; instead, it aims to reflect the performance of gold prices before deducting expenses.

The competitive moat of IAU lies in its ability to offer a cost-efficient and accessible way for investors to gain exposure to gold without the complexities associated with holding physical bullion. By eliminating the need for direct ownership of gold, which can be logistically challenging and expensive, IAU provides a streamlined investment option that is easily tradable through traditional brokerage accounts.

Management's stated priorities include maintaining operational efficiency, ensuring transparency, and minimizing costs. The trust's sponsor, iShares Delaware Trust Sponsor LLC, assumes most administrative and marketing expenses, keeping the only recurring expense at 0.25% of the trust's net asset value. This fee structure is designed to keep costs low for investors.

The risks flagged in the filing include market risk associated with fluctuations in gold prices, liquidity risk due to potential discrepancies between trading hours on NYSE Arca and major global gold markets, and operational risks such as custody and insurance issues. Additionally, the trust's reliance on the LBMA Gold Price for valuation introduces dependency on external market mechanisms that could impact pricing accuracy.
