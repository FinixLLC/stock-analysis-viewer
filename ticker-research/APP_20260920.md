## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (APP_10-K_20260906.txt)

AppLovin operates as a provider of end-to-end, artificial intelligence-powered advertising solutions, functioning as a critical infrastructure layer within the global advertising ecosystem. The company’s business model is performance-based, generating revenue primarily when advertisers achieve their return on advertising spend (ROAS) targets. This creates a direct alignment between AppLovin’s growth and its clients' success. The core of this model is the Axon AI recommendation engine, which powers the company’s primary revenue driver, Axon Ads Manager, a user acquisition tool that matches advertiser demand with publisher supply through microsecond-level auctions. Complementary products include MAX, an in-app bidding solution that optimizes publisher inventory value through real-time competitive auctions; Adjust, a measurement and analytics platform for attribution and fraud prevention; and Wurl, a connected TV (CTV) platform for streaming distribution and monetization. Following the sale of its Apps business in June 2025, AppLovin now operates as a single reportable segment focused exclusively on these advertising technologies.

The company asserts a durable competitive advantage derived from its scaled business model, which creates a data flywheel. As more advertisers and publishers utilize the platform, AppLovin gains access to more data regarding user engagement, which strengthens its scaled distribution. This expanded distribution provides better insights to the Axon AI engine, which in turn enhances the efficiency and effectiveness of the advertising solutions, further attracting clients. Management emphasizes that this intricate link to the advertising ecosystem, combined with the ability to operate at vast scale and microsecond speeds, provides a moat against competitors. Additionally, the company cites its unique position in the ecosystem, the speed of its technological development, and the flexibility of its solutions as critical contributors to its success, noting that it competes favorably against large established entities like Meta, Google, and Amazon based on technology, expertise, and relationships.

Management’s stated priorities center on expanding the company’s reach beyond its traditional mobile gaming base into new verticals, specifically web-based e-commerce and the CTV industry. The company is actively applying its Axon AI technology to these new sectors, with early results from web-based advertisers described as positive. A key strategic focus is the continuous enhancement of AI-based technologies to improve efficacy and drive growth. Management also prioritizes attracting and retaining top engineering talent, noting that approximately 42% of the total headcount is dedicated to research and development. Furthermore, the company intends to pursue strategic transactions, including acquisitions and partnerships, to accelerate growth and expand into new markets.

The filing flags several material risks, most notably the company’s reliance on third-party platforms such as the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. These platforms hold significant market power and discretion over policies, fees, and data access, and any adverse changes to their policies could rapidly impact AppLovin’s ability to target users and allocate campaigns efficiently. The company also highlights the evolving legal and regulatory landscape regarding data protection, privacy, and AI as a key risk requiring continuous innovation to mitigate. Additionally, the business is subject to seasonality driven by fluctuations in advertising demand associated with mobile gaming and e-commerce cycles, which may become more pronounced as the advertiser base expands. Finally, the company notes that its success depends on protecting its intellectual property while operating without infringing on third-party rights, acknowledging that legal protections alone may not guarantee competitive advantage.
