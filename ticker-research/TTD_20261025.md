## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (TTD_10-K_20260906.txt)

The Trade Desk (TTD) operates as a global leader in advertising technology, specifically providing a self-service platform that empowers ad buyers—including advertising agencies, advertisers, and service providers—to create, manage, and optimize digital advertising campaigns across various formats, channels, and devices. The company’s business model is built on independence and objectivity, focusing exclusively on the buy side of the digital advertising ecosystem to avoid the conflicts of interest associated with competitors who also sell inventory. TTD generates revenue primarily by charging clients a platform fee, generally calculated as a percentage of the client’s total spend on the platform, as well as through value-added services and data offerings. Clients enter into ongoing master services agreements (MSAs) rather than episodic insertion orders, a structure that supports a highly scalable model and has resulted in a customer retention rate exceeding 95% for over a decade. The platform leverages artificial intelligence capabilities, such as the "Koa" AI co-pilot, and a rich ecosystem of inventory, publisher, and data partner integrations to enable precise targeting and campaign optimization.

TTD’s competitive moat is derived from its independent, buy-side focus, which fosters long-term trust and allows clients to ingest proprietary first-party data without fear of conflict of interest. This independence is paired with a data-driven approach, where the company built a data-management platform before its ad-buying technology, creating a "flywheel" effect where data usage enhances targeting and measurement capabilities. The company emphasizes transparency, offering granular, real-time reporting and channel-agnostic access to premium omnichannel inventory, including connected television (CTV), audio, and display. Additionally, TTD maintains an open platform architecture with enterprise APIs that allow clients to customize functionality and integrate third-party tools, further deepening client reliance on the platform. The company’s technology is underpinned by a scalable architecture, predictive models, and real-time optimization engines that analyze millions of data points to shift bids and budgets dynamically.

Management’s stated priorities center on increasing the share of existing clients’ digital advertising spend, particularly as budgets shift toward programmatic channels, and growing the client base by expanding into the mid-market and international regions such as Europe and Asia. The company is actively investing in the development and incorporation of AI into its platform, including the recent "Kokai" upgrade and the "Audience Unlimited" feature, to drive automation and simplification of data buying. TTD also prioritizes the expansion of its omnichannel reach, specifically in CTV and other video formats, and the integration of high-quality, privacy-compliant data, including retail and first-party data, to enhance measurement and targeting. The company anticipates that operating expenses will continue to rise as it invests in platform operations, AI development, and sales and marketing teams to support these growth initiatives, even if this negatively impacts near-term profitability.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily centered on the company’s dependence on the growth of the programmatic advertising market and its ability to retain and grow spend from existing clients in a highly competitive environment. TTD notes that its future revenue growth depends on differentiating its platform’s value against competitors who may offer artificially low prices enabled by conflicts of interest. The company also highlights the risks associated with macroeconomic uncertainty, including changes in interest rates, inflation, and geopolitical developments, which could decrease demand for advertising and disrupt supply chains. Additionally, TTD identifies the rising costs of hosting infrastructure components and the need to manage the increased costs associated with developing and hosting its AI-rich platform as significant challenges. The filing also points to the importance of successfully expanding into international markets and the potential for seasonal fluctuations in revenue, with the fourth quarter historically being the highest for advertising activity.
