## Business Profile

Copart, Inc., incorporated in California in 1982 and reincorporated in Delaware in 2012, is a leading global provider of online auctions and vehicle remarketing services. The company operates across multiple countries, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Brazil, Canada, the United Arab Emirates, Spain, Finland, Oman, the Republic of Ireland, and Bahrain. Its primary business involves facilitating the sale of vehicles, particularly those deemed total losses by insurance companies, through its proprietary VB3 auction platform. This platform enables a two-step bidding process, enhancing competition and efficiency while eliminating the need for physical auctions.

Copart's competitive moat is bolstered by its extensive global network, advanced technology, and focus on environmental stewardship. The company emphasizes sustainability by extending vehicle lifecycles, reducing the need for new manufacturing, and promoting recycling. Additionally, Copart plays a crucial role in disaster recovery, as evidenced by its response to hurricanes in South Florida.

Management's priorities include expanding operations through facility openings and acquisitions, enhancing service offerings, and leveraging technology to improve efficiency. The company has opened numerous facilities in recent years, reflecting its commitment to global coverage and market penetration.
