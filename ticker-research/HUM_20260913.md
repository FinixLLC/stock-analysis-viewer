## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (HUM_10-K_20260906.txt)

Humana Inc. is a healthcare services company headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky, offering insurance products and health care services through its two main segments: Insurance and CenterWell. The Insurance segment provides Medicare Advantage plans, stand-alone prescription drug plans (PDPs), and state-based Medicaid contracts, while the CenterWell segment focuses on enhancing member health outcomes through pharmacy solutions, primary care, and home care services. Humana's business model emphasizes an integrated care delivery system that combines quality care, member engagement, and data analytics to improve health outcomes and affordability.

Humana's competitive moat is built on its extensive network of healthcare providers, long-standing presence in the Medicare market, and innovative care delivery models. The company has a geographically diverse membership base, particularly in Medicare Advantage plans, which are renewed annually with CMS. Its risk-adjustment model allows it to receive higher payments for sicker members, enhancing profitability.

Management's priorities include driving value creation through cost-saving initiatives, transforming the business to align with market trends, and exiting unprofitable segments like the Employer Group Commercial Medical Products business. The company also focuses on integrating care delivery models to improve member outcomes and reduce costs.

Key risks flagged in the filing include regulatory changes impacting Medicare Advantage profitability, such as adjustments to payment rates or benefit designs, which could affect revenue growth. Additionally, Humana faces risks from macroeconomic pressures, competitive dynamics, and potential impairments of intangible assets. The company is also exposed to operational challenges related to its integrated care models and reliance on provider networks for accurate diagnosis coding under the risk-adjustment model.
