## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. is a real estate investment trust (REIT) and S&P 500 company that operates at the intersection of housing and hospitality, focusing on rental housing for aging seniors in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company’s business model is built on a portfolio of over 2,500 communities, predominantly consisting of seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care facilities. Welltower structures its operations through an umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT), with Welltower Inc. holding approximately 98.4% of Welltower OP LLC. The company generates revenue through three primary segments: Seniors Housing Operating, which accounted for 78% of total revenues in 2025 and involves managing properties through incentive-based contracts with partners such as Care UK, Cogir, and Sunrise Senior Living; Triple-net, which represented 11% of revenues and involves leasing properties to operators under long-term master leases that pass through operating costs; and Outpatient Medical, which comprised 7% of revenues and consists primarily of triple-net leased buildings affiliated with health systems. Additionally, Welltower deploys capital into construction projects, real estate loans, and equity method investments in unconsolidated entities.

The company’s competitive moat is anchored in its scale and geographic diversification across affluent micro-markets that benefit from secular demand driven by an aging population. Welltower emphasizes a disciplined approach to capital allocation powered by a proprietary Data Science platform and superior operating results driven by the "Welltower Business System." A key structural advantage in its Triple-net segment is the use of integrated master leases, which bundle multiple properties under a single tenant agreement. This bundling feature mitigates risk by preventing tenants from cherry-picking high-performing assets during renewals or bankruptcies, thereby spreading risk across the entire leased portfolio. Furthermore, the company seeks to protect downside risk and avoid permanent capital loss by investing at compelling bases with meaningful margins of safety and partnering with high-quality operators who demonstrate staying power across economic cycles.

Management’s stated priorities center on delivering long-term compounding of per share earnings growth for existing investors. To achieve this, the company focuses on generating durable cash flow growth by deploying capital into real estate in markets with strong secular demand. Welltower prioritizes the selection of aligned, high-quality operators and structures investments to balance sustained long-term growth with downside protection. The company also highlights its commitment to providing high-quality and affordable healthcare infrastructure for an aging global population, viewing this as a requirement for vast investment and development.

The filing flags several material risks and accounting complexities. In the Seniors Housing Operating segment, the company relies heavily on third-party partners for effective and efficient property management, creating operational dependency on these external entities. In the Triple-net segment, while master leases provide bundling benefits, certain leases contain purchase options that could result in property dispositions for less than full market value if exercised. Additionally, the company faces credit risk in its loan portfolio, where it must maintain allowances for credit losses based on the financial strength of borrowers and the value of collateral; the filing notes that loans with deteriorated credit quality may be placed on non-accrual status. Finally, the company recognizes significant stock-based compensation expense, particularly related to Executive Key Employee LTIP Unit Awards, which requires complex fair value estimates using Monte Carlo models and contributes substantially to total compensation costs.
