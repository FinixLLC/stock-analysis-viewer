## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (IAU_10-K_20260906.txt)

The iShares Gold Trust (IAU) is a grantor trust designed to provide investors with exposure to the price of gold. The company's business model revolves around issuing shares that represent fractional ownership in its net assets, which are primarily composed of physical gold held by a custodian. Investors can buy or sell these shares on the NYSE Arca under the ticker symbol IAU. The trust does not engage in active management or speculative trading; instead, it aims to reflect the performance of gold prices before accounting for expenses.

The competitive moat of IAU lies in its ability to offer a cost-efficient and accessible alternative to investing in physical gold. By eliminating the complexities associated with directly holding gold, such as storage and insurance costs, the trust makes gold investment more feasible for retail investors. Additionally, the trust's structure allows for easy trading through traditional brokerage accounts, enhancing liquidity and accessibility.

Management priorities are centered around maintaining operational efficiency and minimizing expenses. The sponsor, iShares Delaware Trust Sponsor LLC, assumes most of the trust's administrative and marketing costs, which helps keep fees low. The trust's only recurring expense is the sponsor fee, set at 0.25% annually, ensuring that costs remain predictable and manageable for investors.

The risks highlighted in the filing include market risk associated with fluctuations in gold prices, liquidity risk due to potential discrepancies between trading hours of major gold markets and NYSE Arca, and operational risks related to the custody and valuation of gold. The trust also notes regulatory risks since it is not subject to the same protections as registered investment companies or commodity pools. These factors could impact the trust's ability to maintain its net asset value and provide accurate pricing for shares.

In summary, IAU operates as a passive vehicle for gold exposure with a focus on cost efficiency and accessibility. While it benefits from a strong competitive position in offering gold investments, it faces risks related to market volatility, liquidity, and operational integrity that could affect performance and investor outcomes.
