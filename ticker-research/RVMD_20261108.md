## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (RVMD_10-K_20260827.txt)

Revolution Medicines (RVMD) is a clinical-stage precision oncology company focused on developing novel targeted therapies for RAS-addicted cancers. Its business model relies on a proprietary structure-based drug discovery platform that utilizes deep chemical biology and cancer pharmacology know-how to create small molecules tailored to unconventional binding sites. Specifically, the company develops "RAS(ON) Inhibitors," which are oral, tri-complex inhibitors designed to directly bind to active RAS variants, differing from first-generation KRAS(OFF) inhibitors that sequester the inactive form. The pipeline includes multi-selective and mutant-selective inhibitors such as daraxonrasib, zoldonrasib, elironrasib, and RMC-5127, intended for use as monotherapies or in combination with other agents to address genetic drivers and adaptive resistance mechanisms in solid tumors like pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, non-small cell lung cancer, and colorectal cancer.

The company’s competitive moat is built on its innovative, proprietary technologies that enable the creation of small molecules for unconventional binding sites, allowing it to target RAS variants that are traditionally considered undruggable. This approach is supported by a sophisticated structure-based drug discovery capability and a strategy of deploying precision medicine to inform monotherapy and combination regimens. Additionally, the company leverages external collaborations to enhance its capabilities, including a partnership with Iambic Therapeutics to use artificial intelligence for lead discovery and optimization, and clinical collaborations with major pharmaceutical companies such as Bristol Myers Squibb, Amgen, Summit Therapeutics, and Tango Therapeutics to evaluate combinations of its RAS(ON) Inhibitors with their respective compounds.

Management’s stated priorities center on advancing its deep pipeline of RAS(ON) Inhibitors through clinical development, with a particular focus on registrational Phase 3 trials in pancreatic cancer and non-small cell lung cancer. Key milestones include the ongoing evaluation of daraxonrasib and zoldonrasib in global Phase 3 studies (RASolute 302, 303, 304, and 305) and the planned initiation of new trials such as RASolute 309 and RASolve 308. The company is also prioritizing the development of a new class of RAS(ON) Inhibitors designed to overcome drug resistance, with first-in-human trials expected to begin in late 2026. Furthermore, management is focused on expanding its collaborative network to explore combination therapies and biomarker identification, as seen in partnerships with Break Through Cancer and Aethon Therapeutics.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily the inherent uncertainty and lengthy duration of the regulatory approval process by the FDA and EMA, noting that the company has not yet obtained approval for any product candidate. There is a significant risk that clinical trials may fail to demonstrate safety or efficacy, or that interim data may differ materially from final results, potentially leading to the denial of marketing approval. The company also highlights the risk of clinical trial delays due to various factors, including patient enrollment difficulties, regulatory actions, and third-party contractor failures. Additionally, the filing notes that the company substantially relies on third parties for preclinical studies, clinical trials, and manufacturing, which introduces operational risks related to the quality and timeliness of these services. Finally, the company faces competitive risks from other biopharmaceutical companies that may develop safer, more effective, or less expensive products, or obtain regulatory approval more rapidly, which could reduce or eliminate RVMD’s commercial opportunity.
