## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (NDSN_10-K_20260827.txt)

Nordson Corporation is a diversified precision technology company that engineers, manufactures, and markets differentiated products and systems for the precision dispensing, application, and control of fluids, adhesives, coatings, and biomaterials, as well as for surface treatment, testing, and inspection. The company’s business model relies on a direct global sales force and qualified distributors to serve a broad customer base across consumer, industrial, medical, and technology end markets, with no single customer accounting for more than 10% of sales. Nordson generates approximately two-thirds of its revenue from international operations, with principal manufacturing facilities located in the United States, China, and various European and Asian countries. The company’s strategy, known as Ascend, focuses on delivering top-tier revenue growth with leading margins and returns through three pillars: the Nordson Business System (NBS) Next growth framework, an owner mindset organizational structure, and a talent strategy. Growth is driven by organic innovation, new product introductions, and acquisitions of companies with differentiated precision technology portfolios that complement existing capabilities and expand into attractive high-growth end markets.

Nordson’s competitive moat is derived from a combination of intellectual property and human capital. The company holds a global portfolio of over 2,100 granted and pending patents and more than 1,000 trademarks, which it uses to protect its proprietary technologies. However, management explicitly states that the company’s competitive advantage is largely attributable to the technical, marketing, and sales competence of its employees rather than any single patent or trademark. Nordson competes in a global marketplace against large, well-established manufacturers and alternative bonding, sealing, and fluid control techniques, differentiating itself through extensive application expertise, direct global service, and solutions that increase customer uptime, line speed, and material efficiency.

Management’s stated priorities center on long-term growth and returns, balanced benefits for all constituencies, and customer satisfaction. The company emphasizes a self-renewing organization that creates wealth for customers, employees, shareholders, and communities. Specific operational priorities include solving customer needs globally, driving organic growth through new products and technology, capturing opportunities in emerging geographies, and leveraging existing technology into new applications. Additionally, management prioritizes employee self-fulfillment, growth, and equitable compensation, as well as community impact through the Nordson Corporation Foundation and employee volunteer programs. The company explicitly states that it does not intend to alter its foundational objectives to mitigate short-term sales or earnings swings, focusing instead on consistent, long-term value creation.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to macroeconomic conditions, supply chain volatility, and raw material costs. Nordson notes that its business is affected by global economic conditions, currency exchange rate fluctuations, and industry capacity changes. Specific supply chain risks include the cost of raw materials, particularly imports from China, and the potential impact of tariffs and disruptions in the availability of components and finished goods. While the company reports stabilization of the global supply chain and improved lead times, it acknowledges that Asia-origin shipments remain volatile and that parcel and domestic trucking rates are increasing. Additionally, the company faces risks associated with intellectual property protection, noting that while it relies on patents and trade secrets, its competitive advantage is heavily dependent on employee competence, meaning the loss of key personnel could impact its ability to maintain its market position.
