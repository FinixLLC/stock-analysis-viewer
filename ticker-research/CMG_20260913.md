## Business Profile

Chipotle Mexican Grill is a fast-casual restaurant chain specializing in burritos, bowls, tacos, and salads made with responsibly sourced ingredients. The company emphasizes "Food with Integrity," focusing on high-quality, sustainably raised meats and produce without artificial additives. As of 2025, it operates over 3,900 restaurants in the U.S. and 104 internationally, with a growing number of partner-operated locations. Its business strategy revolves around five pillars: protecting its core operations, evolving brand messaging, modernizing technology, expanding globally, and cultivating top talent.

Chipotle's competitive moat lies in its commitment to quality ingredients, digital innovation, and customer experience. The company has invested heavily in digital platforms, with digital sales accounting for 36.7% of revenue in 2025. It also emphasizes employee development, with nearly 90% of restaurant leadership roles filled through internal promotions.

Management priorities include driving operational excellence, accelerating menu innovation, leveraging AI and technology, expanding globally, and fostering a positive workplace culture. The company is focused on sustainability, sourcing responsibly raised ingredients, and maintaining high food safety standards. It also prioritizes employee well-being with competitive compensation, benefits, and career development programs.

Key risks flagged in the filing include supply chain disruptions, inflationary pressures on labor and materials, regulatory compliance costs, and increased competition from delivery services and other fast-casual brands. Additionally, the company faces challenges related to fluctuating consumer spending due to economic conditions and seasonal sales patterns.
