## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (MDB_10-K_20260906.txt)

MongoDB is a developer data platform company that provides a modern, flexible database solution designed to empower developers in building, transforming, and disrupting industries through software and data. The company's core offering is a document-based database that combines the strengths of relational and non-relational databases, enabling efficient handling of unstructured data while maintaining performance, scalability, flexibility, and reliability. MongoDB's platform integrates various capabilities such as search, vector search, time series analysis, and stream processing, allowing developers to address diverse application requirements without relying on multiple single-purpose technologies.

The competitive moat for MongoDB lies in its developer-centric approach, which has garnered significant mindshare among developers since its inception. The company's document-based architecture simplifies data management, reducing the need for extensive database maintenance and enabling faster innovation. Additionally, MongoDB's ability to run applications across various environments—cloud, on-premises, or hybrid—provides flexibility and reduces vendor lock-in. The acquisition of Voyage AI further strengthens MongoDB's position in the generative AI space, enhancing its capability to support sophisticated AI applications with accurate and reliable data handling.

Management priorities include extending product leadership through continuous innovation, growing the customer base by leveraging developer adoption, and expanding global reach. The company emphasizes converting existing users of its free Community Server and Atlas free tier into paying customers, while also investing in sales and marketing efforts to attract new clients. MongoDB is committed to retaining and expanding revenue from existing customers by offering scalable solutions that adapt to growing application needs.

Key risks highlighted in the filing include macroeconomic challenges such as slower economic growth and inflation, which could impact customer spending and growth rates. Additionally, geopolitical tensions may introduce uncertainties affecting the company's operations and market conditions. The competitive landscape remains intense, with legacy relational databases and other non-relational alternatives posing significant competition. MongoDB must continue to invest in product development and marketing to maintain its leadership position and address these risks effectively.
