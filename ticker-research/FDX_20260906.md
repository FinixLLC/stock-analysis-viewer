## Business Profile

FedEx Corporation (FDX) is a global leader in logistics and transportation services, operating one of the world's largest and most complex air and ground networks. Its business model revolves around providing integrated shipping, e-commerce, and supply chain solutions to businesses and consumers worldwide. The company offers a range of services including express delivery, ground shipping, freight transport, and custom clearance, leveraging its extensive infrastructure and technology to maintain high service standards.

FDX's competitive moat is built on several key factors: its vast network of aircraft, vehicles, and facilities; its advanced information systems for real-time tracking and management; and its strong brand reputation for reliability and efficiency.
