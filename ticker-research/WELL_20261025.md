## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. operates as a real estate investment trust (REIT) and umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT) focused on the "silver economy," providing rental housing and healthcare infrastructure for aging seniors in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company’s business model centers on owning a portfolio of over 2,500 communities, predominantly in seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care. Welltower generates revenue through three primary segments: Seniors Housing Operating, where it relies on third-party partners to manage properties under incentive-based contracts; Triple-net, where it leases properties to operators under long-term master leases that shift operating costs to the tenant; and Outpatient Medical, which consists largely of triple-net leased buildings affiliated with health systems. The company deploys capital through acquisitions, development, and joint ventures, aiming to generate long-term compounding of per-share earnings growth by investing in affluent micro-markets with secular demand.

The filing suggests Welltower’s competitive moat is derived from its scale, geographic and property-type diversification, and a disciplined capital allocation process powered by a proprietary Data Science platform and the "Welltower Business System." The company emphasizes its ability to partner with high-quality operators who demonstrate staying power across economic cycles, while structuring investments to protect downside risk and avoid permanent capital loss. Additionally, the Triple-net segment benefits from master lease structures that bundle properties, preventing tenants from cherry-picking high-performing assets and spreading risk across the entire leased group, which also provides advantages in bankruptcy scenarios.

Management’s stated priorities are explicitly focused on delivering long-term compounding of per-share growth for existing investors. To achieve this, the company prioritizes a singular focus on generating durable cash flow growth, investing at a compelling basis that provides a meaningful margin of safety, and partnering with aligned, high-quality operators. The company seeks to diversify its portfolio by property type, relationship, and geographic location while monitoring investments through rigorous asset management processes, including creditworthiness reviews, property inspections, and covenant compliance checks.

The most material risks flagged in the excerpts include reliance on third-party operators for the Seniors Housing Operating segment, where specific partners like Care UK, Cogir Management Company, and Sunrise Senior Living account for significant portions of revenue. The filing also highlights credit risk associated with its loan portfolio, noting that the allowance for credit losses depends on judgments regarding borrower financial strength, collateral value, and general economic conditions. Furthermore, the company faces execution risks in its construction and development activities, with substantial outstanding construction investments and commitments to provide additional funds to complete projects. Finally, the Triple-net segment carries lease-related risks, including the potential for tenants to exercise purchase options at less than full market value and the inherent credit risk associated with long-term lease obligations.
