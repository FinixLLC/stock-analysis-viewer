## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ULTA_10-K_20260827.txt)

Ulta Beauty, Inc. operates as the largest specialty beauty retailer in the United States, founded in 1990 to bridge the historical separation between prestige, mass, and salon product channels. The company’s business model centers on a "beauty enthusiast" demographic, offering a differentiated assortment of approximately 30,000 products from around 600 brands across all price points, supported by in-store salon services and a robust omnichannel presence. Ulta’s competitive moat is derived from several key strategic differentiators: a best-in-class loyalty program with over 46 million members that drives approximately 95% of total sales and provides deep consumer data for personalization; a convenient store footprint of over 1,500 U.S. locations, predominantly in high-traffic power strip centers; and a strong brand partnership ecosystem where top partners like L’Oréal and Estée Lauder account for roughly half of net sales. Additionally, the company leverages exclusive and private-label products, which represented about 11% of net sales in fiscal 2025, to enhance margins and guest perception.

Management’s stated priorities are outlined in the 2025 "Ulta Beauty Unleashed" plan, which focuses on three areas: driving core business growth through operational excellence, digital acceleration, and merchandising innovation; scaling new, accretive businesses such as wellness products, the UB Marketplace, and international expansion via subsidiaries like Space NK; and aligning the company’s foundation by optimizing cost structures and fostering an associate-centered culture. The filing highlights that while the U.S. beauty market is large and growing, the company faces material risks related to persistent inflationary and macroeconomic pressures, which could impact consumer spending habits, sales growth, and profitability. Furthermore, the company notes that its variable-rate credit facilities expose it to increased interest costs in periods of elevated rates, and it acknowledges that comparable sales have historically fluctuated due to economic conditions, merchandise mix, and marketing effectiveness.
