## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (CDNS_10-K_20260906.txt)

Cadence is a global leader in developing computational, AI-driven software, accelerated hardware, and silicon intellectual property (IP) products and solutions for engineers and scientists. The company's mission is to empower innovative companies to deliver advanced electronic products that drive economic growth and improve daily life. Cadence serves customers across various industries, including semiconductor manufacturers and systems companies designing electromechanical systems.

**Business Model:**
Cadence operates through a subscription-based model, offering software licenses, hardware sales or leases, maintenance services, engineering support, cloud access, and IP solutions. The company emphasizes recurring revenue from software licensing and maintenance, which accounted for 80% of its total revenue in fiscal 2025. Its product portfolio is organized into three categories: Core EDA (Electronic Design Automation), Semiconductor IP, and System Design and Analysis (SDA). These products are critical for designing, verifying, and optimizing semiconductors and electronic systems.

**Competitive Moat:**
Cadence's competitive advantage stems from its leadership in AI-driven design tools, a comprehensive product portfolio, and strategic acquisitions. The company has integrated cutting-edge technologies like agentic and generative AI into its solutions, enhancing efficiency and precision for customers. Its extensive IP portfolio, bolstered by recent acquisitions such as Arm Artisan foundation IP and Secure-IC, provides mission-critical designs across high-volume markets. Additionally, Cadence's ability to address complex system-level design challenges through its SDA platform further differentiates it in the market.

**Management Priorities:**
Management prioritizes innovation, customer success, and strategic growth. The company invests heavily in research and development (R&D) to integrate advanced technologies into its products. Strategic acquisitions, such as VLAB Works, Arm Artisan foundation IP, and Secure-IC, expand Cadence's capabilities and market reach. Management also focuses on optimizing operational efficiency and maintaining strong customer relationships to drive recurring revenue growth.

**Material Risks:**
Cadence faces several material risks, including macroeconomic factors like inflation, interest rate changes, and geopolitical tensions, which can impact global markets and customer demand. Geopolitical risks are heightened by trade control laws and export restrictions, particularly in China, where Cadence has faced license requirements for EDA software exports. The company also notes the risk of future reimposition of such restrictions. Additionally, Cadence is subject to antitrust regulations and foreign investment reviews, which could affect its ability to complete acquisitions. Lastly, the company faces competitive pressures from other technology providers, which could impact market share and pricing.
