## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (NDSN_10-K_20260827.txt)

Nordson Corporation operates as a diversified precision technology company that engineers, manufactures, and markets differentiated products and systems for the precision dispensing, application, and control of adhesives, coatings, polymers, sealants, and biomaterials. The company’s business model relies on a direct global sales force and qualified distributors to serve a broad customer base across consumer non-durable, consumer durable, and technology end markets, including packaging, electronics, medical, and transportation. Nordson generates the majority of its revenue outside the United States, with approximately 67% of 2025 sales originating internationally, and supports its operations through a network of direct facilities in over 35 countries. The company’s strategy, known as Ascend, focuses on delivering top-tier revenue growth with leading margins by leveraging a data-based segmentation framework to identify profitable growth opportunities, driving organic growth through new product introductions and emerging geographies, and executing acquisitions that complement existing capabilities and diversify into high-growth end markets.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from a combination of intellectual property and human capital. Nordson holds a global portfolio of more than 2,100 granted and pending patents and over 1,000 trademarks, which it uses to protect its proprietary technologies. However, management explicitly states that the company’s competitive advantage is largely attributable to the technical, marketing, and sales competence of its employees rather than any single patent or trademark. This is supported by a culture of innovation and a "Owner Mindset" organizational structure that empowers division-led teams to define strategic priorities. Additionally, Nordson creates value for customers by developing solutions that increase uptime, enable faster line speeds, and reduce material consumption, fostering strong customer relationships and satisfaction.

Management’s stated priorities center on long-term growth and returns, balanced against ethical behavior and community impact. The company emphasizes a self-renewing organization that creates wealth for customers, employees, shareholders, and communities. Key strategic pillars include the NBS (Nordson Business System) Next growth framework, which uses data to drive resource allocation, and a talent strategy focused on employee self-fulfillment, growth, and equitable compensation. Nordson also prioritizes genuine customer satisfaction as the foundation of its business and maintains a strong commitment to community engagement, having donated over $183 million to communities since 1989. The company aims to maintain leading margins and returns while managing short-term swings without altering its foundational long-term objectives.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to macroeconomic conditions and supply chain dynamics. Nordson operates in a competitive global marketplace affected by industry capacity changes, global competition, and economic conditions in the United States and abroad. The company notes that its business is sensitive to fluctuations in currency exchange rates and changes in the market price of raw materials, particularly imports from China, which may be affected by tariffs. Additionally, the filing highlights risks associated with the availability of raw materials, components, and sourced finished goods, noting that while most materials are available from multiple sources, disruptions in availability and geopolitical tensions can impact costs and logistics. The company also identifies risks related to intellectual property protection and enforcement, as well as the need to manage compliance requirements and cost effectiveness, which may lead to a reduction in the number of suppliers.
