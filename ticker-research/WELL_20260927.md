## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. is a real estate investment trust (REIT) and S&P 500 company that positions itself at the center of the "silver economy" by focusing on rental housing for aging seniors across the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company operates through an umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT) structure, with Welltower Inc. holding approximately 98.378% of Welltower OP LLC. Its business model involves predominantly investing in seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care communities, which it manages through three reportable segments: Seniors Housing Operating, Triple-net, and Outpatient Medical. In the Seniors Housing Operating segment, which generated 78% of total revenues in 2025, Welltower relies on a network of 62 partner operators to manage properties under incentive-based contracts, with major partners including Care UK, Cogir Management Company, and Sunrise Senior Living. The Triple-net segment, accounting for 11% of 2025 revenues, involves leasing properties to operators under long-term master leases (typically 10 to 20 years) where the tenant assumes all operating costs and often funds capital expenditures; this segment is heavily concentrated in relationships with operators like Integra Healthcare Properties. The Outpatient Medical segment, representing 7% of 2025 revenues, consists primarily of triple-net leased buildings affiliated with health systems. Beyond real estate ownership, Welltower engages in construction development, real estate lending (with an 8.9% yield on outstanding loans), and investments in unconsolidated entities, including a private funds management business.

The company’s competitive moat is described as being driven by a "disciplined approach to capital allocation" powered by a proprietary Data Science platform and superior operating results achieved through the "Welltower Business System," an end-to-end operational platform. Management emphasizes that this combination allows for the long-term compounding of per-share growth. Additionally, the company cites the structural benefits of its master lease arrangements in the Triple-net segment, specifically the "bundling feature" that prevents tenants from cherry-picking high-performing properties during bankruptcy or renewal processes, thereby spreading risk across the entire leased portfolio.

Management’s stated priorities center on generating long-term compounding of per-share earnings growth for existing shareholders. To achieve this, the company focuses on deploying capital into real estate in affluent micro-markets that benefit from secular demand, aiming to generate durable cash flow growth. The investment strategy seeks to partner with high-quality operators who demonstrate staying power across economic cycles, while investing at a compelling basis that provides a meaningful margin of safety. A key objective is to structure investments to protect downside risk and avoid permanent capital loss while sustaining long-term growth.

The filing flags several material risks and critical accounting estimates. A significant risk is reliance on third-party operators for the Seniors Housing Operating segment, where the company depends on partners to manage properties effectively and efficiently. In the Triple-net segment, there is exposure to tenant credit risk, as leases are often credit-enhanced by security deposits, guarantees, or letters of credit, and the company notes that certain leases contain purchase options that could result in dispositions for less than full market value. The company also highlights the risk associated with its loan portfolio, noting that it must maintain an allowance for credit losses based on quarterly evaluations of borrower financial strength and collateral value; in 2025, the company recognized a provision for loan losses of $9.4 million. Furthermore, the filing identifies stock-based compensation as a critical accounting estimate, with $1.56 billion in expense recognized in 2025, largely driven by Executive Key Employee LTIP Unit Awards that require complex fair value determinations using Monte Carlo valuation models.
