## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (QLYS_10-K_20260827.txt)

Qualys operates as a leading provider of a cloud-based platform that delivers integrated IT, security, and compliance solutions, primarily through a software-as-a-service (SaaS) model with renewable annual subscriptions. The company’s Enterprise TruRisk Platform allows organizations to identify and manage IT and operational technology assets across on-premises, endpoint, cloud, container, and mobile environments, while simultaneously collecting security data, prioritizing vulnerabilities, quantifying cyber risk, and verifying remediation actions. By consolidating these functions into a single multi-tenant cloud infrastructure, Qualys replaces disparate point tools with a unified view of security posture, enabling customers to reduce total cost of ownership and eliminate the need for hardware management. The company serves over 10,000 customers globally, including a majority of the Forbes Global 100, and generated $669.1 million in revenue in 2025, representing a 10% increase over the prior year.

Qualys’s competitive moat is rooted in its integrated, single-platform approach that addresses the fragmentation of traditional security stacks. The company leverages a shared core services architecture that includes a large knowledge base of vulnerability signatures, real-time security updates, and a scalable multi-tenant cloud infrastructure that allows for seamless expansion of coverage and users. This integration enables a common user interface, shared data access, and automated workflows for remediation and compliance, which management argues provides unique advantages such as real-time visibility without plugins or VPNs and easy global scanning. Additionally, the platform’s ability to correlate data across millions of devices using Elasticsearch clusters and its TruRisk scoring capabilities, which quantify risk in financial terms based on severity, exploitability, and business context, differentiate Qualys from competitors who rely on heterogeneous point solutions.

Management’s stated priorities focus on continuing to invest in research and development to improve existing solutions and develop new capabilities, as well as expanding sales and marketing efforts to support new solutions and drive demand from both new and existing customers. The company emphasizes the importance of maintaining a strong market position to sustain revenue growth, noting that 76% of the revenue increase in 2025 came from existing customers. Furthermore, management is focused on supporting business growth through hiring and operational expansion, which has led to increased personnel costs in cost of revenues, R&D, and sales and marketing. The company also continues to evaluate the impact of macroeconomic factors, including inflation, high interest rates, and geopolitical conflicts, on customer spending and sales cycles.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to the macroeconomic environment, where uncertainty, inflationary pressure, and reduced spending could lead customers to cut IT security budgets, potentially having a material adverse effect on the business. The company also notes risks associated with the timing and productivity of new sales personnel, as increased hiring may not immediately translate to revenue growth. Additionally, Qualys faces risks related to its reliance on a small number of large customers, as indicated by the concentration of revenue among major global organizations, and the ongoing need to manage the complexity of globally distributed IT assets and evolving cyber threats. The company also highlights the impact of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) on its tax provision and the uncertainty surrounding the release of valuation allowances in certain jurisdictions, which depends on achieving sustained profitability.
