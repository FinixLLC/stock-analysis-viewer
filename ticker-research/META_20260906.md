## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (META_10-K_20260906.txt)

Meta Platforms Inc., commonly known as Meta, operates a diverse ecosystem of social media platforms, augmented reality (AR), and virtual reality (VR) technologies. The company's mission is to build the future of human connection through innovative products that enable people to connect, share, and engage in immersive experiences. Its primary business model revolves around generating revenue from advertising on its Family of Apps, which includes Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, and Threads. These platforms allow users to share content, communicate, and discover information, while advertisers can target specific audiences across multiple apps.

Meta's competitive moat is built on its extensive user base, advanced AI technologies, and ecosystem of interconnected products. The company leverages AI to enhance advertising effectiveness, improve content recommendations, and develop new features. Its investment in AR and VR through Reality Labs (RL) positions it as a leader in the emerging metaverse space, with products like Meta Quest devices and AI glasses aiming to redefine how people interact digitally.

Management's priorities include expanding AI capabilities, enhancing user engagement through Reels and other content formats, developing wearables and immersive technologies, and improving monetization across platforms. The company also emphasizes protecting user privacy and security while navigating regulatory challenges.

Key risks flagged in the filing include dependence on advertising revenue, which is vulnerable to regulatory changes, privacy concerns, and shifts in consumer behavior. Additionally, Meta faces intense competition from tech giants and emerging platforms, particularly among younger demographics. Regulatory scrutiny over data usage, content moderation, and antitrust issues poses significant compliance challenges. The company's heavy investment in RL also carries financial risks, as these projects are long-term and may not yield returns for years. Economic uncertainties and geopolitical tensions further complicate the business environment, impacting user growth and advertiser spending.
