## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (MP_10-K_20260827.txt)

MP Materials Corp. is the largest producer of rare earth materials in the Western Hemisphere and the only scaled, vertically integrated source of critical rare earths and magnet materials in North America. The company operates two reportable segments: Materials and Magnetics. The Materials segment encompasses upstream and midstream operations at the Mountain Pass facility in California, where the company mines bastnaesite ore to produce rare earth concentrate and refines it into separated rare earth oxides, primarily neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr). The Magnetics segment consists of downstream operations at the Independence Facility in Texas, which converts NdPr oxide into rare earth metals, alloys, and permanent magnets. The company’s business model has shifted from historically selling raw concentrate to Chinese refiners to a vertically integrated strategy that sells separated oxides and finished magnets directly to end-users in the automotive, defense, and technology sectors.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its unique position as the sole scaled rare earth mining and processing site in North America, combined with an industry-leading cost structure and high environmental standards. This geographic exclusivity is reinforced by a "transformational" public-private partnership with the U.S. Department of War (DoW), formerly the Department of Defense. This partnership provides a significant structural advantage through a ten-year price floor protection agreement for NdPr products, a long-term offtake agreement for magnets produced at a new "10X" facility with guaranteed EBITDA, and substantial financial support including $400 million in preferred stock, a $150 million loan, and access to committed bank financing. Additionally, the company has secured long-term supply agreements with major commercial customers, including General Motors and Apple, the latter of which includes $200 million in prepayments for magnets and magnet recycling capabilities.

Management’s stated priorities center on restoring the full rare earth magnetics supply chain to the United States to maximize long-term stockholder returns while supporting U.S. national security. Key strategic initiatives include expanding domestic magnet production capacity to an estimated 10,000 metric tons per year by building the 10X Facility and expanding the Independence Facility. The company is also focused on extending heavy rare earth element (HREE) refining capabilities at Mountain Pass to produce samarium oxide, terbium, and dysprosium, and implementing commercial-scale recycling of magnet scrap. A critical operational priority has been the cessation of all product sales to China, a move made in July 2025 to align with DoW agreements and domestic supply chain objectives, which has shifted the company’s revenue focus toward separated products and domestic magnet sales.

The filing flags several material risks, most notably the immediate financial impact of ceasing shipments to China, which resulted in a 21% year-over-year revenue decline for the Materials segment in 2025. The company acknowledges that this strategic decision had a material negative impact on business, operating results, and liquidity in the short term. Other risks include the execution challenges of scaling the new Magnetics segment, which only began generating substantial revenue in 2025, and the dependence on the successful commissioning of the 10X Facility and HREE capabilities. The company also notes exposure to global market volatility and disruption caused by China’s export controls and trade restrictions, as well as the risks associated with forward-looking statements regarding the timing and success of its capital-intensive expansion projects.
