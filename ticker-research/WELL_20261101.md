## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. operates as a real estate investment trust (REIT) and umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT) focused on the "silver economy," providing rental housing and healthcare infrastructure for aging seniors in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company’s business model is built around a portfolio of over 2,500 communities, predominantly consisting of seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care facilities. Welltower generates revenue through three primary segments: Seniors Housing Operating, where it manages properties through incentive-based contracts with third-party operators; Triple-net, where it leases properties to operators under long-term master leases that shift operating costs and maintenance obligations to the tenant; and Outpatient Medical, which involves leasing buildings to healthcare providers. The company structures its investments to diversify by property type, geographic location, and partner relationships, often utilizing joint ventures and equity method investments to deploy capital into affluent micro-markets with secular demand.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its scale, geographic diversification, and a disciplined capital allocation process powered by a proprietary Data Science platform and the "Welltower Business System." Management emphasizes that this end-to-end platform drives superior operating results and allows for the monitoring of asset performance through rigorous asset management processes, including creditworthiness reviews, property inspections, and covenant compliance checks. Additionally, the company leverages the structural benefits of master leases in its Triple-net segment, which bundle properties to prevent tenants from cherry-picking high-performing assets and to mitigate bankruptcy risk by requiring the assumption or rejection of the entire lease portfolio rather than individual properties.

Management’s stated priorities center on delivering long-term compounding of per-share earnings growth for existing investors. To achieve this, the company focuses on allocating capital with a singular focus on generating durable cash flow growth, seeking to partner with high-quality operators who demonstrate staying power across economic cycles. The investment strategy prioritizes acquiring assets at a compelling basis that provides a meaningful margin of safety, while structuring investments to protect downside risk and avoid permanent capital loss.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to reliance on third-party operators and credit exposure. In the Seniors Housing Operating segment, Welltower relies on partners to manage properties effectively, with significant revenue concentration among a few key operators such as Care UK, Cogir Management Company, and Sunrise Senior Living. In the Triple-net segment, the company faces credit risk from tenants, with Integra Healthcare Properties accounting for a notable portion of segment revenues. Furthermore, the company maintains a substantial loan portfolio, with outstanding loans of approximately $2.08 billion as of December 31, 2025, which exposes it to credit loss risks. The filing notes that the determination of the allowance for credit losses requires significant judgment regarding the collectability of loan payments, the financial strength of borrowers and guarantors, and the value of underlying collateral, with provisions for loan losses recognized based on historical loss experience and individual credit assessments.
