## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. is a real estate investment trust (REIT) and S&P 500 company that operates at the intersection of housing and hospitality, focusing on rental housing for aging seniors in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company’s business model is built around a portfolio of over 2,500 communities, predominantly consisting of seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care facilities. Welltower structures its operations through an umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT), with Welltower Inc. holding approximately 98.378% ownership in Welltower OP LLC, which conducts the day-to-day business. The company generates revenue through three primary segments: Seniors Housing Operating, which accounts for the majority of revenue and involves managing properties through incentive-based contracts with partners; Triple-net, where properties are leased to operators under long-term master leases that obligate tenants to cover operating costs and capital expenditures; and Outpatient Medical, which consists primarily of triple-net leased buildings affiliated with health systems. Welltower also engages in construction, real estate lending, and investments in unconsolidated entities, seeking to diversify its portfolio by property type, relationship, and geographic location to generate durable cash flow growth.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its position in the "silver economy," targeting secular demand in affluent micro-markets. Welltower emphasizes a disciplined approach to capital allocation powered by its Data Science platform and superior operating results driven by its proprietary "Welltower Business System." The company seeks to partner with aligned, high-quality operators who demonstrate staying power across economic cycles, investing at a compelling basis that provides a meaningful margin of safety. Additionally, the structure of its Triple-net leases, particularly the use of integrated master leases, creates a bundling feature that protects the company from tenants cherry-picking better-performing properties or rejecting leases in bankruptcy, thereby spreading risk across the entire group of leased properties.

Management’s stated priorities center on delivering long-term compounding of per-share earnings growth for existing investors. To achieve this, the company focuses on allocating capital with a singular focus on generating durable cash flow, protecting downside risk, and avoiding permanent capital loss. Welltower aims to invest in high-quality, affordable healthcare infrastructure for an aging global population, utilizing acquisitions, developments, and joint venture partnerships. The company also prioritizes the deployment of capital into real estate that benefits from secular demand, while monitoring investments through rigorous asset management processes that include financial reviews, creditworthiness assessments, and property inspections.

The filing flags several material risks and accounting complexities. A significant portion of the company’s risk profile is tied to its reliance on third-party operators for the Seniors Housing Operating segment, where the effectiveness and efficiency of partners like Care UK, Cogir Management Company, and Sunrise Senior Living are critical to performance. In the Triple-net segment, the company faces credit risk associated with its tenants, including the potential for lease defaults or bankruptcy, although the master lease structure mitigates some of this risk. Additionally, the company carries substantial credit risk from its loan portfolio, with over $2 billion in outstanding loans, requiring ongoing evaluation of collectability and maintenance of allowances for credit losses. The filing also highlights the complexity of stock-based compensation, particularly the Executive Key Employee LTIP Unit Awards, which involve significant judgments in fair value estimation using Monte Carlo models and represent a substantial portion of total stock-based compensation expense.
