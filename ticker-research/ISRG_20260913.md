## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ISRG_10-K_20260906.txt)

Intuitive Surgical (ISRG) is a leading developer and provider of robotic-assisted surgical systems designed to enhance minimally invasive care. The company's core product is the da Vinci surgical system, which enables surgeons to perform complex procedures with greater precision, dexterity, and control compared to traditional open surgery or conventional minimally invasive surgery. ISRG's business model revolves around providing a comprehensive ecosystem that includes robotic-assisted systems, instruments, accessories, customer learning, and support services, all integrated through digital tools to improve clinical outcomes and efficiency.

The company's competitive moat is built on its technological leadership, extensive product portfolio, and strong brand recognition in the field of robotic surgery. ISRG has consistently innovated, introducing successive generations of its da Vinci systems, such as the fifth-generation da Vinci 5, which incorporates advanced features like force feedback technology and enhanced computational power. This commitment to innovation helps maintain a significant competitive advantage.

Management's priorities are centered on advancing minimally invasive care through continuous product development, expanding access to its technologies globally, and improving operational efficiency. The company also emphasizes aligning with the Quintuple Aim framework, focusing on better patient outcomes, experiences, provider satisfaction, cost reduction, and increased accessibility to high-quality care.

Key risks highlighted in the filings include macroeconomic factors such as trade tariffs and supply chain disruptions, which have already impacted costs and could continue to affect profitability. Additionally, the potential uptake of remanufactured instruments by third parties poses a risk to revenue and brand reputation. Geopolitical tensions and inflationary pressures further complicate the operating environment, potentially affecting supply continuity and hospital purchasing decisions. These factors underscore the need for ISRG to navigate an increasingly complex global market while maintaining its leadership in robotic-assisted surgery.
