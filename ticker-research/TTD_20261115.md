## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (TTD_10-K_20260906.txt)

The Trade Desk (TTD) operates as a global leader in advertising technology, specifically providing a self-service demand-side platform (DSP) that empowers advertising buyers to create, manage, and optimize digital campaigns across various formats, channels, and devices. The company’s business model is built on independence and objectivity, focusing exclusively on the buy side of the digital advertising ecosystem to avoid the conflicts of interest associated with competitors who also sell inventory. TTD generates revenue primarily by charging clients a platform fee, which is generally calculated as a percentage of the client’s total spend on the platform, alongside fees for value-added services and data. Clients, which include advertising agencies, advertisers, and service providers, enter into ongoing master services agreements (MSAs) rather than episodic insertion orders, a structure that supports a highly scalable model and has resulted in a customer retention rate exceeding 95% for over a decade. The platform leverages artificial intelligence, such as its "Koa" AI co-pilot, to automate campaign optimization, enhance targeting precision through "expressiveness," and provide real-time analytics and measurement capabilities.

TTD’s competitive moat is derived from its buy-side focus, which fosters deep trust with clients who are willing to share proprietary first-party data for campaign optimization, a level of data access that competitors with inventory conflicts of interest may not achieve. This data-centric approach creates a "flywheel" effect where increased data usage improves targeting and measurement, further driving client adoption. Additionally, the company maintains an open platform architecture with enterprise APIs that allow clients to customize functionality and integrate third-party tools, creating a rich ecosystem of inventory, publisher, and data partner integrations. TTD differentiates itself through its channel-agnostic approach, providing access to premium omnichannel inventory, including connected television (CTV), audio, and display, while offering granular, real-time reporting that emphasizes transparency and performance optimization.

Management’s stated priorities center on increasing the share of existing clients' digital advertising spend, particularly as budgets shift toward programmatic channels, and expanding the client base by targeting the mid-market and international markets, specifically in Europe and Asia. The company is heavily investing in technology innovation, with a specific focus on integrating AI capabilities to simplify data buying and enhance automation, as well as expanding access to omnichannel inventory sources like CTV. TTD also prioritizes the development of advanced data usage, measurement, and targeting capabilities, including the adoption of third-party retail data and first-party data, to maintain its position as a leader in data-driven advertising.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily the dependence on the continued growth of the programmatic advertising market and the ability to retain existing clients and gain a larger portion of their spend in a highly competitive environment. TTD notes that its future growth is contingent on its ability to differentiate its platform’s value against competitors who may offer artificially low prices enabled by inherent conflicts of interest. Additionally, the company highlights macroeconomic uncertainty, including changes in interest rates, inflation, and geopolitical developments, which could decrease demand for advertising services and disrupt supply chains. Rising costs for hosting infrastructure components, driven by high demand for AI-rich platform capabilities, are also cited as a challenge that could impact profitability. Finally, TTD identifies the need to manage the increased costs associated with developing and hosting its AI-driven platform and expanding its international presence, which may negatively impact near-term profitability despite long-term growth potential.
