## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ISRG_10-K_20260906.txt)

Intuitive Surgical, Inc. (ISRG) is a leader in robotic-assisted surgical technology, providing innovative solutions to enhance minimally invasive care. The company's mission revolves around improving patient outcomes through advanced robotics, offering a comprehensive ecosystem that includes robotic systems, instruments, accessories, training, and support services. Its flagship product, the da Vinci Surgical System, enables surgeons to perform complex procedures with precision, control, and reduced invasiveness. Introduced in 1999, the da Vinci system has evolved into multiple generations, including the fifth-generation da Vinci 5, which features enhanced capabilities such as force feedback and advanced imaging.

ISRG's competitive moat is bolstered by its strong intellectual property portfolio, extensive product offerings, and a large installed base of robotic systems. The company benefits from high switching costs for hospitals due to significant investments in training and infrastructure, as well as the clinical evidence supporting superior outcomes with its technology. Additionally, ISRG's focus on continuous innovation and expansion into new markets, such as diagnostic procedures with the Ion endoluminal system, further differentiates it from competitors.

Management emphasizes the Quintuple Aim framework, focusing on better patient outcomes, improved patient experiences, enhanced clinician efficiency, reduced healthcare costs, and increased access to care. The company is committed to rigorous clinical evidence and invests heavily in research and development to maintain its technological edge.

Key risks flagged in the filing include trade tariffs and supply chain disruptions, which have already impacted costs and could worsen with further changes in global trade policies. Additionally, the potential uptake of remanufactured instruments by third parties poses a risk to revenue and brand reputation. Macroeconomic factors such as inflation, elevated interest rates, and geopolitical tensions also present challenges, potentially affecting hospital purchasing power and supply chain stability.
