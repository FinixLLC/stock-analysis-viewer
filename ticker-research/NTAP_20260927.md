## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (NTAP_10-K_20260906.txt)

NetApp, Inc. is a global provider of Intelligent Data Infrastructure that has evolved from a storage hardware manufacturer into a software-driven, cloud-centric company. Its business model is built around the ONTAP data management software, which serves as the foundational technology for both its on-premises and cloud-based solutions. The company operates through two primary segments: Hybrid Cloud, which offers unified storage portfolios combining software, hardware, and services for on-premises and private cloud environments, and Public Cloud, which delivers storage and data services natively embedded within major hyperscalers such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. NetApp’s revenue is generated through a mix of software licenses, hardware sales, and subscription-based cloud services, all managed via a unified control plane called the NetApp Console.

The company’s competitive moat is anchored in its status as the only enterprise data infrastructure provider natively integrated with all major public cloud providers, offering seamless data mobility and unified management across on-premises and cloud environments. This deep cloud integration is supported by decades of leadership in data management, evidenced by the reliability and scalability of the ONTAP software. Additionally, NetApp differentiates itself through comprehensive built-in security features, including autonomous ransomware detection and automated compliance controls, as well as strong ecosystem partnerships with technology leaders like NVIDIA and Cisco. The company also cites its operational excellence and financial discipline, which have resulted in strong margins and robust cash flow, as enduring competitive strengths.

Management’s stated strategic priorities focus on four key areas: modernizing data infrastructure to improve performance and reduce total cost of ownership; enabling resilient and secure operations through advanced cyber resilience and data protection; optimizing cloud strategies by leveraging its unique native integration with hyperscalers to provide flexible consumption models; and accelerating AI adoption by providing AI-ready infrastructure and the NetApp AI Data Engine to streamline data pipelines and support model training. The company emphasizes its commitment to helping customers harness the power of their data for AI initiatives while maintaining security and governance.

The filing identifies several material risks, primarily stemming from the intensifying cyber threat landscape and evolving regulatory requirements, which necessitate continuous innovation in security and compliance. The company also highlights the challenges associated with the fragmentation of data across multiple silos, including on-premises data centers, edge locations, and various cloud environments, which creates complexities in data distribution, security, and governance. Furthermore, the rapid rise of AI and machine learning is cited as a transformative force that requires organizations to manage vast, diverse, and distributed data estates, posing both opportunities and challenges for NetApp to effectively connect, protect, and activate this data.
