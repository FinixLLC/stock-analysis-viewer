## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ULTA_10-K_20260827.txt)

Ulta Beauty, Inc., also known as Ulta Beauty, is an international specialty beauty retailer and a leading destination for cosmetics, fragrances, skincare products, wellness items, haircare solutions, and salon services. Founded in 1990 in Illinois, the company operates under a unique business model that combines prestige, mass-market, and professional beauty products into one convenient and welcoming shopping environment. Its target audience is "beauty enthusiasts," individuals passionate about self-expression and experimentation through beauty products. Ulta Beauty's U.S. operations make it the largest specialty beauty retailer in the country, with over 1,500 stores, while its international presence includes Space NK in the UK and Ireland, a joint venture in Mexico, and a franchise in the Middle East.

The company's competitive moat is built on several strategic advantages: a differentiated assortment of brands across various price points, an omnichannel footprint that seamlessly integrates physical stores with digital platforms, a best-in-class loyalty program, and exceptional guest experiences. Ulta Beauty offers over 30,000 products from approximately 600 brands, including exclusive and private-label options, and provides services like virtual try-ons and personalized recommendations through its digital channels. Its loyalty program, Ulta Beauty Rewards, with over 46 million members, drives customer retention and personalization.

Management's priorities are centered around three strategic initiatives: driving core business growth through operational excellence and enhanced marketing; scaling new, accretive businesses such as wellness products and e-commerce platforms like UB Marketplace; and aligning its foundation for future success by optimizing operations and fostering a strong corporate culture. The company aims to leverage these strategies to maintain market leadership and drive long-term profitable growth.

Key risks highlighted in the filings include macroeconomic pressures, such as inflation affecting consumer spending and profitability, as well as challenges related to rising interest rates impacting debt costs. Additionally, the company faces competitive pressures from alternative distribution channels and must adapt to evolving consumer preferences and trends in the beauty industry.
