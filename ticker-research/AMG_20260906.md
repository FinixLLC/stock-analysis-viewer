## Business Profile

AMG is a strategic partner to leading independent investment firms globally, focusing on generating long-term value by investing in high-quality, partner-owned firms known as Affiliates. The company's business model involves a unique partnership approach that allows Affiliates to retain autonomy and significant equity ownership while leveraging AMG's strategic capabilities, including access to growth capital, product strategy, distribution, succession planning, and strategic advisory services. This model is designed to enhance the Affiliates' ability to expand their reach, diversify their businesses, and achieve long-term success.

AMG's competitive moat lies in its innovative partnership approach, which preserves the independence and ownership culture of its Affiliates while providing them with institutional support and resources. The company's diverse portfolio of Affiliates across alternative strategies (private markets and liquid alternatives) and differentiated long-only strategies (equities, multi-asset, and fixed income) enhances earnings stability and growth potential.

Management's stated priorities include investing in high-quality new Affiliates operating in areas of durable client demand, supporting existing Affiliates with growth opportunities, and enhancing its own strategic capabilities. The company also focuses on expanding its U.S. wealth platform and launching innovative products to meet evolving client needs. Additionally, AMG emphasizes succession planning solutions for Affiliates, ensuring alignment between management teams and long-term success.

The risks flagged in the filing include market volatility impacting assets under management and performance fees, competitive pressures from larger firms consolidating resources, regulatory changes affecting investment strategies, and operational risks such as cybersecurity threats.
