## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (MP_10-K_20260827.txt)

MP Materials Corp. is a leading producer of rare earth materials in the Western Hemisphere, operating as the sole large-scale rare earth mining and processing facility in North America at its Mountain Pass site in California. The company also runs a magnet manufacturing facility in Fort Worth, Texas, known as the Independence Facility. Its operations are divided into two segments: Materials, which handles upstream mining and midstream refining of rare earth oxides (REEs), and Magnetics, focused on downstream production of magnets and related products.

The company's competitive moat stems from its vertical integration across the entire rare earth supply chain, from mining to magnet manufacturing, coupled with a commitment to sustainability and low production costs. This positions MP Materials as a critical supplier for industries reliant on rare earth elements, such as electric vehicles, renewable energy, aerospace, and defense.

Management has prioritized expanding domestic production capacity, reducing reliance on foreign sources, and securing long-term partnerships with key customers like General Motors and Apple. The company has also entered into significant public-private partnerships, including a transformative agreement with the U.S. Department of War (DoW), to build out an end-to-end rare earth magnet supply chain.

Key risks flagged in filings include dependency on government support and contracts, potential volatility in rare earth prices, and challenges associated with scaling production capacity. Additionally, geopolitical tensions and trade restrictions, particularly involving China, pose significant risks to the company's operations and market dynamics.
