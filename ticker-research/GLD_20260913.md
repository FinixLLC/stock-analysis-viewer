## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (GLD_10-K_20260906.txt)

SPDR Gold Trust (GLD) is an investment trust established to provide investors with exposure to the price of gold bullion. The company operates by holding physical gold and issuing shares that represent fractional ownership in the trust's gold holdings. These shares are traded on major stock exchanges, including NYSE Arca, the Mexican Stock Exchange, Singapore Exchange, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited, and the Tokyo Stock Exchange. The primary objective of GLD is for its shares to reflect the performance of gold prices, adjusted for the trust's operational expenses.

The competitive moat of GLD lies in its ability to offer a cost-effective and accessible way for investors to participate in the gold market without the logistical challenges of holding physical gold. By managing storage, insurance, and other related costs internally, GLD reduces barriers to entry for both institutional and retail investors. Additionally, the trust's structure allows for efficient trading through traditional brokerage accounts, making it a convenient alternative to direct gold ownership.

Management priorities include maintaining operational efficiency, ensuring transparency in gold holdings, and adhering to regulatory requirements. The trust is not actively managed like a typical investment fund but instead focuses on passively holding gold and managing expenses. The sponsor emphasizes the importance of accurate valuation methods, using the LBMA Gold Price PM as the benchmark for determining the net asset value (NAV) of the trust.

Key risks identified in the filings include market volatility, which can significantly impact the price of gold and, consequently, the value of GLD shares. Additionally, the trust's reliance on the LBMA Gold Price PM introduces dependency on the integrity and accuracy of this pricing mechanism. Regulatory changes or disruptions in the global gold markets could also affect the trust's operations. Furthermore, the gradual reduction in the amount of gold per share due to expense-related sales may impact long-term returns for shareholders.

In summary, GLD provides a vehicle for investors seeking exposure to gold with lower barriers to entry and operational costs. Its competitive advantages stem from its efficient structure and accessibility, though it is subject to market risks and dependencies on external pricing mechanisms.
