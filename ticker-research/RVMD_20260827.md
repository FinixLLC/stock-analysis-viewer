## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (RVMD_10-K_20260827.txt)

RVMD is a clinical-stage precision oncology company focused on developing targeted therapies for RAS-addicted cancers. The company leverages advanced structure-based drug discovery technologies to create small molecules that target unconventional binding sites on RAS proteins, which are common drivers of various cancers. RVMD's pipeline includes multiple RAS(ON) Inhibitors designed as monotherapies or in combination with other therapies, targeting different RAS mutations such as G12C, G12D, and G13C.

The company's competitive moat stems from its proprietary technologies and deep expertise in RAS biology, enabling the development of highly selective inhibitors that directly target active RAS variants. This approach is designed to overcome limitations of existing therapies and address unmet needs in cancers like pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), and colorectal cancer.

Management has prioritized advancing a deep pipeline of RAS(ON) Inhibitors, including daraxonrasib, zoldonrasib, and elironrasib, which have received Breakthrough Therapy Designations from the FDA. The company is also exploring combination therapies with other agents to enhance efficacy and overcome resistance mechanisms.

Key risks include the lengthy and uncertain regulatory approval process, potential delays in clinical trials due to recruitment challenges or adverse events, competition from other biopharmaceutical companies, and reliance on third-party manufacturers and collaborators. Additionally, RVMD faces financial uncertainties as it continues to invest heavily in research and development without any approved products yet generating revenue.
