## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (IDXX_10-K_20260906.txt)

IDEXX is a global leader in veterinary diagnostics, water quality testing, and livestock health solutions. The company operates through three main segments: Companion Animal Group (CAG), Water, and Livestock, Poultry, and Dairy (LPD). CAG focuses on diagnostic products and services for companion animals, including point-of-care instruments, consumables, reference laboratory services, and veterinary software. The Water segment provides testing solutions for water quality, primarily targeting microbial contamination in drinking water. LPD offers diagnostics for livestock and poultry health, including tests for disease detection and herd management.

IDEXX's competitive moat is built on its differentiated product portfolio, strong customer relationships, and recurring revenue streams. The company emphasizes high-margin recurring revenues from consumables, software subscriptions, and diagnostic services, which account for a significant portion of total revenue. Its ability to innovate and integrate products with advanced technologies, such as AI-powered imaging and cloud-based solutions, further enhances its market position.

Management priorities include expanding the installed base of premium diagnostic instruments, increasing instrument utilization through new test offerings, and leveraging customer commitment arrangements to drive recurring revenue growth. The company also focuses on enhancing software capabilities, improving operational efficiency, and broadening its product portfolio across all segments.

Key risks identified in the filing include macroeconomic challenges impacting veterinary practice spending, sector-specific headwinds such as competition and pricing pressures, and regulatory hurdles in water testing markets. Additionally, the company faces risks related to integrating acquired businesses and achieving profitability from new or acquired reference laboratories.
