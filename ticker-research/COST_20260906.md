## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (COST_10-K_20260906.txt)

Costco Wholesale Corporation operates as a membership-only warehouse retailer that sells a wide range of products, including groceries, general merchandise, and services. The company's business model relies on offering high-quality goods at competitive prices, with a focus on bulk purchasing to achieve cost efficiencies. Members pay an annual fee for access to its stores, which are designed to provide a convenient shopping experience.

Costco's competitive moat is built on several key factors: its ability to leverage economies of scale through large volume purchases, its strong brand reputation, and the loyalty of its membership base. The company emphasizes customer satisfaction by maintaining high standards for product quality and service, which helps differentiate it from competitors.

Management has stated that their priorities include maintaining low prices, enhancing member value, and expanding both domestically and internationally. They also focus on operational efficiency to ensure profitability while keeping costs low for members.

The 10-K filing highlights several material risks, including the impact of economic conditions on consumer spending, supply chain disruptions, labor shortages, and the competitive retail environment. Additionally, risks related to cybersecurity and data privacy are noted as significant concerns given the company's reliance on technology and customer information systems.
