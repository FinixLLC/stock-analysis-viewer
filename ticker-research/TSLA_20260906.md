## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (TSLA_10-K_20260906.txt)

Tesla operates as a vertically integrated company focused on designing, developing, manufacturing, selling, and leasing high-performance fully electric vehicles and energy generation and storage systems. The company emphasizes performance, safety, and sustainability in its products, which include consumer vehicles such as the Model 3, Y, S, X, Cybertruck, and Tesla Semi, as well as energy storage solutions like Powerwall and Megapack. Tesla also offers services related to autonomous driving (FSD) and has launched a Robotaxi service leveraging its AI capabilities. The company's business model integrates software updates, over-the-air enhancements, and direct sales channels to enhance customer experience and reduce costs.

Tesla's competitive moat is rooted in its advanced AI technology, vertically integrated supply chain, and focus on innovation. The company leverages real-world data from its vehicles to improve autonomous driving systems and develop AI-enabled robots like Optimus. Its ability to integrate hardware, software, and services provides a unique advantage over competitors.

Management priorities include profitable growth through cost reduction, operational efficiency, and expanding the product portfolio. Tesla is investing in next-generation vehicle platforms, battery technologies, and AI infrastructure while localizing production to reduce costs and improve supply chain resilience. The company also aims to scale its energy storage business and expand its Supercharger network to support growing demand.

Key risks flagged by the filing include macroeconomic factors such as inflation, interest rate fluctuations, and regulatory changes impacting consumer demand. Trade policies and tariffs could increase production costs and affect profitability. Additionally, competition in the electric vehicle market and supply chain disruptions pose challenges to maintaining growth and margins.
