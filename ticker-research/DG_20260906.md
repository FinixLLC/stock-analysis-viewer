## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (DG_10-K_20260906.txt)

Dollar General (DG) is a leading discount retailer in the United States, operating over 20,959 stores across 48 U.S. states and Mexico as of February 27, 2026. The company specializes in offering everyday low prices on a wide range of consumable items, seasonal products, home goods, and apparel, with a focus on providing value and convenience to its customers. Its business model emphasizes small-box stores located in close proximity to consumers, allowing it to cater to both rural and urban markets effectively.

The competitive moat for Dollar General lies in its ability to deliver highly competitive prices while maintaining a low-cost operating structure. This is supported by a focused merchandise assortment, strategic sourcing, and efficient distribution networks. The company's private brand offerings further enhance its price advantage, often at substantial discounts compared to national brands. Additionally, Dollar General leverages digital tools like its app and delivery services to improve customer convenience and engagement.

Management has outlined four key priorities: driving profitable sales growth, capturing growth opportunities, enhancing its position as a low-cost operator, and investing in team development. These strategies aim to optimize the company's operations, expand its market presence, and strengthen its competitive edge.

The 10-K filing highlights several material risks, including macroeconomic factors such as inflation, wage growth, and changes in consumer spending patterns, which can significantly impact customer behavior. The company also faces intense competition from larger retailers like Walmart, Family Dollar, and Dollar Tree, which may have greater resources and market reach. Additionally, supply chain disruptions, tariffs, and labor costs pose challenges to maintaining profitability and operational efficiency. Despite these risks, Dollar General has demonstrated resilience through consistent same-store sales growth over the years, reflecting its strong value proposition and customer appeal.
