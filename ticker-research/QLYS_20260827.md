## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (QLYS_10-K_20260827.txt)

Qualys is a leading provider of cloud-based IT, security, and compliance solutions that help organizations manage their internal and external IT and operational technology (OT) assets across various environments, including on-premises, endpoints, cloud, containers, and mobile. The company's platform integrates multiple tools into a single solution, enabling customers to identify and manage assets, collect and analyze security data, discover and prioritize vulnerabilities, quantify cyber risk exposure, recommend and implement remediation actions, and verify their implementation. This comprehensive approach addresses the growing complexity of IT infrastructures and the increasing threat landscape.

The company's competitive moat lies in its ability to offer a unified platform that simplifies tool fragmentation, providing real-time visibility and seamless scalability. Qualys' cloud-based model eliminates the need for on-premise hardware, reducing costs and enabling rapid deployment. The platform leverages extensive vulnerability signatures and integrates with third-party tools, offering a vendor-agnostic solution.

Management's priorities include expanding the customer base through direct sales and channel partners, investing in research and development to enhance existing solutions and develop new capabilities, and increasing marketing efforts to promote its cloud apps. The company also focuses on maintaining strong financial performance by managing costs and optimizing operations.

Key risks identified in the filing include macroeconomic factors such as inflation, high interest rates, and geopolitical tensions, which could reduce customer spending on IT security. Additionally, the company faces competition from other cybersecurity providers, reliance on third-party services, and potential regulatory changes that could impact its business model.
