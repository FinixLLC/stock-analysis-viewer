## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ETN_10-K_20260906.txt)

Eaton Corporation plc operates as an intelligent power management company with $27.4 billion in 2025 revenue, serving customers in 180 countries across data center, utility, industrial, commercial, machine building, residential, aerospace, and mobility markets. The company’s business model focuses on protecting the environment and improving quality of life by providing products and solutions that manage power reliably, efficiently, and sustainably. Eaton positions itself to capitalize on macroeconomic megatrends, specifically electrification, digitalization, reindustrialization in North America, and global infrastructure growth. The company actively expands its portfolio through strategic acquisitions, such as Fibrebond, Resilient Power Systems, Ultra PCS, and Boyd Thermal, to strengthen its presence in data center solutions, solid-state transformer technology, and aerospace applications. Conversely, Eaton is divesting its Mobility business (Vehicle and eMobility segments) via a planned spin-off into an independent public company.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its status as a market leader in many of its product lines, supported by a strong competitive position in performance, technology, customer service, and price. Eaton leverages a significant portfolio of intellectual property, including patents, trademarks, and trade secrets, which management considers valuable to the business as a whole, although no single right is deemed material to consolidated financials. The company also benefits from a broad product scope that mitigates the risk of any single intellectual property expiration. Additionally, Eaton maintains supply chain resiliency by sourcing raw materials from many suppliers and investing in partner relationships to mitigate supply chain risks.

Management’s stated priorities center on operating sustainably with the highest ethical standards, reducing environmental footprint, and eliminating waste. The company emphasizes solving urgent power management challenges to build a more sustainable society. Strategic priorities include strengthening participation across the entire electrical power value chain, particularly in data center and utility end markets, and benefiting from the growth cycle in commercial aerospace and defense. Human capital management is also a key priority, with goals to attract and retain top talent through competitive compensation, inclusion and diversity initiatives, and a focus on employee safety and wellbeing, evidenced by a 2024 Total Recordable Case Rate of 0.39 and a 2030 target of 0.25.

The filing flags several material risks, including exposure to raw material costs and supply chain disruptions, although the company states it generally has no difficulty obtaining materials. Eaton notes that compliance with current environmental laws is not expected to have a material adverse effect on capital expenditures or earnings, but compliance with future laws may require increased capital expenditures. The company also highlights customer concentration risks, with 22% of Electrical segment sales tied to six large customers, 20% of Aerospace sales to three large OEMs, 37% of Vehicle sales to four large OEMs, and 18% of eMobility sales to one large OEM. Furthermore, the loss or expiration of intellectual property rights is identified as a potential risk, though management believes the broad scope of product lines mitigates the material impact of any single right's loss.
