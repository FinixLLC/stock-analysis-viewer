## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (NTAP_10-K_20260906.txt)

NetApp, Inc. is a global provider of intelligent data infrastructure that has evolved from a storage hardware manufacturer into a software-driven, cloud-centric company. Its business model is built around the ONTAP data management software, which serves as the foundational technology for both its Hybrid Cloud and Public Cloud segments. In the Hybrid Cloud segment, NetApp sells a unified portfolio of all-flash, hybrid-flash, and object storage hardware (such as the AFF, FAS, and AFX series) along with software licenses and support services to help enterprises modernize on-premises and private cloud environments. In the Public Cloud segment, the company generates revenue primarily through subscription-based, as-a-service offerings, including fully managed cloud storage services natively embedded within major hyperscalers like AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. This dual approach allows NetApp to manage, protect, and move data seamlessly across on-premises, hybrid, and multi-cloud environments, with a growing emphasis on enabling AI workloads through solutions like the NetApp AI Data Engine.

The company’s competitive moat is anchored in its deep, first-party native integration with all major public cloud providers, a position it claims is unique among enterprise data infrastructure providers. This integration, combined with decades of leadership in data management via the ONTAP software, creates high switching costs and operational stickiness for customers who rely on NetApp for consistent data services across diverse environments. Additionally, NetApp differentiates itself through built-in security and resilience features, such as autonomous ransomware protection and automated compliance controls, as well as strong ecosystem partnerships with technology leaders like NVIDIA and Cisco. The company also cites its operational excellence and financial discipline, which have resulted in strong margins and robust cash flow, as enduring competitive strengths that support continuous R&D investment and customer-centric innovation.

Management’s stated strategic priorities focus on four key areas: modernizing data infrastructure to improve performance and agility; enabling resilient and secure operations to counter intensifying cyber threats; optimizing cloud strategies by leveraging its unique position as the only provider natively integrated with all major public clouds; and accelerating AI adoption by helping customers streamline data pipelines and activate their data estates for machine learning and generative AI workloads. The company emphasizes its role in connecting, protecting, and activating data across every environment to help customers realize the full promise of AI.
