## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ETR_10-K_20260906.txt)

Entergy Corporation operates as an integrated energy company primarily focused on electric power production and delivery to retail customers across Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, and Texas. The company owns and operates approximately 25,000 MW of generating capacity and serves roughly 3.1 million utility customers, generating $12.9 billion in annual revenues for 2025. Its business model is centered on a single reportable Utility segment that encompasses generation, transmission, distribution, and sales. The generation portfolio is diverse, including nuclear, hydroelectric, solar, and natural gas assets. A key structural component is System Energy, a subsidiary that owns 90% of the Grand Gulf nuclear facility and sells power at wholesale to Entergy’s retail affiliates. While Entergy previously operated natural gas distribution businesses in Louisiana, it sold these assets in July 2025, narrowing its focus to electric utility services.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its status as a regulated utility with significant barriers to entry, including substantial rate bases ranging from $1.2 billion to $16.7 billion across its subsidiaries. Entergy benefits from formula rate plans and cost recovery riders in most of its operating territories, which allow for the recovery of fuel, purchased power, and capital investment costs, thereby providing a degree of revenue stability. Additionally, the company holds a strategic position in the MISO market and maintains a diverse generation mix that includes nuclear power, which offers a reliable baseload capability. However, the filing notes that Entergy’s growth strategy increasingly depends on a limited number of large customers, specifically data centers, for new generation and transmission investments, introducing a concentration risk that contrasts with its traditional broad retail base.

Management’s stated priorities are to operate and grow the utility business by understanding and meeting customer needs while creating value for stakeholders. The company emphasizes keeping customer costs as low as possible while ensuring reliable energy delivery. Entergy is committed to investing significant capital in customer growth, reliability, and resilience, as well as in clean energy to meet evolving customer demands. The management team highlights disciplined project management and robust analysis, including broad stakeholder outreach, as essential to executing its strategy and managing risks associated with new investments.

The filing identifies several material risks, with regulatory uncertainty being a primary concern. Entergy faces risks related to the length and outcome of rate proceedings, potential disallowances of costs, and changes in state or federal legislation and subsidies. Nuclear operations present specific hazards, including the potential for unplanned outages, regulatory changes by the NRC, and significant financial liabilities associated with spent fuel storage, decommissioning, and potential nuclear incidents under the Price-Anderson Act. The company also flags substantial risks related to its dependence on capital markets for liquidity and the potential for credit rating downgrades. Furthermore, Entergy highlights the material impact of severe weather and climate change, including hurricanes and droughts, which can disrupt operations and increase restoration costs. Finally, the filing notes risks associated with cyber attacks, data breaches, and the company’s ability to attract and retain a qualified workforce.
