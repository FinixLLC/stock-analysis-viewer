## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ULTA_10-K_20260827.txt)

Ulta Beauty, Inc. operates as the largest specialty beauty retailer in the United States, founded in 1990 to disrupt a market where prestige, mass, and salon products were previously siloed in distinct channels. The company’s business model centers on a "unique specialty retail concept" that aggregates approximately 30,000 products from roughly 600 brands across all price points, ranging from entry-level to luxury, alongside full-service salon offerings. This omnichannel approach combines a physical footprint of over 1,500 U.S. stores—typically located in high-traffic power strip centers—with digital platforms and fulfillment options like buy-online-pickup-in-store. Ulta targets "beauty enthusiasts," a demographic it estimates at 140 million in the U.S., leveraging a loyalty program with over 46 million members that drives approximately 95% of total sales. While the U.S. operations are the core, the company also maintains an international presence through its subsidiary Space NK in the U.K. and Ireland, a joint venture in Mexico, and a franchise in the Middle East.

The company’s competitive moat is built on several strategic differentiators, primarily its differentiated assortment and its best-in-class loyalty program. By offering a wide selection of established and emerging brands, including exclusive private-label products like the Ulta Beauty Collection, Ulta positions itself as the retail partner of choice for brand launches and scaling. The loyalty program serves not only as a retention tool but as a data engine; with 73% of loyalty members transacting solely in stores and omnichannel guests spending over three times as much as store-only guests, Ulta utilizes this data for personalized marketing and merchandising. Additionally, the company cultivates a "human connection" through knowledgeable associates and a welcoming store environment, which management argues is a key competitive advantage over purely digital or less service-oriented competitors.

Management’s stated priorities are outlined in the "Ulta Beauty Unleashed" plan, which focuses on three areas: driving core business growth, scaling new accretive businesses, and aligning the foundation for future success. To drive core growth, the company aims for operational excellence, digital acceleration, and store expansion, with a long-term goal of exceeding 1,800 freestanding U.S. stores. Scaling new businesses involves expanding into the wellness category, launching the UB Marketplace online platform, and enhancing its retail media network, Ulta Beauty Media. Finally, the company seeks to optimize its cost structure and cultivate an associate-centered culture to support long-term profitability.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to macroeconomic pressures and execution. Management notes that persistent inflationary and macroeconomic trends could impact consumer spending habits, the company’s ability to grow sales, and its profitability. There is also a risk that inflation could keep interest rates elevated, increasing the cost of debt, as evidenced by the shift from net interest income in fiscal 2024 to net interest expense in fiscal 2025 due to increased borrowings. Furthermore, the company acknowledges that comparable sales have fluctuated in the past and are expected to continue to fluctuate, influenced by general economic conditions, changes in merchandise strategy, and the effectiveness of marketing activities. The filing also highlights the risk of deleveraging in selling, general, and administrative expenses, which increased significantly in fiscal 2025 due to higher incentive compensation, store payroll, and corporate overhead related to strategic investments.
