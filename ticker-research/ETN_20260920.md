## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ETN_10-K_20260906.txt)

Eaton Corporation plc operates as an intelligent power management company with $27.4 billion in 2025 revenues, serving customers in 180 countries across data center, utility, industrial, commercial, machine building, residential, aerospace, and mobility markets. The company’s business model is anchored in the electrification, digitalization, and reindustrialization megatrends, positioning it to benefit from expanding end markets and increased global infrastructure spending. Eaton actively manages its portfolio through strategic acquisitions, such as Fibrebond, Resilient Power Systems, Ultra PCS, and Boyd Thermal, to strengthen its presence in data center solutions, solid-state transformer technology, and aerospace, while simultaneously planning to spin off its Mobility business (Vehicle and eMobility segments) into an independent public company.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its status as a market leader in many of its product lines, supported by a strong competitive position in performance, technology, customer service, and total cost of ownership. Eaton leverages a significant portfolio of intellectual property, including patents and trade secrets, which management believes are valuable to the business as a whole, though the loss of any single right is not expected to be material. Additionally, the company maintains a resilient supply chain for raw materials like copper, steel, and electronic components, and benefits from deep integration into the electrical power value chain, allowing it to serve customers from the chip to the grid.

Management’s stated priorities focus on capitalizing on the aforementioned megatrends to drive long-term growth, particularly in the data center and utility sectors, while maintaining a commitment to sustainability and ethical standards. The company is prioritizing the modernization of infrastructure and the electrification of society, with specific strategic moves to expand its data center portfolio through liquid cooling and modular solutions. Furthermore, management is focused on human capital, aiming to be a model of inclusion and diversity, maintaining high employee engagement (86% favorable in the 2025 survey), and achieving world-leading safety ratings, with a 2030 target for a Total Recordable Case Rate of 0.25.

The filing flags several material risks, including customer concentration, where 22% of Electrical segment sales and 37% of Vehicle segment sales are attributed to a small number of large customers. The company also notes exposure to raw material costs and supply chain disruptions, although it states it has no difficulty obtaining materials under normal circumstances. Environmental compliance is identified as a potential driver for increased future capital expenditures, and the company acknowledges that its products rely on intellectual property that may expire, though it mitigates this through ongoing development and acquisition of new IP.
