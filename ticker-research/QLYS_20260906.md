## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (QLYS_10-K_20260827.txt)

Qualys is a leading provider of cloud-based IT, security, and compliance solutions that help organizations manage their internal and external IT and operational technology (OT) assets across various environments, including on-premises, endpoints, cloud, containers, and mobile. The company's Enterprise TruRisk Platform offers an integrated suite of tools designed to identify and manage assets, collect and analyze security data, discover and prioritize vulnerabilities, quantify cyber risk exposure, recommend remediation actions, and verify their implementation. This platform is delivered through a software-as-a-service (SaaS) model, primarily via renewable annual subscriptions, allowing customers to access multiple cloud-based solutions on a single platform.

The company's competitive moat lies in its comprehensive, unified approach to IT security and compliance, which addresses the growing complexity of modern IT infrastructures. Qualys' platform provides real-time visibility, seamless scalability, and up-to-date resources, including one of the largest knowledge bases of vulnerability signatures in the industry. Additionally, the platform is designed to be cost-effective, with no hardware requirements for customers, reducing their operating costs.

Management's stated priorities include expanding the company's market presence through investments in research and development, sales and marketing, and customer support. The company also emphasizes continuous improvement of its existing solutions and the development of new capabilities to meet evolving customer needs. Qualys is focused on leveraging its strong market position to drive further demand for its solutions, expecting revenue growth from both new and existing customers.

The risks flagged in the filing include macroeconomic uncertainties such as inflationary pressures, high interest rates, and geopolitical conflicts, which could reduce customer spending on IT security. Additionally, the company faces challenges related to competition, regulatory changes, and the need to maintain strong cybersecurity measures for its own platform. These factors could impact the company's ability to sustain revenue growth and profitability.
