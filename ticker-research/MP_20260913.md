## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (MP_10-K_20260827.txt)

MP Materials Corp. is a leading producer of rare earth materials in the Western Hemisphere, operating as the sole large-scale rare earth mining and processing facility in North America at its Mountain Pass site in California. The company also owns a magnet manufacturing facility in Fort Worth, Texas, known as the Independence Facility, which produces neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) permanent magnets and precursor products. MP Materials operates through two segments: Materials, which focuses on upstream mining and midstream refining of rare earth oxides (REEs), and Magnetics, which handles downstream magnet manufacturing.

The company's competitive moat stems from its vertical integration across the entire rare earth supply chain, from mining to magnet production, as well as its strategic partnerships with government entities like the U.S. Department of War (DoW) and major corporations such as Apple Inc. This integration allows MP Materials to reduce reliance on foreign sources and enhance supply chain security. Additionally, the company benefits from significant cost advantages due to optimized operations at Mountain Pass and a strong commitment to sustainability.

Management's priorities include expanding domestic production capacity, particularly through partnerships with the DoW to build out an end-to-end rare earth magnet supply chain. The company is also focused on recycling capabilities to support a circular economy and reduce environmental impact. MP Materials aims to capitalize on growing demand for rare earth products in electric vehicles, renewable energy, and defense applications.

Key risks flagged by the filing include market volatility due to geopolitical tensions and trade restrictions, particularly involving China, which remains a dominant player in the rare earth industry. The company also faces challenges related to transitioning away from reliance on Chinese markets, as evidenced by its cessation of concentrate sales to China in 2025. Additionally, the success of new ventures, such as the 10X Facility and magnet recycling initiatives, depends on achieving projected production volumes and securing customer demand.
