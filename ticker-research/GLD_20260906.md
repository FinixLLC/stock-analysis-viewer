## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (GLD_10-K_20260906.txt)

SPDR Gold Trust (GLD) is an investment trust established to provide investors with exposure to the price of gold bullion. The company operates by holding physical gold and issuing shares that track the performance of gold, minus its expenses. These shares are traded on major stock exchanges, including NYSE Arca, and allow investors to gain access to the gold market without the logistical challenges of owning and storing physical gold.

The competitive moat of GLD lies in its ability to offer a cost-effective and convenient way for both institutional and retail investors to participate in the gold market. By handling storage and insurance through its custodians, HSBC and JPMorgan, GLD eliminates these barriers for investors. Additionally, the trust's structure as an exchange-traded fund (ETF) provides liquidity and transparency, with the net asset value (NAV) published daily.

Management priorities include maintaining operational efficiency, ensuring accurate valuation of gold holdings, and providing clear information to investors. The trust is not actively managed but rather structured to passively reflect the price of gold, with expenses deducted from its holdings over time.

Key risks flagged in the filing include market volatility, which can affect the share price and NAV, as well as operational risks such as potential disruptions in gold storage or custodial services. Additionally, regulatory changes could impact the trust's operations, and there is a risk associated with the cumulative effect of expenses on the value of shares over time.
