## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (MP_10-K_20260827.txt)

MP Materials Corp. is the largest producer of rare earth materials in the Western Hemisphere, operating a vertically integrated business model that spans the entire supply chain from mining to finished magnet manufacturing. The company’s operations are divided into two reportable segments: Materials and Magnetics. The Materials segment, centered on the Mountain Pass facility in California, handles upstream mining and midstream refining to produce rare earth oxides, primarily neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr), which are sold to customers in Asia and increasingly to domestic partners. The Magnetics segment, based at the Independence Facility in Texas, converts these oxides into permanent magnets and precursor products, with manufacturing of NdFeB magnets commencing in late 2025. This integration allows MP to capture value across the full spectrum of rare earth processing, a capability it claims is unique in North America.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its status as the only scaled, vertically integrated rare earth source in the Western Hemisphere, combined with a low-cost production structure and high environmental standards. MP leverages the Mountain Pass ore body, described as one of the world’s largest and highest-grade resources, to achieve world-class production costs for concentrate. Furthermore, the company has secured a "transformational" public-private partnership with the U.S. Department of War (DoW), which provides a significant structural advantage through a ten-year price floor protection agreement for NdPr products and a long-term offtake agreement for magnets produced at a new facility. This government backing, coupled with exclusive long-term supply agreements with major commercial clients like General Motors and Apple, insulates the company from some market volatility and ensures demand for its downstream products.

Management’s stated priorities focus on restoring the full rare earth magnetics supply chain to the United States to maximize long-term stockholder returns while supporting national security. Key strategic initiatives include the "Upstream 60K" strategy to increase annual rare earth oxide production to approximately 60,000 metric tons, the construction of the 10X Facility to expand domestic magnet capacity to 10,000 metric tons annually, and the development of heavy rare earth element (HREE) refining capabilities at Mountain Pass. Additionally, management is prioritizing the integration of recycling capabilities to process magnet scrap, a move supported by a $200 million prepayment agreement with Apple. The company has also explicitly ceased all sales to China to align with domestic supply chain objectives, signaling a strategic pivot away from its historical reliance on Chinese refiners.

The most material risks flagged in the filing include the immediate financial impact of ceasing shipments to China, which resulted in a 21% year-over-year revenue decline for the Materials segment in 2025 and created short-term liquidity pressures. The company faces significant execution risk in scaling its downstream operations, as the Magnetics segment is still in its early stages of commercial production. Additionally, MP is exposed to geopolitical and market volatility, evidenced by recent Chinese export controls and trade disputes, which have disrupted global supply chains. The company also carries substantial capital expenditure obligations, including the use of up to $600 million in existing cash to fund DoW-mandated projects, and relies on complex financing structures, including convertible preferred stock and warrants issued to the DoW, which introduce potential dilution and financial complexity.
