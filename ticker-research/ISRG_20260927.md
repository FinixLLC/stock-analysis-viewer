## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ISRG_10-K_20260906.txt)

Intuitive Surgical operates as a provider of robotic-assisted surgical and diagnostic platforms, primarily through its da Vinci surgical systems and the Ion endoluminal system. The company’s business model is built on a comprehensive ecosystem that combines capital equipment (surgical systems) with recurring revenue streams from instruments, accessories, and services. This ecosystem includes a suite of stapling, energy, and core instruments, as well as a digital portfolio offering customer learning, simulation, and support services designed to enhance clinical efficiency and reduce variability. The company positions its technology to expand minimally invasive care, aiming to improve patient outcomes, reduce total cost of care, and augment clinician skills through features like force feedback, 3D high-definition vision, and tremor filtration.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its established installed base and the integration of hardware, software, and clinical workflows. Intuitive leverages over three decades of digital enablement to create an integrated user experience that reduces procedural variability. Key differentiators include the proprietary da Vinci platform, which offers specific capabilities such as motion scaling, wristed instrument articulation, and the recently introduced force feedback technology in the da Vinci 5 system. The company also maintains a broad regulatory footprint, with clearances in the U.S., Europe, China, Japan, and South Korea, and a global network of field service engineers and distributors that supports system maintenance and program optimization for hospitals.

Management’s stated priorities, guided by the "Quintuple Aim," focus on delivering better clinical outcomes validated by peer-reviewed evidence, improving patient and clinician experiences, lowering the total cost of care per episode, and expanding access to high-quality minimally invasive care. Operationally, management is prioritizing the phased global launch of the fifth-generation da Vinci 5 surgical system, which features enhanced computing power and new digital integrations, alongside the continued rollout of the single-port da Vinci SP system. Additionally, management is focused on mitigating supply chain risks and managing the financial impact of evolving trade policies and tariffs, which have already increased cost of revenues by approximately $63.0 million in 2025.

The filing flags several material risks, most notably the adverse impact of tariffs and trade barriers on product costs and supply chain reliability. Intuitive notes that while many instruments are manufactured in Mexico under USMCA protections, imports from Germany and China are subject to tariffs, and potential restrictions on rare earth elements and critical minerals from China could materially affect operations. Other significant risks include the financial health of hospital customers, which may face staffing challenges, inflationary pressures, and elevated interest rates that could reduce the number of procedures performed or systems placed. The company also highlights the risk of third-party remanufactured instruments, which have received FDA clearance for certain systems, potentially reducing revenue if uptake increases. Finally, the success of new product introductions, such as the da Vinci 5 and SP systems, depends on market acceptance, pricing, and the absence of quality defects, all of which remain uncertain.
