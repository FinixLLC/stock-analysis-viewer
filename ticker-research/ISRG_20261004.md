## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (ISRG_10-K_20260906.txt)

Intuitive Surgical operates as a provider of robotic-assisted surgical and diagnostic systems, primarily through its da Vinci surgical platforms and the Ion endoluminal system. The company’s business model is built on a comprehensive ecosystem that combines capital equipment (surgical systems) with recurring revenue streams from instruments, accessories, learning technologies, and customer support services. This "razor and blade" approach is supported by a digital portfolio that connects these components to provide actionable insights, aiming to augment clinician skills, reduce procedural variability, and lower the total cost of care. The company targets a broad range of surgical specialties, including general, urologic, gynecologic, cardiothoracic, and head and neck procedures, as well as diagnostic lung biopsies via the Ion system.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its established installed base, proprietary technology, and regulatory approvals. Intuitive has been commercializing its technology for over three decades, with the first da Vinci system launched in 1999. Its technology differentiates itself through features such as 3D high-definition vision, tremor filtering, motion scaling, and wristed instruments that mimic human dexterity. The recent introduction of the fifth-generation da Vinci 5 system, which includes force feedback technology and significantly increased computing power, further entrenches this advantage by offering enhanced precision and integration with digital tools like virtual reality simulation and computational observers. Additionally, the company maintains a global network of field service engineers and distributors, creating high switching costs for hospitals due to the integration of hardware, software, and training.

Management’s stated priorities, as reflected in their "Quintuple Aim," focus on delivering better clinical outcomes validated by independent evidence, improving patient experiences by reducing recovery times and complications, enhancing the experience of care teams by reducing fatigue and increasing efficiency, lowering the total cost of care per patient episode, and expanding access to high-quality minimally invasive care. The company emphasizes the importance of rigorous, peer-reviewed evidence to demonstrate value to patients, providers, and payers, while also focusing on the continuous improvement of its technology to address barriers to care and resource constraints in healthcare systems.

The filing flags several material risks, with trade and tariff issues being particularly prominent. Intuitive notes that tariffs and trade measures increased its cost of revenues by approximately $63.0 million in 2025, with expectations of further increases in 2026. The company is exposed to supply chain disruptions due to its reliance on manufacturing in Mexico and imports from Germany and China, including critical components like endoscopes and rare earth elements. Other significant risks include the potential impact of remanufactured instruments by third parties, which could reduce revenue or cause negative publicity if they fail; macroeconomic factors such as inflation, elevated interest rates, and geopolitical conflicts affecting supplier stability; and the financial pressures faced by hospital customers, which could limit their ability to purchase new systems or perform procedures. Additionally, the success of new products like the da Vinci SP and da Vinci 5 depends on regulatory clearances, market acceptance, and the absence of quality defects.
