## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (AMD_10-K_20260906.txt)

AMD is a leading technology company specializing in high-performance computing solutions for data centers, client devices, and embedded systems. The company's business model revolves around designing and manufacturing advanced semiconductors, including CPUs, GPUs, FPGAs, and other specialized processors, which are integrated into full-stack solutions to address diverse computational needs across various industries.

**Competitive Moat:**
AMD's competitive advantage stems from its broad portfolio of AI-optimized products, strong ecosystem partnerships, and investments in cutting-edge technologies. The company is a pioneer in integrating AI capabilities directly into mainstream x86 platforms, providing differentiated solutions that support a growing ecosystem of AI-enabled applications. AMD's leadership in design, integration, and advanced packaging further strengthens its position as a preferred silicon provider for custom designs.

**Management's Priorities:**
In 2025, AMD prioritized accelerating growth in the Data Center segment by introducing next-generation products like the 5th Gen AMD EPYC processors and AMD Instinct MI350 Series GPUs. The company also expanded its AI software capabilities through strategic acquisitions and partnerships, such as with OpenAI, to enhance its full-stack solutions for AI workloads.

**Material Risks:**
The 10-K filing highlights several material risks, including dependence on a small number of customers for a significant portion of revenue, exposure to U.S. export controls impacting certain products like the AMD Instinct MI308 GPUs, and the potential financial impact of ongoing litigation. Additionally, the company faces challenges related to global supply chain disruptions, macroeconomic conditions, and intense competition in the semiconductor industry.
