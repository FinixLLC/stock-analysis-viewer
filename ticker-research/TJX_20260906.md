## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (TJX_10-K_20260906.txt)

TJX is a leading off-price retailer specializing in apparel and home fashions, operating over 5,200 stores globally across multiple brands. The company's business model centers on offering a rapidly changing assortment of brand name and designer merchandise at prices 20% to 60% below full-price retailers. This value proposition is supported by a flexible approach to buying, inventory management, and store operations, allowing TJX to adapt quickly to market trends and customer preferences.

The company's competitive moat lies in its opportunistic buying strategy, which enables it to acquire merchandise at significant discounts from manufacturers, brands, and other retailers. This strategy leverages TJX's global vendor relationships and allows for lean inventory levels, rapid turnover, and a treasure hunt shopping experience that encourages frequent customer visits. Additionally, TJX benefits from a strong brand portfolio, including well-known chains like TJ Maxx, Marshalls, HomeGoods, and TK Maxx, which operate in various regions worldwide.

Management's priorities include expanding the store network, enhancing the e-commerce presence, and maintaining a low-cost structure to sustain profitability. The company also focuses on improving inventory management and distribution efficiency to better serve its diverse customer base across income levels.

Key risks identified in the filing include global economic uncertainties, such as tariff fluctuations and trade policy changes, which could impact merchandise sourcing and pricing. Additionally, competition from traditional and online retailers remains intense, with factors like brand appeal, fashion trends, and price sensitivity playing critical roles. The company also faces challenges related to foreign currency exchange rates, which can affect financial results, particularly in international markets.

Overall, TJX's ability to maintain its competitive edge hinges on its flexible business model, strong vendor relationships, and strategic execution of growth initiatives while managing external risks effectively.
