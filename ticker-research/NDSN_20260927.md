## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (NDSN_10-K_20260827.txt)

Nordson Corporation is a diversified precision technology company that engineers, manufactures, and markets differentiated products and systems for the precision dispensing, application, and control of adhesives, coatings, polymers, sealants, and biomaterials. The company also provides solutions for testing, inspecting, treating, and curing surfaces, as well as manufacturing medical components such as catheters, cannulas, and balloons. Nordson’s business model relies on a direct global sales force and qualified distributors to serve a broad customer base across consumer non-durable, consumer durable, and technology end markets, including packaging, electronics, medical, and transportation. The company operates through three primary segments: Industrial Precision Solutions, Medical and Fluid Solutions, and Advanced Technology Solutions. With approximately 8,000 employees and manufacturing facilities in over 35 countries, Nordson generates the majority of its revenue internationally, with 67% of 2025 sales coming from outside the United States. The company emphasizes a "scalable growth framework" known as the Ascend strategy, which focuses on data-based segmentation to identify profitable growth opportunities, organic innovation, and strategic acquisitions that complement existing capabilities and diversify into high-growth end markets.

Nordson’s competitive moat is derived from a combination of intellectual property and human capital. The company holds a global portfolio of more than 2,100 granted and pending patents and over 1,000 trademarks, which it uses to protect its proprietary technologies. However, management explicitly states that the company’s competitive advantage is largely attributable to the technical, marketing, and sales competence of its employees rather than any single patent or trademark. Nordson differentiates itself by providing extensive application expertise and direct global service, creating value for customers by increasing uptime, enabling faster line speeds, and reducing material consumption. The company notes that it competes in a global marketplace with many large, well-established manufacturers, but its reputation for creativity and engineering expertise in high-technology application equipment allows it to maintain leading margins and returns.

Management’s stated priorities are centered on the Ascend strategy, which aims to deliver top-tier revenue growth with leading margins and returns. This strategy is built on three pillars: the NBS (Nordson Business System) Next growth framework, which uses data to drive resource allocation; an "Owner Mindset" division-led organizational structure; and a "Winning Teams" talent strategy focused on employee self-fulfillment and growth. Nordson prioritizes long-term growth and balanced benefits for all constituencies, explicitly stating that it does not intend to alter its foundational objectives to mitigate short-term sales or earnings swings. The company focuses on organic growth through new product introductions and leveraging existing technology into new applications, while also pursuing acquisitions that offer differentiated precision technology portfolios. Additionally, management emphasizes ethical behavior, community engagement through the Nordson Corporation Foundation, and the development of a highly qualified global team through training and leadership development.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to macroeconomic conditions and supply chain dynamics. Nordson’s business is affected by industry capacity changes, global competition, economic conditions in the United States and abroad, and currency exchange rate fluctuations. The company notes that while the global supply chain has stabilized, it continues to monitor risks related to raw material costs, tariffs on imports (particularly from China), and disruptions in the availability of components. Nordson relies on multiple sources for significant raw materials but acknowledges that cost and availability can be affected by market prices and geopolitical tensions. Additionally, the company faces risks associated with intellectual property enforcement and the potential for goodwill impairment, although no impairment was recorded in 2025. The filing also highlights the sensitivity of pension obligations to economic assumptions such as discount rates and expected returns on assets, which can significantly impact reported expenses and liabilities.
