## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (NDSN_10-K_20260827.txt)

Nordson Corporation is a diversified precision technology company that engineers, manufactures, and markets differentiated products and systems for the precision dispensing, application, and control of adhesives, coatings, polymers, sealants, and biomaterials, as well as for surface treatment, testing, and inspection. The company serves a broad range of end markets, including medical, electronics, consumer durables, industrial, and agriculture, through a direct global sales force and qualified distributors. Nordson’s business model relies on solving customer needs globally to drive organic growth through new product introductions, high levels of customer service, and expansion into emerging geographies, supplemented by acquisitions of companies with differentiated precision technology portfolios. In fiscal year 2025, the company generated approximately $2.79 billion in sales, with 67% of revenue derived from outside the United States, and employs approximately 8,000 people worldwide.

The company’s competitive moat is attributed less to any single intellectual property asset and more to the technical, marketing, and sales competence of its employees, combined with a global reputation for creativity and expertise in high-technology application equipment. While Nordson maintains a substantial intellectual property portfolio comprising over 2,100 granted and pending patents and more than 1,000 trademarks, management explicitly states that the expiration or loss of any single patent or trademark is not considered material to the business as a whole. Instead, the company’s advantage stems from its ability to create value by developing solutions that increase customer uptime, enable faster line speeds, and reduce material consumption, supported by extensive application expertise and a direct global service network.

Management’s stated priorities are centered on the "Ascend" strategy, launched in 2021, which aims to deliver top-tier revenue growth with leading margins and returns. This strategy is driven by three interconnected pillars: the NBS (Nordson Business System) Next growth framework, which uses data-based segmentation to identify profitable growth opportunities; an "Owner Mindset" organizational structure led by divisions; and a "Winning Teams" talent strategy focused on employee self-fulfillment and growth. The company emphasizes long-term value creation for all constituencies, noting that it does not intend to alter its foundational objectives to mitigate short-term sales or earnings swings. Additionally, management prioritizes genuine customer satisfaction as the foundation of the business and continues to invest in community relations through the Nordson Corporation Foundation and employee volunteer programs.

The filing flags several material risks, primarily related to the company’s dependence on macroeconomic conditions, global competition, and currency exchange rate fluctuations. Nordson operates in a competitive global marketplace against large, well-established manufacturers and faces risks associated with raw material costs, particularly tariffs on imports from China and disruptions in the availability of components and sourced finished goods. The company notes that while it sources from multiple suppliers, ongoing efforts to improve service and manage compliance may result in a reduction in the number of suppliers, potentially increasing supply chain vulnerability. Furthermore, the business is subject to seasonal variation, with sales typically highest in the second half of the fiscal year due to the timing of customer capital spending programs, and faces risks related to the interpretation of global income tax laws and the realization of deferred tax assets.
