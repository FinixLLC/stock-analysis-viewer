Entergy Corporation is an integrated energy company primarily focused on electric power production and distribution to retail customers across Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, and Texas. The company operates through its Utility segment, which includes five regulated utility subsidiaries—Entergy Arkansas, Entergy Louisiana, Entergy Mississippi, Entergy New Orleans, and Entergy Texas—and System Energy, a wholly-owned subsidiary that owns and operates nuclear power plants. As of December 31, 2025, Entergy served approximately 3.1 million retail customers and had annual revenues of $12.9 billion.

Entergy's business model centers on generating, transmitting, distributing, and selling electric power. The company has a diverse generation portfolio, including nuclear, hydroelectric, solar, and natural gas. It also invests in infrastructure to support customer growth, reliability, and resilience, while focusing on clean energy initiatives to meet evolving customer demands.

The company's competitive moat is rooted in its extensive utility network, regulatory approvals for rate mechanisms, and a diversified generation portfolio that provides stability against market fluctuations. However, Entergy faces significant risks, including regulatory uncertainties, nuclear operational risks, capital market disruptions, and the impacts of climate change. The company also relies on its ability to recover costs through regulated rates, which can be delayed or disallowed, affecting its financial performance.

Management's priorities include maintaining low customer costs while ensuring reliable energy supply, investing in infrastructure for growth and resilience, and transitioning toward cleaner energy sources. Entergy emphasizes disciplined project management and stakeholder engagement to manage risks effectively.

Key risks highlighted in the filing include regulatory changes impacting rates and operations, nuclear plant operational challenges, capital market disruptions affecting liquidity, and the physical and transition risks associated with climate change. Additionally, the company faces risks related to insurance costs, commodity price volatility, workforce availability, and potential litigation or cyberattacks.
