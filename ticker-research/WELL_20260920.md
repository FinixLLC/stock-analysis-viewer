## Business Profile
Source: 10-K (WELL_10-K_20260906.txt)

Welltower Inc. is a real estate investment trust (REIT) and S&P 500 company that operates at the intersection of housing and hospitality, focusing on rental housing for aging seniors in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada. The company’s portfolio consists of over 2,500 communities, predominantly in seniors housing, wellness housing, and post-acute care. Welltower’s business model is structured as an umbrella partnership REIT (UPREIT), with Welltower Inc. holding approximately 98.4% of Welltower OP LLC, which conducts the day-to-day business. The company generates revenue through three reportable segments: Seniors Housing Operating (78% of 2025 revenues), Triple-net (11%), and Outpatient Medical (7%). In the Seniors Housing Operating segment, Welltower relies on external partners for property management under incentive-based contracts, with major partners including Care UK, Cogir Management Company, and Sunrise Senior Living. The Triple-net segment involves leasing properties to operators under long-term master leases (typically 10 to 20 years) that obligate tenants to pay operating costs and often include escalating rent structures; this segment is heavily concentrated in relationships with operators like Integra Healthcare Properties. The Outpatient Medical segment consists primarily of triple-net leased buildings affiliated with health systems. Additionally, Welltower engages in construction, real estate lending (with an 8.9% yield on outstanding loans), and investments in unconsolidated entities.

The company’s competitive moat is derived from its scale, geographic and property-type diversification, and its proprietary operational frameworks. Welltower cites a "disciplined approach to capital allocation powered by our Data Science platform" and the "Welltower Business System," an end-to-end platform designed to drive superior operating results. The company emphasizes investing in affluent micro-markets with secular demand to generate durable cash flow growth while protecting against permanent capital loss. In its Triple-net segment, Welltower utilizes integrated master leases with bundling features, which prevent tenants from cherry-picking high-performing properties and provide structural advantages in bankruptcy scenarios by requiring the assumption or rejection of the entire lease portfolio.

Management’s stated priorities center on delivering long-term compounding of per-share earnings growth for existing investors. To achieve this, the company focuses on allocating capital with a singular focus on generating durable cash flow, partnering with high-quality operators who demonstrate staying power across economic cycles, and investing at a compelling basis that provides a meaningful margin of safety. The company seeks to structure investments to protect downside risk while allowing for sustained long-term growth, monitoring assets through rigorous review of financial statements, partner creditworthiness, and covenant compliance.

The filing flags several material risks and critical accounting estimates. A significant risk is reliance on third-party operators for the Seniors Housing Operating segment, where the company depends on partners to manage properties effectively. In the Triple-net segment, there is exposure to tenant credit risk, although mitigated by master lease structures and credit enhancements. The company also faces credit risk in its loan portfolio, requiring quarterly evaluations for collectability and maintenance of allowances for credit losses, with some loans potentially placed on non-accrual status. Additionally, the filing highlights the complexity of stock-based compensation, particularly for Executive Key Employee LTIP Unit Awards, which require significant judgment in fair value estimation using Monte Carlo models and contributed the majority of the $1.56 billion in stock-based compensation expense recognized in 2025.
